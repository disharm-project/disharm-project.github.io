People

People

DISHARM is a MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowship led by Marco Antonio Stranisci and supervised by Christian Hardmeier. The project is hosted by the IT University of Copenhagen.

Project team

Marco Antonio Stranisci is the principal investigator of the project. His research lays at the intersection of Natural Language Processing and Semantic Web technologies and focuses on the inequalities that are reflected and perpetuated by language technologies. Before starting his academic career at the University of Turin, he worked in the public school as a teacher and in non-profit organizations aimed at promoting civic education and social justice among the youth. Between 2023 and 2026 he has been co-founder of aequa-tech, a start-up focused on developing solutions to tackle online disinformation.

Collaborations

DISHARM involved collaborations with several researchers and institutions on specific tasks.

  • Anne Lauscher, Pranav A, and Rossana Damiano coauthored the paper "Epistemic Injustice in Language Models: An Audit of Pretraining Filters and Guardrails" in which we systematically explore which contents are flagged by guardrails and data filtering strategies.
  • We collaborated with Daniel Russo, Oscar Araque, and Marco Guerini on "MoralTrajectories: Evaluating Moral Steerability in Large Language Models", in which we evaluate political opinion shifts of LLMs when they are steered with moral foundations.
