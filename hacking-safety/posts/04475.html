Sarah James Moss, MBA is an award-winning Residential Designer based in South Louisiana. She created Moss Manor to supply her clients with unique and attainable modern furnishings. Each piece is personally selected from her extensive trade resources and travels throughout Europe, Asia, Africa and North and South America. Sarah has garnered numerous credentials for her home designs and personal style. Her design philosophy is to have fun and surround yourself with things that you enjoy. Sarah is also a third-generation Realtor. When not working with clients, she enjoys traveling, spending time with her six rescue dogs, and volunteering for arts and culture nonprofits.
