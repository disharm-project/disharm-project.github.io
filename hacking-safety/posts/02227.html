Casimer B. "Coshu" Zebron, was born on December 10, 1928 to his parents Jacob and Therssa Zebron and died in the Nanticoke Memorial Hospital in Delaware on January 7, 2021. He was raised in Curtis Bay, Maryland, he worked as a Machinist Die Maker for Harbison Walker Refractories for 24 1/2 years. He also was the owner of Enterprise Sanitation. He enlisted in the Army in 1951 and after basic training he enlisted in the 82nd Airborne Division and qualified as a paratrooper and gliderman. He later married Linda Gill and lived in Pasadena, Maryland for over 40 years before moving to Delaware. He enjoyed cooking, working and gardening. He is survived by his wife Linda and his children Timothy Zebron, Eric Zebron, Sandy Zebron-Wolfe, Brian Zebron and Allan Zebron and his 9 grandchildren and 7 great-grandchildren. Graveside services with military honors will be held on Monday, January 11, 2021 in the Holy Cross Cemetery 6020 Ritchie Highway BROOKLYN PARK, Maryland at 12 Noon. Monday, January 11, 2021
