Bhawanipur village is located in Jaspur block and Jaspur tehsil of Udham Singh Nagar district. It comes under Ahmad Nagar gram panchayat. The geographical area of Bhawanipur village is 257.28 heactares. Bhawanipur village in Udham Singh Nagar district has a total population of 636 comprising of 121 families that reside in Bhawanipur village. The village has a male population of 342 and female population of 294. The sex ratio of Bhawanipur village is 860 which is lower than the Uttarakhand average of 963. In Bhawanipur village, the population of children within age group of 0-6 yrs is 62. The child sex ratio of Bhawanipur village is 771 which is lower than the Uttarakhand average of 890.
