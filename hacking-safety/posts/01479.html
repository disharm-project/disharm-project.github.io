RapidSOS In the time it takes you to read this job description, RapidSOS will have handled ~1,380 emergencies. At RapidSOS, we are committed to using technology to build a safer, stronger future and working together to save lives. We’re in an exciting phase of growth, welcoming new members from across the globe to our mission-driven, ambitious, and inclusive team. Our work is founded on our values of trust and safety, pioneering, urgency, and purpose over pride, all of which support a company culture where people can innovate, collaborate, grow, and, above all, make an impact. If that sounds like an exciting opportunity, we want to hear from you! At RapidSOS, we are empowering safer, stronger communities with faster, data-driven emergency response. In partnership with public safety, RapidSOS created the world’s first intelligent safety platform that securely links life-saving data from 500+ million connected devices, apps and sensors and 100+ technology partners directly to RapidSOS Safety Agents, 911, and first responders globally. The platform is used by over 16,000+ first responder agencies and supports 171+ million emergencies each year. When people need help during an unsafe moment or an emergency, their connected device, home or building that is RapidSOS Ready, delivers essential data to the right place, when it matters most. What this role is about: As the Regional Sales Manager, you'll be working with Emergency Communications Centers (ECCs), state and local public safety officials, and our resellers to drive new business through our lead generation engine while identifying new opportunities. You will promote RapidSOS solutions which delivers location & additional data to where it's needed most: 9-1-1 call-takers and dispatchers who are answering calls and saving lives. You will be responsible for a regional territory and work collaboratively with ECC Engagement Managers along with other stakeholders throughout the sales cycle. This is a great opportunity for a sales professional to work in a dynamic start-up that brings innovation to public safety communications! What you’ll do: What we’re looking for in our ideal candidate: Nice-to-have experience (but not required! ): What we offer: If you're curious to learn more about RapidSOS, you can check out https://rapidsos.com/blog/ Starting pay for a successful applicant will depend on a variety of job-related factors, which may include experience, relevant skills, training, education, location, business needs, or market demands. The base salary range for this role is $110,000 - $130,000 with on-target-earnings (OTE) of $220,000 - $260,000. This role will also be eligible to receive equity options. #LI-Remote If you are based in California, we encourage you to read this important information for California residents linked here: https://rapidsos.com/privacy/california/ RapidSOS is proud to be an equal opportunity workplace.
