Adventure awaits treehouse in bali

1 of 22 sentences flagged

Free UK Delivery Let Little Hotdog Watson inspire you with this fantastic hideout in the mountains of Gunung Agung Volcano. This unique eco stay is for adventurous families. Its an amazing way to experience authentic life of balinese village. There is room for all the family in the treehouse and the views are breathtaking. If you are looking to tap into your creative side the Hideout comes with books, nine musical instruments and many more fun things! The views from the the house focus directly on one of the most sacred places around - Campuhan. Also close to the Besakih temple which is beautiful. Theres many places to go on adventures trekking through and up mount Agung, running through rice fields, snorkelling or diving! The adventures are endless.
