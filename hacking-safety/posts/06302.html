About Harvest Kings – professional folk style wedding band based in Somerset Harvest Kings are a Somerset-based 4 piece folky pop band, who entertain crowds with their Mumford and Sons style covers. We turn popular rock and pop songs into quirky and catchy numbers! View our list of the UK's best Mumford-style wedding bands ➡️ We're regularly booked to perform for weddings and events at Half Moon Farm in Hambleton, Rutland. Contact Harvest Kings As well as a fantastic high energy, folky live gig, there is the option of a 60-minute acoustic live lounge, chilled set which provides a great backdrop for reception drinks. View our list of the UK's best Mumford-style wedding bands ➡️ Also, if you like to vary the music for your event, this band can play a folky style set and then a rock/pop set after so you can lose yourself in your favourite summer festival tracks all night long. Harvest Kings are from Somerset but perform across the UK and southwest including Devon, Cornwall, Bristol, Wiltshire and Dorset. The band provide everything a client will need for their evening such as PA, Lighting and music between their performances. Every band member has been playing music since their childhood, playing in many other bands around the UK, so now exactly how to play great music and entertain crowds.
