5 hidden gems in amsterdam

www.bravetraveller.com · https://www.bravetraveller.com/5-hidden-gems-in-amsterdam/

As a travel writer, I’ve been lucky enough to visit many popular destinations around the world. But what I’ve learned is that sometimes, the best parts of a city are the ones that are off the beaten path. This is certainly true for Amsterdam, a city known for its canals, bicycles, and bustling tourist attractions. But beyond the well-known sights, there are hidden gems waiting to be discovered. In this blog post, I’ll share with you five of my favorite hidden gems in Amsterdam that will give you a unique and authentic experience of this vibrant city. Tucked away in the heart of Amsterdam’s bustling center, the Begijnhof is a peaceful oasis that will transport you back in time. This historic courtyard was once home to a community of Beguines, single women who devoted themselves to a religious way of life in the 14th century. Today, it is a serene and picturesque place, filled with quaint houses, a charming chapel, and a hidden garden. As you wander through the Begijnhof, you’ll feel like you’ve stepped into a different world, away from the hustle and bustle of the city. It’s the perfect spot to escape the crowds and take a moment of quiet reflection. Amsterdam is known for its love of cats, and there’s no better place to experience this than at the Catboat. This floating animal sanctuary is home to around 50 cats, all of whom have been rescued from the streets of Amsterdam. The Catboat is run by volunteers who are passionate about animal welfare and provide a safe and loving home for these feline friends. As a cat lover, I couldn’t resist paying a visit to this unique attraction. The cats are friendly and love attention, so be prepared for some cuddles and purring. It’s a must-visit for any animal lover and a great way to support a worthy cause. If you’re a fan of quirky and unusual museums, then the Electric Ladyland is a must-visit. This one-of-a-kind museum is dedicated to fluorescent art, and you’ll find yourself surrounded by a magical world of glowing rocks, minerals, and other objects. The museum is the brainchild of artist Nick Padalino, who has been collecting fluorescent objects for over 30 years. He will personally guide you through the museum, sharing fascinating stories and facts about the fluorescent world. It’s a truly unique experience that will leave you in awe. Amsterdam is a city known for its vibrant nightlife, but if you want a more intimate and authentic experience, head to De Nieuwe Anita. This cozy and eclectic bar is a hidden gem that is popular with locals and visitors alike. It’s a great spot to grab a drink, listen to live music, and even dance the night away. What makes this place even more special is that it’s also a cultural hotspot, hosting a variety of events such as movie screenings, art exhibitions, and poetry nights. So if you want to experience Amsterdam’s creative side, be sure to check out De Nieuwe Anita. Escape the city and immerse yourself in nature at the Amsterdamse Bos. This sprawling park is often overlooked by tourists, but it’s a favorite spot for locals to relax and enjoy the outdoors. There are endless activities to do here, from cycling and hiking to boating and picnicking. You can also visit the goat farm, where you can pet and feed the adorable goats. It’s the perfect place to recharge your batteries and get a taste of the Dutch countryside. These are just five of the many hidden gems waiting to be discovered in Amsterdam. So next time you visit this beautiful city, make sure to venture off the beaten path and experience these unique and authentic spots. Trust me, you won’t be disappointed.
