THE RUTLAND GALLERY Telephone: 01572 510048 Home Artists Gallery Store Gallery Exhibition About News and Media Visiting the Gallery Contact Us More Medium: Oil on canvas Size: 45 X 60 cm Signed: Yes Framed: Yes Paul P Smith was born in East Anglia in 1966. Paul has been painting in oils for over thirty five years and his highly detailed landscapes tie in with events and experiences in his life. Paul produces paintings that reflect the way he sees the world, in some way tactile, sensitive, and meaningful. Paul also paints portraits using traditional techniques to produce work of exceptional quality. Paul was a BP Portrait award finalist in 2015 and 2017 at the National Portrait Gallery in London.
