The bicycle racks from Klaver Bicycle Parking meet the requirements of FietsParKeur. This standard guarantees that the bicycle rack meets strict quality requirements in accordance with the Standards Setting Document. In order to continue to offer high-quality products, we have imposed additional requirements on our bicycle parking systems in addition to the FietsParKeur standard. Safety The FietsParKeur certificate makes a distinction in the space where the bicycle is parked. Is this a public space or a secure bicycle shed? The answer determines whether the bicycle racks are required to have a tie-down facility or not. All bicycle parking systems in our range have such a connection facility as standard. The bicycle racks also have roll-back protection: If the bicycle is pushed too far forward, the bicycle will automatically return and remain well supported. User-friendliness The floor bicycle rack is designed in such a way that double bicycle parking capacity can be achieved by means of upper and lower parking spaces. For effortless parking of bicycles on the upper spaces, the lifting weight of the upper gutter is reduced by an integrated gas spring. This in combination with a low lifting height makes the 2ParkUp a user-friendly double-layer bicycle parking system. Durability The choice of different centre-to-centre distances makes the bicycle racks suitable for most bicycles. A center-to-center distance indicates the distance between the bicycle racks. Important aspects when determining this distance are the available space, type of bicycles and number of bicycle spaces. Klaver Bicycle Parking applies a minimum centre-to-centre distance of 37.5 cm. In addition, all our bicycle racks are equipped with a spherical base plate, which prevents the accumulation of dirt under the bicycle rack. Damage prevention The bicycle racks are equipped with front fork support as standard. With front fork support, the bicycle is supported by a strong part, the front fork. This prevents a knock in the wheel. In addition, the wear-resistant protective sleeves prevent damage to the front fork of the bicycle. Solutions Service
