Uncategorized | 0 comments The following article, Biden Abandonment in Full Swing, was first published on The Black Sphere. The State of the Union was supposed to save Biden. “Just give a good speech,” Democrats implored from Biden. A low bar that even Biden couldn’t step over. Remember after the SOTU, the media proclaimed Biden was back. I felt like I watched that stupid movie, “The King’s Speech”, where everybody was impressed the King of England, well…spoke English. Biden got through the SOTU without falling completely flat on his face. But what is the state of the Union? It sucks, and it’s Biden fault. Still, fast-forward and Democrats asked for the debate to be moved up. This after many Democrats suggested Biden not debate Trump at all. So Joe stepped up to the plate for another swing at the ball Democrats teed up for him. Remember, according to Democrats Trump is dumb and universally disliked. Plus, Biden had 16 advisers to prepare him for Trump. And with all that help, Biden blew it. Shit his pants for all the world to see. Biden performed so poorly that Democrats called for his head almost immediately. But Biden and the Democrats had one more scheme to save Biden’s ass. What Biden needed was the unencumbered opportunity to get his message to the world. So Biden chose to debate himself. When you debate yourself, you control everything. And that’s what Team Biden did. They removed his opponent, and limited the questions to authorized press. As I wrote of Biden’s latest train wreck, So the much anticipated “big boy” conference was held. And before we even launched the main part of the festivities of the day around NATO, Biden had already demonstrated his 25th Amendment acumen. The old clown introduced Zelenskyy as “Putin”. Laughter ensued. But Biden wasn’t finished. In fact, after this initial showcase of Biden’s “5th Dementian”, he said, “Hold my beer!”. Then, he announced that Trump was his VP. With Democrats internal mortal combat over should Biden stay or go, we got both sides of the debate. Most of the fake news media actually continue to brutalize Biden. Meanwhile, Democrat leadership appeared to swing back Biden’s way, albeit tepid approval. In fact, Stephen Colbert actually mocked Pelosi’s “passive aggressive” non-support of Biden. Well three more Democrats have jump off Biden’s boat: “Dementia”. Further, the first Senator has called for Biden to resign as well. The public defection came from Sen. Peter Welch (D-VT). The media continues to pile on. ABC News anchor George Stephanopoulos expressed concern on Tuesday over President Joe Biden’s ability to serve another four years in the White House, just days after he sat down with the president. Stephanopoulos was approached by a pedestrian with a camera in New York City, who asked the ABC News anchor whether Biden should drop out of the 2024 presidential race. “Do you think Biden should step down? You’ve talked to him more than anyone else has lately,” questioned the pedestrian in footage obtained by TMZ. Stephanopoulos replied, “I don’t think he can serve four more years.” “You don’t think he can serve four more years?” the pedestrian asked. “Alright. That’s an answer.” After the remark went viral, Stephanopoulos expressed regret in a statement for weighing in on the matter. “Earlier today I responded to a question from a passerby. I shouldn’t have,” he told Puck News. Seriously? Stephanopoulos knows that you can’t unhear that type of statement. He definitely meant it. And he’s not the only legacy Democrat calling for Biden to fall on the sword. James Carville, David Axelrod, even Barack Obama are all said to be conspiring against Biden. A recent story has donors threatening to withhold $90 million in funds from a Biden PAC should he remain in the race. This is a major blow to the DNC given that Trump now out-fundraises Biden. And while Biden edged Trump out in June, July will be a bloodbath favoring Biden. Frankly, I think Biden will be gone before we get the July numbers. The night of the debate set things into motion Democrats have tried to avoid by relying on cheating and controlling the narrative by any means necessary. So Democrats now plan to toss Biden away like Christmas paper wrapping. And from my vantage point, they appear to be allowing Biden to bow out gracefully. But if Biden doesn’t take the hint, Democrats will show Biden just how bad things can get.
