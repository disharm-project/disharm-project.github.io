Attendance

mvh.sweetwaterschools.org · https://mvh.sweetwaterschools.org/attendance/

1 of 17 sentences flagged

Parents/guardians may call the attendance office at (619) 628-5715 to report an absence. If no one answers, please leave a voicemail message providing: (1) your name and relationship to the student; (2) your student’s name and date of birth; (3) a reason for the absence; (4) the date(s) of the absence(s) being reported. Alternatively, parents/guardians may email the Attendance Technician at david.delrio@sweetwaterschools.org. Attach all relevant documents to excuse the absent date(s) in question. Important: Both excused and unexcused absences count toward your student’s absence rate. Absences cleared in Saturday School count as days present in school, and do not contribute to individual student absence rates. If your student must leave early during the school day, an adult who is an emergency contact for your student must request an off-campus pass from the school. Additionally, your student MUST be picked up by an adult who meets the following criteria: Is listed as an emergency contact in your student’s school profile. Arrives in person to pick up the student. Presents a valid state ID. If you have any questions or concerns regarding off-campuses, or need to request one, please visit or contact the MVH Attendance Office at (619) 628-5715. You may also reach the Attendance Technician by email: david.delrio@sweetwaterschools.org.
