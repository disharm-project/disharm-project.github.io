A top finalist and one of the most popular contestants of Big Boss OTT 3, Sana Makbul, is a fashion model and actress predominantly working in the television industry. The actress has been vocal about her desire to win the show from the very beginning and all the internet polls in the run-up of today's grand finale suggest she just might! Whether she wins the show or not, the one title she has surely won is of the 'most fashionable contestant'. And we have proof! Let's take a look at 5 best outfits of the star that you can't help but want in your wardrobe. From the very get-go, she showed her impeccable sense of style with a stunning bright pink number at the premiere event. The breezy gown featured a plunging neckline along with a sheer panel and off-the-shoulder cape sleeves. You can never go wrong with an Anarkali suit, and this a beautiful one. Sana wore an elegant full-sleeved white suit which featured intricate golden neck detailing and paired it with gold-toned earrings. During the show, Sana was seen in a stunning black velvet strapless gown that she paired with a matching stole. She accessorized her look with a beautiful green-hued necklace and matching earrings. Credit: divasana/Instagram In one of the episodes, Sana can be seen wearing a beautiful sea-green co-ord set featuring an angrakha-style flared kurta paired with dhoti-style pants. She completed her look with statement earrings and a classic jutti. Orange is really the new black and this co-ord set is proof! Sana was seen in a casual set by Pankh Clothing that featured cute balloon sleeves. Keeping her look minimal she went with a half up-half down hairdo and completed her look with simple pearl hoops.
