We would like to share the story of these kittens..... The momma cat showed up at a home. The kind man fed her and took care of her, he posted to find owners as she was very friendly..... she was not claimed. One night she come in and started having these kittens. She was a young female and was scared. She went out through an open door taking the first kitten she had. On her way out the door, another baby fell out. She was gone in the night, a long night for the nice man left to take care of this new little life. He worked hard all night to save his life and keep him warm and fed. He reached out to ACR and brought the little kitten into our foster care the next day. Charlie was so little, but did well in foster care. After about 3 days the momma showed up and brought all three kittens back and left in the master bed of this nice man. We together made the decision that Charlie would stay in foster care with Momma Kim as he was doing so well. They were reunited when the Momma come into Vet Care Pet Hospital to be spayed. She has a wonderful forever home with this kind man. Now all the kittens have forever homes too.... this is what we do! are the best.
