This listing is for a set of a large aquamarine and small mushroom dishes. The large dish has two Mushroom Owls and the smaller mushroom dish has an Aquamarine Owlet. Both have 24kt gold accents. The large dish can be used for your everyday jewellery or as a candle holder/cone incense holder. I find the the small dish I really useful by the bedside table or in the bathroom to leave stud earrings or your rings in a safe place when having a shower, washing your hands or going to bed. To get a good idea of the sizes please check the pictures for reference. Made of white earthenware clay and fired twice around 1130°C, these ceramics have been hand painted with non toxic lead free glazes and underglazes. The gold accents have been painted with 24 kt lustre. Your dish set will arrive on your doorstep inside a sturdy box, wrapped in bubble wrap for safe travels, ready to give away as a gift or to keep for yourself. If you want this sent to an address other than your own, please message me - thanks! Measurements Approximate: Large aquamarine dish 10/12 cm Diameter.
