Attorney Jaime Patterson received her Bachelor of Arts degree from the University of Louisville in 2005 and her Juris Doctorate, conferred cum laude, from Louis D. Brandeis School of Law in 2008. Early in Jaime’s career, she advocated for individuals denied at all echelons of Social Security Administrative claims and at nationwide Social Security federal appeals departments. Over the past 5 years, her practice has focused exclusively on personal injury, fighting for the injured party. At HilliardLaw, Jaime continues to excel at client rapport within the mass tort and class action contexts. Jaime resides in Las Vegas, Nevada. She enjoys spending time with her husband and two adult children. Hilliard Law attorneys represented numerous injury victims and the families of those killed in accidents caused by GM’s defective ignition switch and their concealment of safety defects. Mr. Hilliard was the lead class counsel in Haese v. H&R Block, a class action lawsuit involving every Texan who received a rapid refund from H&R Block (approx. 300,000 plaintiffs). He assisted other class counsel in forcing H&R Block to disclose it received kickbacks for arranging loans between its tax preparation clients and predatory lenders. In September 2021, Hilliard Law attorneys secured a $50 Million settlement for the family of a man who died in a commercial trucking accident. The Hilliard Law trial team won a $33M product liability case against a golf-car company, involving a golf cart that tipped over on a child causing a severe traumatic brain injury. A jury determined that the defective design and marketing of an All Terrain Vehicle were the proximate cause of the decedent's injuries in a fatal ATV accident. In May 2013, Mr. Hilliard was the lead trial attorney who obtained a $25 million jury verdict in a premises liability case in Nueces County, Texas. His client, a security guard for a downtown Corpus Christi building, was severely beaten while at work. © 2024 All Rights Reserved.
