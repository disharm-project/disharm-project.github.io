When comparing Apple Wireless Keyboard vs Das Keyboard Model S, the Slant community recommends Das Keyboard Model S for most people. In the question“What are the best keyboards for programming?” Das Keyboard Model S is ranked 2nd while Apple Wireless Keyboard is ranked 20th. The most important reason people chose Das Keyboard Model S is: With the Cherry MX Browns the Model S Professional is very silent and suitable for an office environment. Key presses rarely "bottom out" and it comes with o-rings to prevent noise from being generated when you do. Visually more appealing than your typical all-black keyboard. Achieves a solid feel without feeling heavy or bulky. A small keyboard means your mouse and/or trackpad can be closer to you, while having your keyboard right in front of you. Being wireless makes it easy to carry around in your backpack and also means less wire clutter on the desk. The easy-click keys allow minimum effort when typing. You will be instantly familiar with MacBook keyboards. this keyboard is compact since it doesn't have the numpad. This keyboard can be used with iPads. With the Cherry MX Browns the Model S Professional is very silent and suitable for an office environment. Key presses rarely "bottom out" and it comes with o-rings to prevent noise from being generated when you do. This keyboard's branding is polished and attractive. Great for staying put and not sliding around the desk under energetic typing. This keyboard has convenient key functions for media control. Instead of having to deal with Windows or Mac branded modifier keys, you can configure the Model S to come with keycaps designed for Linux. All Das Keyboard models come standard with N key roll over. This means that the keyboard can handle all simultaneous inputs, making it great for gaming or for really really fast typists. If you get this keyboard directly from Das and, within 30 days, decide you don't want it, you can return it for a refund. Costar stabilizers significantly reduce friction in key motion. Two powered USB inputs are available on the Model S, making plugging in a wired mouse or other devices really easy and helps clean up your desk wiring. Like the other Apple chiclet keyboards, it is not meant for touch typing: the switches are mushy and lack the tactile feedback, the keycaps are flat and so is the keyboard profile (not DSA or DCS, just plain ... flat). It doesn't have dedicated Insert, Home, End, Page Up or Page Down keys. This keyboard doesn't have a numeric pad. It doesn't work properly with hardware that's not from Apple. The Das Keyboard range does not come in tenkeyless variants, so all the keyboards are pretty big. There was a noticeable decrease in build quality after manufacturer change. After some time, the glossy finish used for the case clearly shows fingerprints. Built By the Slant team
