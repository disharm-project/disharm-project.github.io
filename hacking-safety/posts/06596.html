14 july 2024 eyes that see the divine psalm 11918

spiritmeat.net · https://spiritmeat.net/2024/07/14/14-july-2024-eyes-that-see-the-divine-psalm-11918/

1 of 38 sentences flagged

One major thing that happens to you when you come into the holy presence of the Most High God is “opened eyes.” Your eyes open to see wondrous things out of His law. Your eyes open to see your portion in the Kingdom. Your eyes open to see every area where the devil is hiding, your eyes open to see men that God has sent into your life. Your eyes open to see your future in God, your eyes open to see your destiny. Your eyes open to see the divine pattern of things that God wants you to build on the earth. Don’t be blind in church, see what God is showing you and it shall be well with you in Jesus’ name. As you move on in your walk with God, you must not forget or walk ignorant of what God has done for us at the Dream Centre and for you as an individual. You must know that the times are not as before. You must be sensitive to everything God is doing now in this new season and release yourself for what God is working out in you. This is not the time to return to doubt, pessimism, and fear. It is time to walk in faith, and fear and faith cannot coexist. It is time for you to rise out of all those immature issues and move on and come up to mature matters. Your obedience is what connects you to the supernatural. Until you begin to take the issue of obeying God serious you will keep being cut off from miracles and the supernatural. Perhaps, you have been wondering why you hardly experience miracles, or it seems that the supernatural is not real because you don’t experience it. The reason may not be far-fetched. Ask yourself, “Am I obeying God?” Since I came on the road of the calling, I can stand before the Master and tell Him I have obeyed everything I am sure He told me to do till date.
