Home » Avoid 5 major pitfalls: how to find the best HR people by offering an awesome hiring experience Nothing puts off a top-notch HR candidate like bad hiring practices. And sadly, the world of HR is all too full of bad examples. 65% of businesses are struggling to hire at the moment, and 57% said the main block was a lack of quality applicants. So, let’s get real for a second. Could you be guilty of putting off candidates, even by accident? How many hoops are you expecting HR professionals to jump through? Have you ever left someone hanging? We’re here to help. It’s time to stop damaging your reputation or risk losing out on top talent. At Grace, we don’t mince our words, and we’re here to help you spot the pitfalls and show you how to create an awesome candidate experience. When you’re racing to get top HR people, one thing remains true: people matter the most. By keeping humans at the heart of recruitment, we foster respect, transparency and genuine engagement. This also attracts the best candidates, by showcasing the working culture they can expect when they work for you. How do you do this? Let’s dive in and see how many common pitfalls of bad candidate experience you might be guilty of. Then you can transform your hiring from good to great with our help. Sound familiar? Don’t worry, we’ve got your back. Here are practical tips to turn your hiring process into a far better, memorably awesome experience. Define, prepare, and communicate: Before you post that job ad, get your ducks in a row. Show respect for everyone, throughout: Schedule interviews while showing respect for candidates’ time. Keep communication real and human Don’t leave them hanging Cut the cr*p out of your interview process Make sure everyone feels valued See, it’s not that hard to create awesome hiring practices! And by not being a douchebag, you’ll attract top HR talent and become known as a company that values humans. Each time someone new interacts with you, it’s a chance to build an incredible reputation. At Grace, we’re a female-owned, outspoken recruitment company that’s here to shake things up. We have 25 years of experience and don’t just fill positions; we find the right people and ensure they’re treated with the respect they deserve. Let’s make your candidate experience one that attracts and retains the best talent. We champion candidates and hold businesses to account, because we believe in better hiring practices.
