Home » NTA NEET UG 2024 Notification OUT [neet.ntaonline.in] Apply Online NTA NEET UG 2024: On February 9, 2024, the National Testing Agency (NTA) announced the notification for the National Eligibility Cum Entrance Test (NEET) Undergraduate (UG) 2024. The NTA announced the NEET 2024 Notification on February 9, 2024, and the online application process began. Eligible candidates may apply online for the NTA NEET 2024 through the website neet.ntaonline.in. The latest date to apply for the NTA NEET 2024 is March 9, 2024. The NTA NEET 2024 written exam will be held on May 5, 2024. The Test pattern of NEET (UG) – 2024 comprises four Subjects. Each subject will consist of two sections. Section A will consist of 35 Questions and Section B will have 15 Questions, out of these 15 Questions, candidates can choose to attempt any 10 Questions. So, the total number of questions and utilization of time will remain the same. The pattern for the NEET (UG) – 2024 Examination for admission in the Session 2024-25 is as follows: Follow these steps to apply for NTA NEET UG 2024 Step 1: Review your qualifications in the NTA NEET UG 2024 Notification PDF provided below. Step 2: Click on the Apply Online Link given below or Go to official website neet.ntaonline.in Step 3: Than Click the Register Now button. Step 4: Enter your basic information. Step 5: Enter your registration number and password to log in. Step 6: Click the “Apply” button. Step 7: Type in information about yourself and your educational history. Step 8: Submit the necessary paper in the right way. Step 9: Pay the Application fee and click the button that says “Submit.” Step 10: Check the completed details and confirm them before clicking the final submit button.
