The biggest mistake a mom can make on the internet

If your kids are online, hopefully, so are you. Social media is a great way for a savvy mom know what her teens are up to, earning her the well-deserved moniker ‘Mom Stalker’. Just so you know, in today’s world of the Internet, stalking doesn’t necessarily have a negative connotation. If used wisely, of course. Because Mom stalking has it’s own occupational hazards as one momfazer very quickly found out. With her unwise actions she very nearly jeopardized her ability to easily view what her teenage daughter was doing online. Read on to see how she tipped over the precipice and almost lost her window into her daughter’s life.
