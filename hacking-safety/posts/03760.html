How to access ashlands update content in valheim

If you've been anticipating an update for Valheim, you're not alone. The Valheim Ashlands update is here, and I'll tell you below how you can play it. It's been a while since we got a major Valheim update, until April 22nd when the Ashlands update went live in the Valheim public beta. This new update includes a brand new biome called Ashlands. The biome is one of the penultimate realms in the game, bringing with it many difficult enemies and endgame content. Thus, if you haven't played in a while, or are just starting your Valheim experience, you may not want to jump right in. We've included a summary of what's included in the Ashlands update below. Related: Valheim weapon level list (2024) Currently, the Valheim Ashlands update is undergoing public testing before its release to the live version of the game. However, you can still access and play the update regardless of the platform you use to play Valheim. Once you have completed this step, Steam will automatically start downloading an update for Valheim. Once the download is complete, you can launch the game and explore the brand new Ashlands update. Once you are taken to the Xbox app and the installation is done, you can launch the game to access the Valheim Ashlands update. Before you jump in the game to play Ashlands, you want to remember that it is an endgame area. Progression from previous biomes is required to create new items or recipes, such as the item The Queen drops in the Mists. So, I highly recommend playing the rest of the game before venturing into the Ashlands. It's also worth noting that new content and terrain only spawns in areas you haven't explored before. So, if you've already explored the Ashlands biome and don't realize the new content is coming, you should start a new game. One more thing to keep in mind is that, as with every new update for a game, most of the mods don't work with Ashlands yet, and it will most likely take a while for them to be supported again. If you are someone who can't play without mods, I suggest you wait a bit.
