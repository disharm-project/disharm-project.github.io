Are you looking for the next step in your career? Are you someone who is ambitious and eager to put your stamp on the service? Then this could be the perfect opportunity for you! We are looking for a Senior Cardiac Physiologist to join and lead a team based in their flagship hospital in Northwest London. We are working with one of the largest national networks of independent private hospitals spread across the UK. This organisation was crowned “Private Hospital Group of the Year” in recent years and is recognised as one of the “UK’s top 5 best big companies to work for” as well as the top 5 to work for in health and social care. We are looking for a Cardiac Physiologist who is looking to take the next step in their career. You will have completed your BSc in Clinical Physiology or equivalent and hold a BSE accreditation. You will be able to plan perform, interpret and report on all cardiology investigations as well as, TTE, TEE, 2D and 3D echo, bubble echo, dobutamine echo and stress echos with and without contrast. Having pervious Cath Lab experience, specifically with pacemakers, EP and ICD implantations, is advantageous. The successful candidate will have the opportunity to build and grow the cardiology service within this hospital, working closely with and reporting to the Clinical Service manager. This is a full-time permanent position where you will work 37.5 hours per week. The hours of service are 09:00 – 17:00 Monday to Friday. There are no weekends and no on call!
