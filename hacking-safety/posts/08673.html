Are you self-conscious of your smile? Learn how to love your smile with teeth whitening in Howard Beach, NY, and begin your journey to whiter, brighter teeth at Howard Beach Dental Associates. At Howard Beach Dental Associates, we understand that our patients want to look and feel confident when they smile, which is why we ensure our services for teeth whitening near you provide the best and most immediate results. When you visit our licensed dentists in Howard Beach, NY, they will first assess your suitability for teeth whitening with a standard dental examination. When the dentist assesses you are suitable for the treatment, they will then walk you through the best options for teeth whitening available. Our most popular option for teeth whitening is Philips Zoom Whitening which is the perfect option for patients who would like to receive quick results. Phillips ® ZOOM! Whitening is one of the more popular teeth whitening options, as this service can transform the color of your teeth eight shades in less than an hour. A dental examination before ZOOM! Whitening is mandatory as our patients’ safety and comfort are our main priorities. Along with your treatment with the dentist, ZOOM! Whitening provides take-home whitening trays – allowing patients to brighten their smiles at home! Your dentist will first cover your lips and gums leaving only your teeth exposed for treatment.
