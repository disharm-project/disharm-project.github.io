We are an Illinois-licensed and Medicare-certified home health agency that provides skilled, individualized, and comprehensive home health services to our patients. Our clinicians are not only highly trained professionals, they also treat their patients like family. It is our belief that by combining those skills with genuine compassion, we will truly change the delivery of health care as well as the lives of every patient we touch. Our Registered Nurses are licensed, highly professional, skilled, and trained to assist patients and their Physicians with planning, coordinating, and implementing individualized assessment, care coordination and management. Our RNs also provide education and materials targeted to patients’ specific needs. Our Physical Therapists provide restorative techniques to improve coordination, balance, strength, and mobility. They also provide home exercise programs as well as teaching the safe and proper use of assistive devices, such as a cane or walker. These rehabilitative programs are intended to maintain and restore a patient’s ability for movement as well as to treat impairments. Our Occupational Therapists are skilled to assess and provide patients with energy conservation techniques, assistance in adaptive devices training, and individualized care plans to support in the recovery of patient independence. Occupational therapy services help patients recover by engaging in normal daily living activities through a structured and guided plan from our professional OTs. Our Speech Therapists assist with vocal strengthening, cognitive deficits, memory recovery, and other methodology to assist in patient recovery. Our Medical Social Workers provide assistance with community and financial resources as well as counseling services. They serve as community liaisons to help patients and their families acquire the necessary resources for specific needs to be met. Infusion therapy involves the administration of medication through a needle or catheter. Typically this is prescribed when a condition isn't responding effectively by oral medications or to support nutritional requirements. We offer this service by our well-trained nursing staff to those in the comfort of their home, which allows for less complications and better clinical outcomes. Our Home Health Care Aides provide personal care and assistance with the activities of daily living, including bathing, grooming, and dressing. More than 100 million Americans are at risk of developing complications from diabetes. Those individuals need help learning how to manage this chronic and often overwhelming disease. Our goal is to reach people in Illinois who are living every day with diabetes and help them gain the knowledge, skill and self-care behaviors to achieve the high quality of life they deserve. Medical Nutrition Services aim to assist patients with chronic conditions such as diabetes and cancer. Soul Care Services employs evidence-based practices to educate patients on personal and medical factors that can impact their conditions. We provide positive reinforcement to encourage self-motivation. The patient’s loyalty to the plan is deemed essential and it requires self-management for the plan to succeed. Our LiveWell programs and services are designed to help improve the lives of patients with chronic conditions. Each program is tailored for a specific disease or condition, and includes best practices for reducing adverse events and enhancing each patient’s quality of life. Soul Care Services, Inc. will provide appropriate, caring, professional services without discrimination regarding race, creed, color, religion, sex, national origin, disability (physical or mental) or age.
