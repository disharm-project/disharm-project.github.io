Lydford House provides a relaxed and friendly atmosphere within the Dartmoor National Park, an Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty. Operating as hotel for over 100 years, the house was built by the celebrated artist William Widgery (1822-1893). Now a family run venue the hotel is undergoing extensive improvements; updated with the latest technology and the redesign of the interior decor has restored some of the Victorian charm and period detail.
