News release from St. John Lutheran Church: Brown Bag Bible Study coming to downtown Fort Wayne October 4th

(September 28, 2012) – Looking for something to do over your lunch hour from your job at a downtown Fort Wayne business? Come grow your mind and stretch your spirit at a weekly Bible Study! You can even bring your lunch to strengthen your body as well! Beginning Thursday, October 4, 2012, one of the downtown churches will begin offering a Bible Study intended for Christian and spiritual seeker alike. The location, the basement conference room of The Lincoln Tower, was chosen in order to make the study both close to most of the downtown business district and comfortable for any who may have had negative experiences in church buildings in the past. The schedule, which has 7-8 minutes of gathering time, enables folk who only have the noon hour for lunch to make it to the study without missing any of the content. On the other end, we will finish about 7-8 minutes before 1:00 to provide time for folk to return to work by 1:00 p.m. Further details are printed below. In addition, click here to download a flyer.
