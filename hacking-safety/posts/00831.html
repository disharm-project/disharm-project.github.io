The transition process generally happens in three visits. A longer transitional period will occur if your child needs more time to settle in the centre. There are no charges / fees during the transition period. First Visit Your first visit is a welcoming process for you and your child to become a little familiar with the centre’s surroundings and happenings. A chance to sit back and allow your child to explore what activities are being done, and meet our centre staff and children. We encourage you to stay for at least 20 minutes, but you are welcome to stay longer. Second Visit On the second visit you will help to settle your child into the centre, before you say your farewells and leave your child for approximately 10 minutes with centre staff as you take a small wander outside the centre. Third Visit The third visit will consist of you settling in your child, as the second visit. We ask that you leave your child at the centre with staff for approximately 45 minutes to one hour. Please leave your contact details with the staff in case we need to contact you. We do lots of messy and water play so please have lots of suitable clothing in bags, and named please (especially shoes!! ).
