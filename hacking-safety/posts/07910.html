BY JENNIFER CABRERA GAINESVILLE, Fla. – Adam Lee Clemens, 33, and Mark Lance Irwin II, 36, were arrested last night and charged with aggravated assault after allegedly pointing guns at two different women. Clemens and Irwin were reportedly in a verbal argument at Brookwood Terrace Apartments when Clemens allegedly pulled a gun from his car and pointed it at a woman. Irwin allegedly responded by taking a firearm from his residence and pointing it at a different woman. The incident was reportedly observed by several independent witnesses who provided statements to the responding Gainesville Police Department Officer. The firearm that was allegedly displayed by Clemens could not be located; the firearm displayed by Irwin was reportedly found to be a BB gun that looked like a real firearm. Clemens was charged with aggravated assault with a deadly weapon. He has one felony conviction from 2013; Judge Thomas Jaworski set bail at $2,000. Irwin was also charged with aggravated assault with a deadly weapon. He has four felony convictions and served a 4.5-year state prison sentence for oxycodone trafficking; he was released in 2019.
