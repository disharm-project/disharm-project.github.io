With the third season of Castlevania set to premiere early next month, Netflix has posted a new video that not only shows returning actors, but also the new performers joining the fray. That includes Jason Isaacs (Star Trek: Discovery), as well as veteran actors Bill Nighy and Lance Reddick. After all, Dracula never let death stop him in the Castlevania video games! Netflix also debuted a trailer for Castlevania‘s third season, which sets the stage for the new story. Regardless of Dracula’s death, Carmilla still has a vampire army of her own. Additionally, Trevor, Sypha, and Alurcard will be called back into action when Carmilla strikes. Meanwhile, Isaac also has a mission to find Hector, and save him from Carmilla. However, Isaac may not be aware that Hector betrayed Dracula’s trust. That may also lead to a fatal outcome for the two devil forgemasters. The new season of Castlevania kicks off on March 5 on Netflix.
