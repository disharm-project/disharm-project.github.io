It all began with a dream and a mobile spray tanning booth. Mind blowing, since Blondie's has grown into an award-winning 3,000 square foot full-service salon, spa, and boutique that employs 20 team members! Blondie’s Founder and Owner Lindsey Babinec got her start in 2008, quickly outgrowing her spray tanning setup and evolving to her first brick and mortar location in Columbus, Indiana. She couldn’t predict how high the demand would be in her hometown for reliable beauty services and gradually expanded her business into what it’s become today, located off Washington Street in the historic downtown of Columbus. Meet Penny Hundley, our radiant and passionate Artistic Director. With a warm and inviting presence, Penny has dedicated herself to the beauty industry for an impressive 35 years. Her unwavering commitment to learning and staying ahead of the latest trends has elevated her to the status of an industry expert, particularly in the realms of hair replacement and hair extensions. What truly sets Penny apart is her genuine desire to make every guest who sits in her chair feel comfortable and confident. With her skilled hands and creative vision, she strives to deliver nothing short of breathtaking results. Penny understands that beauty is not just about appearance, but also about embracing one's individuality and inner radiance. Penny's love for all things beautiful extends far beyond the salon walls. When she's not transforming hair, she can be found surrounded by her cherished family and friends, enjoying their company and creating lasting memories. Her love for art and music is a constant source of inspiration, infusing her work with a unique and artistic flair. Meet Sidney, your trusted partner in the world of anti-aging skin micro needling. With a warm and friendly demeanor, Sidney genuinely cares for her clients' well-being and it shines through her every interaction. Sidney's goal is to empower you by providing effective and personalized solutions that address your skin concerns. She makes sure to also educate and guide you through achieving a healthier and more youthful complexion. When not at the Spa, you'll find Sidney cheering on her girls at sporting events, cheering on Alabama (Roll Tide! ), or cheering on her beloved friends. If you'd like her to cheer you on too, book with Sidney for your next Spa appointment. Through training and experience I have developed a style that is effortless yet sophisticated. I enjoy precision in my work, because I find the better I perform in the Salon, the better you can style your hair at home. I strive to interpret rather than copy today’s trends, and to translate them into your everyday lives. I am inspired by Modern Art, Architecture and design (mostly Mid Century Modern), and I am amazed by the beauty in nature and all people. In my spare time I enjoy relaxing with family and friends, and playing in the pool or hiking and biking the trails. I also love to travel, and consistently love to immerse myself in different cultures and places near or far. As a guest in my chair, I hope to develop a relationship based on care and trust in the fact that my ultimate goal will be to make you feel beautiful, confident, healthy and ready to take on the world. Blondie's offers advanced services performed by a talented team of salon and spa professionals who are continually learning the best ways to deliver results so that you can leave your appointment feeling empowered and confident.
