Uche maduwagwu rejects n3 million offer to frame mohbads wife

Uche Maduwagwu, the outspoken actor, has revealed a shocking incident where he was offered N3 million to falsely accuse the late Mohbad’s wife, Omowunmi, of wrongdoing. Maduwagwu disclosed on social media that shortly after Mohbad’s death, unknown individuals lured him to Lekki, Lagos, and proposed paying him N3 million to defame Omowunmi. The plan involved implicating her in a fabricated story, including a demand for a DNA test on her child. Despite facing economic difficulties, Maduwagwu resisted the temptation and refused to tarnish someone’s reputation unjustly. He reflected on his decision, stating that he didn’t want to compromise his integrity for financial gain. Maduwagwu emphasized the importance of considering the consequences of one’s actions, even in times of desperation.
