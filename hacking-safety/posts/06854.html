Tropical fruit punch

Subscribe For Some Easy to Make Weekly New Recipes! Subscribe For Some Easy to Make Weekly New Recipes! This Tropical Fruit Punch is something I’ve made for many years. Usually, I make this punch in August, but this time I decided to make it in July. It’s a pitcher full of different juices and fruits served cold, perfect for a refreshing taste. I love my punch made with tropical fruits, The taste is delicious and gives you a good boost. I used pineapple juice, orange juice, mango juice, passion fruit juice, coconut water, lemon juice, grenadine, and optionally sparkling water or Sprite. It’s so refreshing especially when served cold. And of course, you can ignore or add fruits you like. I love storing this punch in the refrigerator after enjoying it. Every night when I feel thirsty, I pour myself a glass and enjoy it. It usually doesn’t last more than a day because the whole family loves it, so I guess everyone else has the same idea. Table of Contents This is a Tropical Fruit Punch made of pineapple juice, orange juice, mango juice, passion fruit juice, coconut water, lemon juice, and grenadine. The taste is so delicious due to the tropical mix of fruity flavors used. So why not to try make it home, Is refreshing, easy to make, and has a good tropical taste that for sure you will enjoy. I prefer to use a pitcher in this recipe, But a big bowl also will work, So use a pitcher and pour in the pineapple juice, orange juice, mango juice, passion fruit juice, coconut water, and lemon juice. and Stir everything. Pour in the grenadine. Watch as it sinks to the bottom and then rises up, making a pretty layered effect. Stir gently if you want the color to be even. This is an Optional step, If you want your punch to have bubbles, pour in some sparkling water before you serve it. you also pour Sprite, I tried it last time and it was good and added a great flavor to the drink. Cut some fresh fruits like oranges, lemons, limes, and pineapples into slices. Add these slices to the pitcher to make it look pretty and taste even better. Put ice cubes in glasses and pour the punch over the ice. You can add a fruit slice on the rim of each glass to make it look fancy too and Enjoy. If you have any leftover punch, put it in a container with a lid and keep it in the fridge. Drink it in 1-2 days maximum. If you add fizzy water, it won’t be bubbly later, so add new fizzy water when you drink it again. I love having a cold glass from this Tropical Fruit Punch with soft desserts like cakes, muffins, and breads. So try to enjoy it with warm banana bread studded with chocolate chips or fluffy cupcakes with some frosting. Fruit tarts topped with fresh berries make a fruity and sweet addition to your meal, while a big bowl of creamy Mamey Ice Cream is perfect beside it on a hot day. You can also enjoy it with some cookies, brownies, or homemade popsicles made with your favorite fruits. Yes, You can use any favorite tropical juices you like. You can reduce the amount of grenadine or add more lemon juice for a tangier flavor. This recipe is already non-alcoholic. Yes, you can add a splash of vodka if you want. Mint leaves, cherries, or berries make great garnishes for this punch. Yes, frozen fruit can be used as well. This recipe makes about 8-10 servings, depending on the size of your glasses.
