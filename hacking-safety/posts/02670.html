Since Disabilty People 24CARE Australia has operated with great success as a registered NDIS provider in the Australian Capital Territory & Goulburn regions, since 2020. “24CARE Australia has a rich legacy of creating a safe, nurturing, and welcoming environment for individuals in our community living with psycho-social, intellectual, or physical disabilities. We take pride in our proven track record of delivering high-quality services aligned with the standards set by the NDIS (National Disability Insurance Scheme). At 24CARE Australia, we are dedicated to empowering all our participants to lead lives of inclusiveness, independence, and well-being. Our mission is to provide each of our participants with tailored and personalized care that enables them to embrace and enjoy everyday life with comfort, ease, and self-love. Through compassionate support and individualized attention, we strive to enhance the quality of life for every person under our care, fostering a sense of belonging and empowerment within our community." Our aim is to empower participants to reach their fullest potential by maximizing their capacity for independence in personal care, domestic activities, household decision-making, and problem-solving skills. We are committed to providing personalized support and resources to ensure that every individual, regardless of their challenges, can become an independent and engaged member of the community, enjoying a fulfilling and meaningful life.
