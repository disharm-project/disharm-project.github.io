Looking for the best low sugar rice cooker? Low-sugar rice cooker is not your ordinary type of rice cooker. Also known as a low-carb rice cooker… This is an alternative healthy way of cooking rice for those individuals seeking to reduce starch content, especially on white rice. Low sugar rice cookers became popular because of their advertised health benefits. Especially for persons with type 2 diabetes. Notice: “As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases”. If you’re a health-conscious type of person… Or, if you are a diabetic individual who likes to consume rice… Having a low-sugar rice cooker would allow you to reduce the content of starch which is highly present in white rice. Which could be beneficial for you and your healthy living aspirations. Everyone has preferences on what is important or not when deciding on what to buy. But I want to offer my opinion on the most important things that the Best Low Sugar Rice Cooker should have. Double Straining System Logically, this kind of low-sugar rice cooker would produce the best effect since there will be two ways of filtering. I have discussed this in my previous article about an Introduction to Low Sugar Rice Cooker. With double filtering… The rice is separated from the water, while the starchy water that escapes during boiling (water vapor) is trapped at the top and will not be able to go back down to the cooked or steamed rice. That ensures that the slimy water is separated from the cooked rice. Rice Basket for Separation Other low-sugar rice cooker designs only have rice baskets. The purpose of the rice basket is to separate the rice grains from the water. This is the method of separating the cooked rice from the remaining glutinous water at the bottom of the inner pot. Top Water Container Like the rice basket, other low-sugar rice cooker designs only provide the top water container as the means of filtering the starchy water that escapes during boiling. This should be the minimum requirement for low-sugar rice cookers to have. As the water vapor escapes to the top vent, the water trapped on the lid will be held by the container and won’t be able to come back down to the cooked rice. Specifications:
