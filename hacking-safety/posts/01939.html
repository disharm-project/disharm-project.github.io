Dr Richard Raubolt . For now I hope you enjoy the films. -Dr Richard Raubolt

This site is a home for my documentary films but I hope to make it more. Documentaries are, well, about documenting the human condition. For me this means documenting by image and word the subjects I find most compelling both professionally and personally. I am not a filmmaker. I have had excellent, patient, gifted and dedicated technical help with “my docs”. As a psychoanalyst/psychologist my interest focuses on how people live: the choices they make, the conditions under which they labor or celebrate but most of all the stories they tell. We are all our stories. We construct narratives about who we are, what we believe and where we belong. Often these stories are formed in conflict, crisis or the result of trauma. There are many ways of telling these stories. I will include some of them on this site – poetry, commentary, essay and the visual arts – some expressed in my voice and often, I hope, in the voices of others. Your comments are welcome.
