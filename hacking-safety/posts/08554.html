Objective: To create and validate 2 models called RENSAFE (RENalSAFEty) to predict postoperative acute kidney injury (AKI) and development of chronic kidney disease (CKD) stage 3b in patients undergoing partial (PN) or radical nephrectomy (RN) for kidney cancer. Methods: Primary objective was to develop a predictive model for AKI (reduction &gt;25% of preoperative eGFR) and de novo CKD &gt;= 3b (&lt;45 ml/min/1.73m(2)), through stepwise logistic regression. Secondary outcomes include elucidation of the relationship between AKI and de novo CKD &gt;= 3a (&lt;60 ml/min/1.73m(2)). Accuracy was tested with receiver operator characteristic area under the curve (AUC).Results: AKI occurred in 452/1,517 patients (29.8%) and CKD &gt;= 3b in 116/903 patients (12.8%). Logistic regression demonstrated male sex (OR = 1.3, P = 0.02), ASA score (OR = 1.3, P &lt; 0.01), hypertension (OR = 1.6, P &lt; 0.001), R.E.N.A.L. score (OR = 1.2, P &lt; 0.001), preoperative eGFR&lt;60 (OR = 1.8, P = 0.009), and RN (OR = 10.4, P &lt; 0.0001) as predictors for AKI. Age (OR 1.0, P &lt; 0.001), diabetes mellitus (OR 2.5, P &lt; 0.001), preoperative eGFR &lt;60 (OR 3.6, P &lt; 0.001) and RN (OR 2.2, P &lt; 0.01) were predictors for CKD &gt;= 3b. AUC for RENSAFE AKI was 0.80 and 0.76 for CKD &gt;= 3b. AKI was predictive for CKD &gt;= 3a (OR = 2.2, P &lt; 0.001), but not CKD &gt;= 3b (P = 0.1). Using 21% threshold probability for AKI achieved sensitivity: 80.3%, specificity: 61.7% and negative predictive value (NPV): 88.1%. Using 8% cutoff for CKD &gt;= 3b achieved sensitivity: 75%, specificity: 65.7%, and NPV: 96%.Conclusion: RENSAFE models utilizing perioperative variables that can predict AKI and CKD may help guide shared decision making. Impact of postsurgical AKI was limited to less severe CKD (eGFR&lt;60 ml/min 71.73m(2)). Confirmatory studies are requisite.
