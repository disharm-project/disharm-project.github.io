A fan clarifies her position to queen letizia on the royal family the rest fuck you

Queen Letizia has resumed all her appointments after the Holy Week mini break. The last, without going any further, took her to Cordoba, more precisely to the Gran Teatro to preside over the day of the Talent Touran initiative that aims to guide and encourage young people in their respective initiatives and, in turn, to reveal the name of the winner of the Princess of Girona Prize for Arts and Letters 2023. Before starting the act, Doña Letizia took a mass bath, since everyone present received her with applause, shouts and cheers. Indeed, many of them did not hesitate to greet her and send her many expressions of affection. Something that caused a huge smile on the queen. During this mass bath, the Doña Letizia had a real moment, which neither she nor any of us will forget, thanks to a fan who complimented her; that yes, not before throwing a big taunt at the rest of the royalty. The Queen came over to greet one of the admirers who kept calling her name to get her closer. So far everything is normal. The most surprising thing was when she started shouting the following words at him: “Madame Letizia, Madame Letizia! I love you very much, your husband and your daughters. The rest, fuck you!“. Faced with this message, the queen managed to utter a brief “¡vaya!“, while the fan continued to insist that the four members of the royal family were “awesome”. Those words sparked laughter and amazement from everyone present, even if judging by the face by Doña Letizia, it felt more like one of those “earth swallows me” moments in which no one knows how to get out of trouble. The Queen continued to greet the rest of the people who crossed her path until it was time to preside over the act. At that time, everyone noticed the same thing: her look. Doña Letizia opted for the most colorful and spring-like clothes, which caught the attention of everyone present. Queen Letizia greets fans. / Getty / Carlos Alvarez

And it is that we are very little used to seeing her wear prints and on this occasion, Queen Letizia dared with a floral look that she really liked. It was a midi dress from the firm Cayro Woman in crepe fabric and puffed sleeves, which left no one indifferent, since it was another of the most commented points of the day.
