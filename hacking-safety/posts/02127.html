Published May 28, 2013 Tickets for the highly anticipated Frank Ocean concert this summer will go on presale tomorrow at 10:00. Frank Ocean has been a prominent figure in the hip-hop world over the past year for the release of his critically lauded album, ‘channel ORANGE’, which won the 2012 Soul Train Award for Album of the Year as well as nominations for several Grammies. The Odd Future collective member also made headlines last summer for his open statements with regards to his sexuality. His show on July 16 at Laugardalshöll is expected to sell out quickly by promoters Sena, so snap up yours fast! Numbered seats cost 13,990 ISK, and general admission is 8,990 ISK.
