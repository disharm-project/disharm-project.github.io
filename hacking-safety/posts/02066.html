Four helpful tips to save money on bar nights

Going out to bars can be quite expensive and if you are on a tight budget, this can mean compromising on some experiences. However, your visit to the bar does not have to mean you lose your rental budget. There are ways to keep a night at the bar from becoming a bank-breaking exercise. Below are some tricks to help you save money on bar nights: Decide How Much you are Willing to Spend Beforehand Knowing the amount of money you want to spend on a bar night may be a simple concept; however, many people are not mindful of what they want to spend before they begin purchasing drinks. By being aware of what you will spend, you cut down on the possibility of a hangover and an empty wallet. Take Advantage of Drink Specials The majority of bars provide drink specials and deals on a lot of nights of the week. Paying attention to these specials will let you save plenty of money by planning your nights out ahead of time based on what is available for cheap and what you like. Check if your favorite Mad Hatter pool bar has drink specials through their website or Facebook page. If you are at the bar and you plan on returning there, take a photo of its specials with your phone you can refer to later. Go Easy on Shots Shots are a quick spur-of-the-moment thing you can easily get caught up with before you realize the amount it will cost you. Meanwhile, cocktails, beer, and wine tend to take longer to drink, so purchasing one is often less of an impulse decision. For many bar-goers, beer and cocktails are better to get the most out of the money. But, you can also go for shots as long as you get them deliberately. Be a Good Tipper Tipping the bartender is a practice that will help you make a great first impression and establish a relationship with them if you want to be a regular of the bar. When you give good tips, you will get perks over time including better drinks, occasional drinks on the house, and faster and friendlier service. Those who go to the bar have money and you will be competing against them when trying to capture the bartender’s attention. However, you can easily beat the competition if you are a good tipper as bartenders will prioritize you. bandarqq
