Through life's memorable journey I'm sharing my family, milestones in our life and everything about home. My little boy started his school break this week while the two girls will report to school up to next week to get their clearances. I’m now thinking of places where we can spend some days away from home to relax and have some fun. First thing on my mind is to visit our hometown and spend some days there then we’ll go to the nearest beach there which is only a town away. I’m also asking the husband if he has some vacation ideas that we can plan now that summer vacation has started. We just have to schedule it before the summer workshop of the kids or after one month of their classes. Classes usually last for one month and we can all plan to go where we want to go. Hubby will have 2-day discipleship seminar in Baguio at the start of May and after that our schedule is more manageable. I know that many families now are planning their own vacation here in the country or abroad. Well maybe MyCitiesGuide.com can help you plan your own vacation with their Trip Planning Tool. They have collection of vacation ideas and travel destination guides that will assist you in whatever you need to know about destinations, restaurants, art galleries, entertainment center and a lot more. They can help you with the things you don’t know about what you should do before and after each destination and things like that. They can recommend where to go for exquisite dining or for some other vacation concerns. You can check them out now and see their featured vacation destinations. Hometown vacation is good. You will besides of enjoying and relaxing you can also bond with you relatives. Happy vacation. Post a Comment
