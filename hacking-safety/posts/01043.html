The comedians themselves were good, in my showing, but that like always is a bit of a crapshoot with these sorts of things, due to the daily swap over. The twist on the formula in the form of the bucket was, itself, a bit of a mixed bag (or bucket, I suppose.) Some of the prompts were solid, and allowed the comedians to mix up their set and keep things fresh and spontaneous. Other prompts were really unfortunately repetitive, with multiple fairly milquetoast sex prompts coming in one after another. Still, the pre-prepared stand-up was good, and the prompts themselves are different each night, so this likely isn’t a consistent issue.
