Hire a reckless driving attorney to help you with your ticket

personalinjurylawyerwins.com · http://personalinjurylawyerwins.com/2023/03/28/hire-a-reckless-driving-attorney-to-help-you-with-your-ticket/

2 of 44 sentences flagged

It is really easy to start driving too fast and getting reckless on the road. If you have been pulled over because a police officer thought you were being reckless while driving, you want to be able to protect yourself. You should immediately hire an attorney because there are several things that they can do to help you out in this situation. So, what can an attorney do for you? Plea Bargains When you talk to your attorney about the situation, they will give you the best advice they can about your case. Depending on the state where you live and how many tickets you already have to your name, you may have to spend some time in jail. Your attorney can be the go-between for you and the district attorney's office. They can negotiate with the DA to get you the best deal possible. They may be able to get you a lesser fine and no time in jail. No Points Whenever you get a traffic ticket, points get added to your driver's license. If you get enough points, you can get a suspended license. Having too many points can also cost you a lot of money because it can make your insurance rates rise. Your current insurance company may drop you if you get too many points and have to work with an insurance company that handles high-risk drivers. That will definitely mean you are paying a higher premium, at least until you get all the points off your license. Traffic Court If you have to appear in traffic court, that can mean that you have to take several hours out of your day because traffic court doesn't give you an exact time when your case will be called. You are just told to appear on a certain day, and you just have to wait until your case is called. When it is called, you have to talk to the judge. You may not know how to handle yourself in court because there can be some very particular rules about it, and what you see on TV differs from how you should act. Your attorney can appear with you and be the buffer between you and the court, which can help make any consequences better for you. If you have been pulled over for reckless driving and given a citation for it, you need to find an attorney who can help you. For more information, contact a professional like Jerry O. Talton Attorney At Law. Share
