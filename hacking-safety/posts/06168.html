The first look of 2015 and it seems my love of checks is not waning… of course there is the Scottish element to be considered in my fondness for them but there’s also something every appealing to me that is a print without being a print exactly. Stripes almost certainly fall into the same category with both adding a new dimension to a look without the pitfalls that often come along with other ‘awkward’ prints. Reviewing things at the end of the year it transpired that the most popular post was ‘The £855 Burberry saving scheme’ (another easy print) and it seems to be some kind of symmetry that the first post of this year features a similar saving scheme of sorts… Let’s call it ‘the £2000 Delpozo saving scheme’ shall we? It was impossible to miss this tartan rainbow of beauty from fashion season but even in the Net-A-Porter sale my balance was falling short by the value of a reasonable car… So I’ll say thanks to Topshop for this offering, pop it in my basket, swiftly move along and hope Mr Delpozo doesn’t notice.
