The design was inspired by Yoruba embroidery and is framed against a polka dot background. These cushions are redolent of traditional African wax prints but with a fresh, modern feel. A particularly pretty shade of blue, paired up these cushions are a conversation starter on your sofa. Use as inspiration for a sitting room scheme or as an accent to invigorate a calm palette of neutrals, perhaps in a bedroom. Undeniably, a hit for summer sofas and homes in sunnier climes especially teamed with a riot of brights. The bold, printed cotton design with pale velvet reverse would look particularly fabulous on a mid-century modern, tan, leather sofa or buttoned Chesterfield. Try Vinterior for retro Scandinavian and vintage sofas. We also rather like the idea of juxtaposing these bold, turquoise cushion covers with cosy, chunky-knit throws and cushions in a palette of winter whites too. A design for all seasons. Never feel you must dress your sofa with scatter cushions to match the upholstery. Often, these are optional add-ons when buying a new sofa. Always, create an original look. Furthermore, assemble your own ‘cast of cushions’. Buy from different fabric houses. Create an ideal comfort level. Browse our destination-inspired cushion range to add global more vibes to your home. The turquoise cushion covers measure 50cm x 50cm and come with a duck feather pad for a perfectly plump finish. The covers are made from 100% cotton with a white, cotton velvet piping and reverse plus an invisible zip. All Eva Sonaike cushions are printed and made in the UK.
