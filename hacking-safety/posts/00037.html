659 Enrolled Students View Trinity's Student Profile Trinity University's Admissions team can help you plan your campus visit, answer questions about the application process, and connect you to the wonderful faculty, staff, and students who call Trinity University home. Find a team member on campus or a counselor in your area. READY TO APPLY? What application type should I choose? How will my test scores be evaluated? I have AP credits or I have taken dual-credit courses—will they count? Can I take a gap year? Let the Office of Admissions help you understand your application details and options. First-year, transfer, international, and graduate applicants can find unique selection criteria for their application types. Undergraduate applicants for first-year students entering Trinity in the Fall 2024, Fall 2025, and Fall 2026 qualify for Trinity’s standardized test-optional admissions policy. Undergraduate applicants may apply Early Decision, Early Action, and Regular Decision.
