Home » Blog » Do You Qualify for the SUB Ductless Heat Pump Program? Ductless heat pumps can decrease your heating costs since they operate more efficiently than other units. Updating your home’s heating system is a big decision. To make the choice especially appealing and to encourage more people to upgrade to an energy-efficient operation, some homeowners are eligible for the Springfield Utility Board, or SUB, Ductless Heat Pump Program. If you’re considering installation, contact us at Jco Heating A/C Electrical! Get a 0% interest loan or $800 rebate to help pay for the purchase of an energy-efficient heat pump. You’re eligible for the program if you have a single-family home or one that’s manufactured and are currently using electric forced air to heat your home. You’re also eligible for the program if you use a furnace or a permanent zonal electric heating unit to heat your home. Those who use natural gas or any other fossil fuel to heat their homes can also take advantage of the program, but only if their heating system heats less than 50 percent of the home’s total living space. You are not eligible if your home is heated by a fossil fuel central furnace, fossil fuel hydronic system or ducted heat pump system. There are several options available to encourage you to transition to a ductless heating and cooling system. For instance, the agency is offering a $500 cash rebate for energy-efficient ductless heat pump upgrades on qualified homes. Along with this, you may be able to receive a zero-interest loan for the system. To qualify for the program, you will need to meet minimum insulation levels. Along with the rebates and no interest loans that are available when you upgrade to a ductless unit, you’ll be doing your part to help the environment since you’ll be using less energy to heat your home.
