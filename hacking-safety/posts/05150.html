Written by Allen Wyatt (last updated July 7, 2018)This tip applies to Word 97, 2000, 2002, and 2003

You know you can use the Field Codes check box on the View tab of the Options dialog box to control whether or not field codes are displayed in your document. You may even know that you can use Alt+F9 to switch between field codes and their results. However, what if you are mouse-bound, and want to accomplish the same task quickly without resorting to the keyboard?
