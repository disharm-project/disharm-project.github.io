Well, it looks like the White House is lying again. The administration is scrambling to cover up Biden’s terrible debate performance. Many are saying Biden’s cognitive decline is so bad, that he’s not fit for office. But the White House claimed he just “had a cold,” which is supposed to explain why he couldn’t finish a sentence. Since then, the administration has been trying to spin a narrative to keep Joe running. This comes as many Democrats want him to drop out now, while they still have time to find a replacement. Reporters demanded answers from the White House press secretary. Karine Jean-Pierre, as usual, carried the White House narrative rather than copping to the facts. When reporters pressed her over whether Biden has seen a doctor since February, she said no. But soon afterward, Biden made this alarming contradiction. From Daily Wire: President Joe Biden reportedly told a group of Democratic governors on Wednesday that he saw a medical doctor after last week’s debate against former President Donald Trump, contradicting a statement made by White House Press Secretary Karine Jean-Pierre on the same day. During a White House press briefing on Wednesday, Jean-Pierre told CNN Senior White House Correspondent MJ Lee that Biden has not undergone “any kind of medical exam” since his annual physical in February. “We were able to talk to his doctor about that, and that is a no,” the press secretary said. “He hasn’t had any kind of medical exam?” Lee asked again. Jean-Pierre simply replied, “No.” How pathetic. This broken-down, dishonest joke of an administration can’t even keep its own lies straight. For years, it has ignored questions about Biden’s mental health. It has come up with pathetic excuses for the obvious signs of decline. And it has long refused to admit to the administration’s many failures, which have hurt millions of Americans. Karine Jean-Pierre told reporters that Biden had not seen a doctor since the debate. Biden then told governors that he saw a doctor. Now, another administration official claims it was just a check for that fake cold they blamed his performance on. They are spinning KJP’s comment that he didn’t get a “physical.” Is this White House ever going to tell the truth? Reporters asked her whether Biden had a “medical exam.” That was the question and that is what she said “no” to. Not to “Did he get a physical.” We should assume Biden hasn’t seen a doctor. Joe lied about that to the governors—since he wanted to convince them he is fit to run. KJP was caught between Biden and the truth and they had to spin it yet again. I don’t know why KJP keeps putting up with this pathetic administration. She is never prepared with facts and has to constantly lie to cover up the aging Biden. Is she getting something out of this sick arrangement? Why not quit now and get a comfy job with NBC, similar to what the last press secretary did? I’m sure it pays a lot better, lying for them.
