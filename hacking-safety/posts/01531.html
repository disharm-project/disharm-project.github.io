Heidan Construction May 31, 2022 By Webmaster Luxury custom homes are a great aspiration for most people. They have so much to offer to professionals, those heading into retirement, and successful young couples making an investment in their future. However, we can’t ignore one of the most common demographics in the country: families! When you have kids, luxury custom family homes are what you’re looking for. And those come with a few extra perks. Here’s what Heidan Construction could do to improve your family’s day-to-day life. Tired of the headache of yelling up the stairs for your kids to come down for dinner? Want to check if your teen is awake in time to leave? A home intercom system is a fantastic addition in luxury custom family homes. They make communication a breeze. Sure, you could text each other. But who wants to feel tied to their cellphone even in their own home? Plus, sometimes text notifications go unheard. Speaking directly through the intercom means contacting each other from anywhere in the home, at any time, without going unnoticed. Not only does this improve communication, it also lowers stress. Did you know that households with a lot of yelling have higher stress levels? This doesn’t just apply to households where anger is the source of the raised voices. Humans just naturally get tense when people are yelling back and forth. Release that tension with an intercom. While no one wants to have their belongings stolen, there’s a bigger concern with potential break-ins: safety. Break-ins can be extremely dangerous to anyone in the house when it occurs. Most robbers aren’t there to hurt people, but fear and adrenaline can lead to irreversible harm. If you’re going to invest in a luxury home, investing in a high quality security system is a must. Your home isn’t just attractive to you; it’s also attractive to those looking for a quick buck. Running a household and caring for a family is a lot of work. If your schedule is full and you’re tired of details falling through the cracks, you’ll benefit from smart-home controls. Many parts of the home can easily be controlled remotely these days. With an app on your smart device, you can control: Say goodbye to leaving the house and wondering if you turned the alarm on. Adjust the thermostat on your way back from summer vacation so that your luxury custom family home is cool when you arrive. Unlock the front door for your sister so she can check on the dog while you and the kids are away for a retreat. Want a custom home tailored to your exact needs? Give us a call at Heidan Construction and we can work together to design one just for you. Get everything you want and nothing that you don’t, all in a beautifully designed package.
