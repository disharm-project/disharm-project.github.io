Are you looking for an engaging culture and work environment? Do you want to make an impact on couples’ lives? Apply to join the hospitality dream team! See our current career openings posted below. We are committed to being a great place to work which is why we offer excellent benefits! Our employees can take advantage of affordable health care options, including medical, dental, vision, life and disability insurance. At Walters Hospitality, we understand the value of taking a step back and enjoying life outside of work. That’s why we offer all exempt, full-time employees unlimited paid time off to stay healthy, enjoy family time, and manage outside responsibilities. As a way to bond and recharge batteries, every year, we invite our top performers to an all-inclusive, paid destination retreat! This gives our exceptional talent across the company an opportunity to connect and share ideas, bond through planned activities, enjoy exquisite team dinners, and of course- have lots of fun in the sun. Walters Hospitality is growing – and it’s growing fast. This means that our employees have ample opportunity to expand and advance their careers. To boost their future, we also provide them with training and development opportunities tailored to their roles and futures. To put it simply, we are building a culture where personal and professional growth is just as important as company growth. We put full trust and confidence in our managers – and we know they’ll get the job done while taking the utmost care of our clients. Flexible schedules are a reflection of that confidence! We want our managers to find a work-life balance that perfectly suits their individual needs. Here at Walters Hospitality, we work hard to make our clients’ dream weddings come true. But that doesn’t mean we don’t have fun along the way! Throughout the year, we host optional fun-filled outings and happy hours for each region and department – just one of the many reasons why our employees are happy to be a part of our team! Explore the listings below and find the perfect fit for you! We can’t wait to hear from you. A Hiring Manager will reach out directly if they feel you would be a fit for our company!
