The way out of boredom

“The Very Bored Fluff Ball” is the most lazy and bored creature on this planet. It was just another normal day of watching TV, eating, and sleeping until a stranger barged into Fluff’s house. The stranger looked around, disgusted at the sight of its messy room and insisted going on a trip to Italy betting she would cure its never-ending case of boredom. The Fluff gets dragged outside, and shoved into the airplane. Will the stranger be successful in helping this bored little creature out of its continuing boredom? Hello! My name is Grace Choi. I went to so many amazing places while living in Italy and got inspired to write this book. I wanted to share my experience with those who are unable to come to Italy due to COVID-19.
