You know youre a postcrosser when

How do you go about making your cards? Do your print at your home? Hi! I use Moo.com, but there are other options that are less expensive, like Canva and Vista print. Search the forum, there is a whole post about printing your own cards. I’m traveling overseas, so searching and posting a link for you is a bit harder for me at the moment. Last Thursday I felt like a real Postcrosser again, when the lovely ladies at the philately day gave me their business cards and asked for my phone number! Wow! My most recent “you know you’re a postcrosser” moment: I went to the post office to pick up a package, and was assisted by a postal worker who I have never spoken to before. As he was handing me the package, he looked at the mailbox address and asked “Don’t you usually receive postcards?” I think he must have recognized my mailbox address as the mailbox getting all the postcards Mine was, when a person joined this, and they got a profile saying “Yes, another profile from this country” The new member thought, what another? Did I get one before this somewhere? I knew, no, it’s just a common country, people “talk” sometimes like this in their profile. Their second address went to Inge Löök card collector, who listed their received ones like this 4557/54445/8686/6888/ and few rows of these numbers. The new postcrosser thought there is error happened I knew at once, no, it’s listed the numbers of the Inge Löök cards they have. I know postal rates better than the postal workers themselves… Postal worker: - Why twenty? Me: - Across country, not registered, not an envelope, not air shipment… Postal worker: - Hmmm, really twenty… I have one more! Last week I had a very stressful day and in the evening I indulged myself a cup of coffee and a bun in a place a bit more expensive than I’m used to. And you know what? I stared at the cup and the bun with the only one thought: “I could have sent six (6!) cards abroad instead!” … if someone asks about your worst nightmare, and you say: “An empty mailbox”. … you look at some painted window in a restaurant and you think “now that would make a nice postcard” Postcards, stamps, and postcrossing supplies are considered essential items in your head, and spending on them is always justified and a priority. I’m sure something along those lines has already been mentioned, but we just can’t overlook it. You know you’re a PostCrosser when you’ve had to explain how much postage is needed for a postcard to post office workers more than once. Happened to us again today. The post office lady inspected our cards and was not happy with the postage put on them, so we had to explain again… and again… and again. Then she complained that the postcards did not say “postcard” either on the front or back, so how can she know they’re not letters? I mean, really? When people pay you in postcards. Haha, so true! Or when people find it an absolute must to tell you of any new fun and interesting postcards/stationery stores they’ve come across, and always tell you excitedly, “You’re going to love it!!”. “Why are you doing something odd like buying and sending postcards?”, someone would ask, chewing something with too much sugar looking like this fancy new … “Think of all the other stuff you could buy instead!” “Well, I prefer cards over chocolate bars”, you reply looking slightly disgusted on the weird juice licking out of the other persons mouth, while she*he still is munching. “It’s much healthier, less sugar, less calories and keeps you moving, learning and being compassionate.” And while you go on musing about the advantages, you dream of establishing a postcard yoga studio, teching classes in basic postcard awareness and starting studies on the relation of happiness and postcards. “I’m sure, it’s the future”, you end and then you need to hurry to catch the mail carrier to send your cards of the day. Some problems are the same all over the world x Its a good day when you rush to catch the 4pm Post Its 4:03 but he is just coming round the corner in his red van as you drop cards through the slot…YES When you’ve missed the 4pm post and rather than have your cards sitting in a cold atmosphere over night potentally weakening stamp adherence you hang on to them and get up earlier to walk further to a 9am collection Probably said before by now, but when you look at your “0 left [to send]” total like this: When you see a weird or strange postcard, or a card you don’t like, and think, what wishes that card could fulfill.
