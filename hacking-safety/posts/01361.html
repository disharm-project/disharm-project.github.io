eXploreRound check before you decide Are you looking for the best store to buy quality and classy fashion items and you came across cubitty.com? Are you contemplating placing an order? Before doing that, read this review to the end to find out whether to go ahead with your purchase or not. It is common to see several products online claiming that they provide the best services in the market. This is as a result of the fact that people are always seeking for easier ways to get quality services with lesser cost. Therefore, one need to thread with caution. Table of Contents Cubitty.com is an online store that claim to specialize in the sale of fashion items such as Overall, Leggings among others. The store is said to be aimed at providing quality products at a cheap price. The email address given on their website is [email protected] Is this store legit as it seems? Certainly not! Read on to find out the hidden truth about this online store. Below are some of the suspicious information we found about this store This store is said to be owned by Kentesh Limited and their office is at 71-75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London, England. WC2H 9JQ. The truth is that the above information is suspicious. This company does not have a good reputation. This company and address is being used by other scam stores. This goes a long way to prove that shopping with them is very risky. We usually advise shoppers to purchase products from well known brands whose authenticity have been verified in order to reduce the risk of being scammed. Looking at their website, you can see that each of these items is being sold at a discounted price. In addition, they are offering free shipping on orders over $29. The discount margin is really unbelievable. Offering mouth watery discounts is one of the tactics commonly used by shady product providers to lure customers into purchasing a product as quick as possible. Therefore, we advise our readers to always be careful and carry out due diligence when dealing with stores like this. We found negative reviews from users of this store on Scamadviser as well as other external sites attesting to the fact that the items ordered are either not delivered to the buyers or the items delivered are of inferior quality. Their email address is not even functional so getting a refund is impossible. This shows that we are dealing with an unreliable store. One of the things that online shoppers should look out for in a website is how long it has been in existence. Unfortunately, cubitty.com happens to be a newly registered website. It was just created in December 14, 2022 and expires December 14, 2023. This shows that the store lacks credibility. Online shoppers need to be cautious when making purchases or using services from a very new website because they lack security. This store is not even present in any of the social media platform. The icons on their website are just used as a disguise. This does not speak well of the store. A trustworthy store should be visible in at least one of the social media platforms. The answer is NO! This is not a trustworthy store. It has poor trust rating, negative reviews online as well as other red flags that should not be ignored. They will end up ripping you off your hard earned money if you decide to buy anything from them. We do not endorse this online store. Other stores we have reviewed are Marvna, Nohmn and ninapod.com Have you purchased any item from cubitty.com recently? Kindly drop a message in the comment section telling us about your experience.
