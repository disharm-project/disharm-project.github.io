Aged Residential Care Service,

Community Health

Surrounded by park-like grounds and award winning gardens, Te Puke has a beautiful rural outlook and great indoor/outdoor flow. Close to town, it's just a short drive to local eateries, shops and other amenities. Originally a Colonial style hotel, our building is large, giving us plenty of space. The corridors are wide and airy, and we have several lounges for you to socialise in with other residents. We have a library as well as a billiards room, and there are several other nooks and crannies dotted around the place where you can sit and relax with your friends and family. We offer various activities and it is completely up to you whether you join in or not. Our industrial size kitchen makes it easy for our cooks to cater to our residents' individual preferences and produce tasty home-cooked meals. Features of Te Puke: We offer a range of activities and entertainment that gives you the opportunity to keep busy, learn new skills and get to know the other residents. You can get involved as much or as little as you like - it’s up to you how you spend your day. At Bupa Te Puke Care Home, we choose all our team carefully to make sure that you get the care you need, from people who are kind and respectful. Older adult / Kaumātua You can arrange a time to visit us by contacting our Care Home Manager, Chris Boudet on (07) 573 9983 and you're always welcome to drop in and see us during business hours for a chat. If you are caring for a family member or friend and feel that you need some respite, you can use Bupa’s short stay services. We’ll manage everything while you recharge and have your ‘me time’. Our short stay services can range from a few days to a few weeks. Admission is dependent on bed availability. Service types: Aged residential care, Respite care.
