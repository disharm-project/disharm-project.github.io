Royal Copenhagen has produced a number of cute colored Christmas cups, unlike the old blue traditional Christmas cups from the same manufacturer. All Royal Copenhagen Christmas cups are designed by Jette Frölich and includes Christmas cup and saucer, and a beautiful cake plate. The base color is white with beautiful decorations in color surrounded by a decorative golden edge. Royal Copenhagen Christmas Cups and Saucer Multi Colour Royal Copenhagen Christmas cups with coloursFrom 1997 to 2002, Bing & Grondahl released a series of beautiful Christmas cups with cake plates, designed by Jette Frölich. When the series ended in 2002, Royal Copenhagen took over the assignment of making Christmas cups in the same style. At the same time they were of course also producing their usual blue Christmas cups. In 2003 and 2004, the cup was a little taller and more angular with an oval pickle dish. In 2005, Royal Copenhagen issued a Christmas china cup and saucer in the same form as the previous Bing & Grondahl series and this continued up to and including 2008, when the series ended. All Royal Copenhagen Christmas cups are designed by Jette Frölich and includes Christmas cup and saucer, and a beautiful cake plate. The base colour is white with beautiful decorations in colours surrounded by a decorative golden rim.
