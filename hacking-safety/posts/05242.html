This app will allow members of Immanuel Lutheran GR to receive announcements, latest updates and interact with each other. It allows prayer requests to be sent and viewed by other members to create a social prayer board. It allows members to view and edit their member directory entry. It offers a truly social experience for church members. Version 15.14.0 - Create your Profile and make the app your own. Get better visibility of your notifications and gain access to our latest features.- The ability to give feedback in an easy and anonymous way directly in your message inbox.- Select the notifications you want to be subscribed to. Get custom recommendations. Easier to opt-in to what you want to know and opt-out of what you don’t.- Create engaging, customized push notifications with images, emojis, and more.- Access your audio on your lock screen. Now you have the ability to play, pause, fast forward and rewind.
