According to a bourse submission, Yeow Seng has proposed that IOIPG acquire all or section of his exclusive vehicle, Shenton 101 Pte Ltd, that is planning to redevelop Shenton House, works for which are arranged to start rearmost of 2025. Yeow Seng and his sibling Datuk Lee Yeow Chor are significant investors of IOIPG through their substantial shareholdings in Vertical Capacity Sdn Bhd, which carries 65.67% in IOIPG. “Yeow Seng has emphasised to IOIPG that Shenton 101 is prepared and able to go ahead with the property development organizing of Shenton House within the conditions of the tender and that Shenton 101 is well on the way to implemented funding to allow it to go on with the redevelopment and also the factor that Yeow Seng is expanding the proposition to IOIPG is to assist deal with or deal with the probable conflict of interest situation,” IOIPG’s declaring read. Shenton House covers 3,377 square metres and is designated for retail usage with a gross plot ratio (GPR) of 11.2. KUALA LUMPUR (June 25): IOI Properties Group Bhd (KL: IOIPG) has gotten a recommendation from its group president cum major shareholder Lee Yeow Seng to take part in the development of Shenton House, a business property located in Singapore that his special vehicle has effectively tendered for, for S$ 538 million (RM1.9 billion). The present added present funding obligation– leaving out the development cost, that is to be finalised– is S$ 476 million, which includes land enhancement fee, lease top-up costs, and operation expenses, it said. According to IOIPG, Yeow Seng has actually recommended the purchase consideration be determined based on the real price of assets acquired by himself and Shenton 101, multiplied by the equity interest in Shenton 101 to be acquired by IOIPG, or an equal subscription worth for the membership of new stakes in Shenton 101. At market close on Tuesday, IOI Properties’ shares dropped four sen or 1.75% to RM2.25, giving the business a valuation of RM12.39 billion. IOIPG claimed the plan stands for 4 months, which may be extended by an additional 2 months if a written application is obtained from IOIPG. “Further, according to the Singapore’s major business district reward scheme, Shenton House is qualified for a 25% reward gross floor area which can be redeveloped into a mixed-use commercial with residential project or a hotel at the GPR of 14. As such, Shenton House is allocated for redevelopment into a fresh 99-year leasehold business enhancement,” IOIPG said.
