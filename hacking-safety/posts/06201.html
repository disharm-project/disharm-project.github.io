In our society celebration is often associated with having a drink, patting each other on the shoulder or banging a huge gong after a sale has been made. That is better than nothing. Yet, that is not the celebration I have in mind here. I invite you rather to think of an event that caressed your soul this week, that touched you. Let that warm feeling sink in you. Let it uplift you. Be proud of your achievements - bathe in them in the intimacy of your own inner world. So, now tell me:

How do you want to celebrate about this week? How to build resilience in a gentle way Magic DOESN'T happen outside the comfort zone. This is where ... Stop being a good girl! Break the rules :) Courses Terms & Privacy Contact
