The deniers

latin-american.news · https://latin-american.news/the-deniers/

2 of 22 sentences flagged

Latest news from Latin America

Within the scenario that dominates us in the Western world (“I respect you as long as you think as I order”)… there are dissenting voices. They call them denialists and it’s a good thing they exist. Among these voices are those of politicians Viktor Orban (Hungarian Prime Minister), Giorgia Meloni (Italy Prime Minister), Donald Trump (former United States President), Ronald De Santis (Governor of Florida), Santiago Abascal ( President of Vox in Spain), Isabel Diaz Ayuso (President of the Community of Madrid). Added to these are many people who openly express their opinion on the networks (and even censor them), and others who speak softly in their homes… so they don’t insult them or call them “conspiracy-ridden” or “insensitive”. Everyone… with more or less courage… dares to have common sense and express themselves without a complex against the media chatter that they have tried to impose on us… as if it were something hegemonic… without admission of questioning. They are the ones who defend the traditional family, as the fundamental axis of a healthy society. They are the ones who oppose the promotion of homosexuality, sex change and transvestite to children in schools. They are the ones who make fun of the exaggerations of climatologists, who keep alarming about an end of the world that never comes. They are the ones who laugh at the ugly feminazis, who do not shave, when they complain that “men harass them.” They are the ones who resist wasting time speaking with inclusive language. They are the ones who oppose the uncontrolled invasion of immigrants… without fear of being blackmailed by calling them racists. They are the ones who did not believe in the confinements or in the mandatory nature of the experimental anti-covid vaccine. Little by little they are seducing people. Above all four types of people:• Those who already realize how absurd politically correct speech is… that it cannot offend… that it must waste time choosing pronouns in the name of diversity… that extols victimhood… and that seeks all kinds of mitigating circumstances to the perversion.• Those who do not want their children to be indoctrinated in schools against their moral values.• Those who need to go out to find their livelihood every morning. And they cannot pay more taxes to finance the cause of the “saviors of the planet or of the turtles and seals from God knows where”, nor of the “activists for abortion and pro-uncontrolled immigration”.• The smart ones. Time and victory at the polls will tell if this seduction becomes a beacon of hope towards a more decent and sensible West. Hopefully it will be like that. Source link
