The Blog Australian lifestyle blog, Chic on the Run, is brought to life to highlight all the marvellous things in life: fashion, motherhood, beauty and food. It is a destination for the curious to discover the most fascinating trends, latest beauty tips or delicious eats right at your fingertips. Everything from the craziest new fashions to the tastiest international dishes. Natalia, creator of Chic on the Run, shares her favourite finds and upcoming trends. Her hope is that this space will encourage and inspire her readers to enjoy the little things in life that we sometimes take for granted. The Author My name is Natalia and by day I work as a PR Consultant in Canberra, Australia. By night, I love to write and run a little blog called Chic on the Run. I am hopelessly addicted to trashy reality shows, overindulging in ice cream, smothering my pets in affection and drinking red wine with my best friend. I am a new mum, a fitness nut, a budding chef, a curious traveller and a make up addict. When I’m not at my computer writing my blog I’m usually playing with the latest beauty products, playing with my daughter, at the gym with my husband or somewhere overseas exploring the world. I am obsessed with everything life has to offer and always looking for the next big adventure. I hope you enjoy reading my blog. xx Natalia Elissa O'Keefe You were a joy to look after Natalia.
