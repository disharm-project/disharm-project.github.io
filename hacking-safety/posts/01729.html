Kiwi House Care Home offers residential, dementia, respite, and daycare in Alvaston This family-run care home in Alvaston, Derby offers award-winning, luxury and high-quality residential and dementia care. Our friendly, well-trained team ensures person-centred care with residents encouraged to lead an enriched life, supported by our engaging social programme packed full of clubs, trips out, and regular entertainment. Each care suite has lounges, lifestyle kitchens and dining rooms, a range of large en-suite bedrooms, cinema, pub, hair salon and spa bathrooms. Overlooking Kiwi Park, our pleasant sensory gardens have patio seating areas. Residents with dementia are supported with sensory areas, reminiscence activities and memory boxes. We’re actively welcoming new residents to Kiwi House. Our team is ready to welcome new residents who need care and support and we are here to provide help and advice to ensure you find the right care for you or your loved one. From creative activities such as painting, baking and gardening, to regular visits from entertainers such as singers, dancers and musicians, religious services and outings, our activities are intended to inspire and engage our residents as individuals and as a community. We pride ourselves on well-being – Visit Kiwi House’s Facebook page by clicking on the link below to find out more We have a great choice of lifestyle facilities within the home, including spa bathrooms, a hair salon and nail bar, The Woolpack Pub and the Flicks Cinema where our residents regularly get together to watch their choice of film. With six separate care suites, each with its own restaurant-style dining room and lifestyle kitchen, as well as a café, our residents have a great choice of dining options. We take pride in offering high-quality food, chosen by our residents and prepared by our experienced catering team. Kiwi House offers luxury, high-quality residential, dementia, respite and daycare. The home has a range of lounges and relaxation areas, residents can find the perfect mix of social interaction or privacy. Our Care provides comfort and peace of mind, tailored around each individual and their own unique needs. You will always find our well-trained and dedicated team members on hand to assist, providing the highest quality of care.
