3 zodiac signs with confidence issues

2 of 16 sentences flagged

Virgo, your pursuit of the unreachable might sap your confidence. You may spot every little error or oversight, but the rest of the world rarely analyses things as critically as you do. You're a high achiever, but you notice the minor things so well that you may feel that your job is worthless if it's not flawless. Virgo, remember that nobody else sees it that way—even those examining your accomplishments is unlikely to uncover any errors! Cancer, you're wonderful at connecting with others, yet your willingness to listen might leave you vulnerable to outside viewpoints. Sometimes people' opinions or casual remarks make you rethink your decisions or value. Your emotional depth lets you love and understand people, but it also makes you subject to others' opinions. Cancer, you must learn to trust your own judgment. You can ask for advice and assistance from loved ones, but your life is your own. It takes effort, but you'll succeed. Pisces, your fluidity might make you question your place in the world. Your creativity is unmatched, yet it can leave you feeling unanchored, leading to self-doubt and concerns about your purpose and destiny. You may feel that you have unattainable aspirations or that your life is better in your thoughts than in reality. Walking the line between your aspirations and reality may be tricky, so it's not your fault if you're unsure where you stand. Learning how to ground yourself may help. Enjoy your life, dreams and all, and take small measures to make it as enticing as your dreams.
