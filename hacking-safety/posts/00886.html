Visit Stay Recreation Services Events Area Members Non-Profit and Charities Show More Bluegill Festival is back for 2024! Grab your family and friends and head on over to Birchwood for the best block party in Northwestern Wisconsin! ​ Come out and enjoy, music, crafts, food, parade, and celebrating our amazing community! Scroll below for a list of events going on all weekend and a daily weekend schedule! The Medallion Hunt begins July 17th, and continues until it is found.​ ​ The finder of the medallion is awarded $75 to those wearing a 2024 Bluegill Button, and $25 if not wearing it. (Best to get a button!) Clues will be posted daily at Gary’s Grocery. If you find the medallion, congrats, and bring the medallion into Gary’s Grocery, fill out a finders card and leave it at Gary’s Grocery. Schedule: ​ ​ Friday, 4pm - dark

Saturday, 7am - dark

Sunday, 8am with afternoon championship Get your collectors buttons and pins! ​ Buttons are available at Dairy State Bank. Queen candidates will also sell buttons. Purchase as many as you'd like for a chance to win 1 of 3 cash prizes. Drawing will be held immediately after Sunday's parade. Need not be present to win. Saturday, July 20th | 9am-3pm Get Crafty! ​ Browse from a series of vendors and artisans selling a variety of goods! Located on the Northwest corner of the Birchwood School, next to playground. ​ Please follow signs for parking. ​ Interested in being a vendor? Click Here Saturday, July 20th

All proceeds go to Timber Bay, a non-profit helping community youth. ​ Registration: 6:45am - 8:00am Kids Fun Run Half Mile: 8:15am Start Annual 5k Run/Walk: 8:30am Start ​ Cost: 5K - $20, Half Mile - $5 (Max of $50/family)

Pre-register Today for a T-shirt! ​ Race Starting Line at Birchwood School, 300 S Wilson St. Birchwood Sunday, July 21st ​ Grab a spot along Main St. for Birchwood's very own Bluegill Parade. ​ The parade starts at 1pm along Main Street ​ Interested in being in the parade?
