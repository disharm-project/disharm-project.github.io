Just as living in the world of love, dating, and glamor is considered a status, similarly love marriage is also a common thing, but maintaining this marriage is not within everyone’s reach. Getting into a relationship and then getting divorced is a common thing in the industry these days. There is a lot of fighting in Bollywood regarding relationships and divorce. In Bollywood, where even marriages of 10-20 years break easily, there are some relationships where couples divorce each other within a few months of marriage. In Bollywood, where even marriages of 10-20 years break easily, there are some situations where marriages break within just 2 months. Today we are going to talk about such high-profile marriages of Bollywood whose marriages could not last even for a year. Bigg Boss Season 4 saw Sara Khan and Ali Merchant get married inside the Bigg Boss house and their love story was blown out of proportion. However, most people called it a publicity stunt because, within just 2 months of coming out of Bigg Boss’s house, the couple realized that they would not be able to live together and their married life broke down. The marriage of Karan Singh Grover and Shraddha Nigam also did not last long. Both of them married each other at the very beginning of their career. Both of them got divorced after just 10 months of marriage. According to reports, Shraddha filed for divorce when Karan Singh Grover’s name started being linked with her co-actress. At that time, there was also talk about Karan’s relationship with a dance choreographer. After this, Karan Singh Grover married Jennifer Winget but that marriage also broke within two years. Both of them lived with each other for a few years and were dating each other. However, the couple divorced in November 2014 after 11 months of marriage. It is said that the reason for their separation was the growing friendship and closeness between Pulkit Samrat and Yami Gautam.
