No day at PCC is the same. Walk onto our campus and you will see the bustle of activity, feel the energy of attending college, and welcome the friendship of fellow Lancers. The PCC experience is vibrant. It's unique and personalized to each student. It's fun, it's exciting and it's the college experience that you make it to be. We like to have fun. Our calendar is busy with campus-wide events that provide you with engaging activities and opportunities to interact with your peers. You can also join one of our 95+ campus clubs to meet others on campus while pursuing a common interest. Choose from political organizations, cultural groups, community service clubs, academic alliances and more! A community is waiting for you! We are a diverse community made up of different races, ethnicities, national origins, socio-economic classes, genders, sexual orientations, and religions. Our Cross Cultural Center advocates for an inclusive campus climate while hosting events and activities to promote diversity and social justice. The Pride Center is a resource for LGBTQIA+ students, staff, and faculty members. They provide a safe and supportive environment that fosters community, empowerment, and celebrates the unique identities and experiences of the LGBTQIA+ community on campus. Additionally, Safe Zones identifies allies in the PCC community who are knowledgeable about the needs of Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, Transgender, Queer, Intersex, and Asexual (LGBTQIA+) and/or undocumented people, and proudly display a safe zones sticker indicating that they are an advocate and have cultural competence. Go Lancers! Whether you're playing hard on the field, court or pool, or you're in the stands cheering for your peers, PCC Athletics add a fun, competitive piece to student life. 16 teams competing in 11 different sports make for year-round excitement.
