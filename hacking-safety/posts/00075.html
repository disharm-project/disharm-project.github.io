Fly is a gorgeous fawn male that just turned 4. His birthday was November 20th. He is a very small guy that raced at just 64lbs. Fly is seriously handsome and is a very good boy too. He quickly settled into his foster home and learned the house rules nicely. Fly is a happy, playful, friendly guy that still seems almost puppyish at times because he is so small and cute. He is a very outgoing, curious guy that doesn't seem to be upset or scared by anything. He loves people and loves to snuggle and will lean on you to get just as close as possible. He's learned "out" (as in, out of the kitchen), "outside", "go to bed" (go to your crate please! ), and "wait". Fly has a higher prey drive but was able to live with a small dog that looked like a white bunny in his foster home. He just needed a few sniffs to realize that it was a dog and not something he should chase. Like many higher prey drive greyhounds he automatically assumes anything small is a bunny or a squirrel until he sniffs it. Because of his prey drive Fly has to have a home without cats. If you have a small dog we can test him individually with your dog since he has been OK with most with proper introductions. He plays nicely with the other dogs in his foster home but prefers to be with people most of the time over playing with other dogs. Fly can be loud when left alone in a crate so would not be a dog for apartment or townhouse living. If you want a cute, loveable guy that will be totally devoted to you Fly is the one! Fly is a hero!! He donated blood at our vet in an emergency situation to help save a Great Dane!!!
