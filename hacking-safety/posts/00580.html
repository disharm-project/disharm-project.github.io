Justin joined our pastoral team in January 2011 as our Pastor of Worship and Arts. Justin is married to Liesl and they have two daughters, Amber and Summer. Justin began his pastoral ministry in Howick, Kwa-ZuluNatal, where he served as a youth pastor for five years. Prior to joining Rosebank Union, he served for 10 years in pastoral ministry at Honeyridge Baptist Church. Justin’s passion and longing is to see people from all walks of life set ablaze in worshipping our great God.
