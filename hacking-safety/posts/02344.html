Home About Advisory Board Live Courses Online Courses Free courses For Companies Blog Join Now More How’s your summer going? Are you enjoying long lazy days? Are you reconnecting with friends and family? Are you eating lots of salads? Are you exercising outdoors (with your sunscreen on of course)? Finally reading that book you bought back in February? Or are you working as hard as ever, worrying about a million little things (and a few big ones), stressing over Q4, driving your kids here, there, and everywhere, and eating more far more popsicles than tomatoes? In other words, are you recharging or are you slowly burning out? Maybe it’s time to create your personal recharge ritual. A recharge ritual isn't a one-time fix. It's a daily practice that refuels your energy and resilience, and creates a space to disconnect from the world and reconnect with yourself. Reflect on your energy levels: When do you feel most drained? What activities restore your energy? Consider your stress triggers: What situations or tasks deplete your energy? Explore your senses: What sounds, smells, tastes, textures, and sights rejuvenate you? Here are some key practices to consider: Mindfulness & Meditation: Dedicate even 5 minutes a day to calming your mind. Mindfulness exercises like meditation or deep breathing can significantly reduce stress and improve focus. There are numerous free guided meditations available online or through apps. Movement & Exercise: Get your body moving! Physical activity is a fantastic mood booster and stress reliever. Find activities you enjoy, whether it's a brisk walk, a dance class, or a yoga session. Even short bursts of movement throughout the day can make a difference. Nature Connection: Immerse yourself in the natural world and connect with your senses. Take your lunch break in a park, go for a hike after work, or simply spend some time sitting outdoors. Studies show that spending time in nature reduces stress hormones and improves mental well-being. Creative Expression: Unleash your inner artist! Engage in activities that allow you to express yourself creatively, whether it's painting, writing, playing music, cooking, or any other form of artistic pursuit. Try our free Summer Journaling Guide. Connection: Social connection is vital for well-being. Brunch on a patio, a gossipy city or nature walk, drinks at sundown, family fun time (that’s actually fun for everyone, including you), it doesn’t matter what it is, just be sure to connect with some peeps you love this summer. Read for Joy, not Work: You know that pile of books you have beside your bed? Put down your phone and pick one up! Self-care isn't a luxury; it's a necessity. Schedule your recharge activities just like you would any other important appointment. Treat your self-care time with respect and avoid letting work commitments override it. Building resilience against burnout requires consistent effort. Make your Recharge Ritual a part of your daily routine. As you experience the positive effects of self-care, celebrate your victories! Feeling more energized, focused, and fulfilled? That's the power of the Recharge Ritual in action. Start small: Begin with a short recharge ritual and gradually increase the duration. Experiment: Try different activities to find what works best for you. Disconnect: Minimize distractions during your ritual time. Self-compassion: Be kind to yourself and allow for imperfections. Remember, the goal of a recharge ritual is to create a personalized space for rejuvenation. Experiment with different elements and find what brings you the most peace and restoration.
