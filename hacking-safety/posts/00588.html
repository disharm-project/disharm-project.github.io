Red white and blue quilt patterns

cottonandjoy.com · https://cottonandjoy.com/blogs/blog/red-white-and-blue-quilt-patterns

Shipping & taxes calculated at checkout Whenever I think of early summer, that time right after Memorial Day and the 4th of July, I can't help but think of red, white, and blue decorations. Maybe it's because my husband was in the military, in addition to very American holidays, that these months just feel like red, white, and blue. In true quilting fashion, the change of season always makes me want to start planning my next quilt – so this season is all about the red, white, and blue quilts! I don't always have time to sew every quilt I want to make, but I've put together a round-up of modern quilt patterns with this patriotic color palette inspiration for you to help get you in the spirit of early summer! Which one will be your next quilt? Mighty Stars quilt pattern is a modern take on a classic block, the friendship star. This pattern is originally is fat-quarter friendly, but it has a new yardage-friendly update! I recently made a new Mighty Stars quilt inspired by the Modern Service Quilts version. Because of their gorgeous Mighty Stars quilt, I created a "Ribbons Extension" pattern so you to make a red, white, and blue quilt just like them! Check it out on my blog now. The Starly quilt pattern combines modern and classic quilt patterns for an eye-catching look. The classic sawtooth star block is wreathed, creating a fun secondary design between blocks. The Starly design works well with both solids and prints, making it the perfect quilt to try with your favorite bundle or favorite fabric collection! Recently, Riley Blake Designs created mockups with one of their patriotic fabric collections, and I love how Starly looks as a red, white, and blue quilt! The Starlight quilt pattern is a modern sawtooth star pattern, made up of a mix and match design. You can use your favorite FQ bundle, scraps, or a layer cake. One of my favorite parts of this design is how the sawtooth stars pop out in the "white space". You can play around with lots of color combinations and still have those hidden stars right there! The Golden Hour quilt pattern is my take on a modern medallion quilt. In a medallion construction the quilt top grows out from one central block, with each new section being added to and building off of the previous. You can also find the Golden Hour cross stitch pattern available in my shop too! This would make such a cute red, white, and blue decoration for your home! Joyful Stars is a fun and modern star quilt pattern that works well with solids, prints, or scraps. No tricky piecing makes this a great beginner quilt pattern. My Scrappy Navy version is the quilt that made me want to put this whole summer quilt patterns list together! I always kick myself thinking about how festive the quilt would have worked with red and white striped binding fabric! Stellar Flare is a clean and modern star quilt pattern. This is the perfect pattern for a newer quilters or for seasoned quilters looking for fast sew. While the original pattern does not include the scrappy version shown below, you can check out my Scrappy Stellar Flare blog post here to see how I accomplished this look! The Reverie Quilt Pattern was originally designed to pair with my Moonchild fabric collection, but I've found that this quilt pattern is incredibly dynamic and can be used for just about any situation! Stick to the modern ombre version to create a beautiful red, white, and blue quilt. You can also stick to an all blue theme on the top and finish it off with some bright red binding fabric. Or grab a Layer Cake pack of your favorite patriotic fabric collection for a fun scrappy look! ← Older Post
