The Armorial Register - International Register of Arms - Albrichi, G.

International Register of Armorial Bearings (Coats of Arms)

Giovanni

Albrichi

Registered: The

International Register of Arms, 20th September 2023. Registration No. 0680 (Vol.4) Arms: Per fess Azure and chequy of eight Gules and Or, a bar Argent and in chief a lion passant Or. Crest: A lion’s head caboshed Or. Historical

Record: XIX century seal. There

is no grant of arms in existence. However, the authenticity and antiquity of the coat of arms are certified by a carved marble block from the ancient family residence and a XIX century seal. The family is an ancient Burgher family that resided in Milan, Italy. The arms are not canting, and their origin is unknown. At the death of Giovanni, the arms were inherited and used by his only daughter, Ester (born 29/05/1875), who married 1897 Vincenzo de Guidi and moved to Tuscany. The arms have been registered at the request of Daniele Scarpi. Ester is the mother of his mother's mother. © The Armorial Register - International Register of Arms
