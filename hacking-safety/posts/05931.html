All the latest news and updates from Services for Young People Services for Young People projects enable young people in all districts of Hertfordshire to develop their personal and social skills and increase their confidence, self-esteem and resilience. Services for Young People delivered 206 Go Wild sessions in the past 12 months, boosting the emotional wellbeing of 592 young people. Over 170 young people came to the ‘Enchanted Forest’ themed Winter Prom event held at Pioneer Young People’s Centre in St Albans on Friday 15 December. 22 young people completed the Duke of Edinburgh's Gold Award Residential section or achieved the John Muir Discover Award with Services for Young People in 2023. Services for Young People is working in partnership with the St Albans and Dacorum Delivering Special Provision Locally groups to offer a series of drop-in support sessions for families dealing with the impact of emotionally-based school avoidance. Services for Young People is proud to support this year’s campaigns against gender-based violence. Our work with young people helps them to develop healthy relationships and challenge harmful attitudes to create a safer future for everyone. Services for Young People celebrated the resilience, determination and compassion of 157 young people from across Hertfordshire at their four Young People’s Awards events during National Youth Work Week. During Talk Money Week, 6-10 November 2023, and in response to young people’s feedback about their worries about the cost of living and the future, Services for Young People is highlighting the financial information and advice it offers to young people across Hertfordshire. From 6 November until 20 December, young people aged 11-19 in Hertfordshire can have their voices heard by taking part in the annual Young People’s Health and Wellbeing Survey. The results will be used to help plan future services which meet the needs of the young people of Hertfordshire. Services for Young People has partnered with Mission 44 to launch the ‘Access to All’ programme, aiming to inspire more young people in Stevenage to pursue careers in science, technology, engineering and maths (STEM). The new Services for Young People Explore More+ careers education programme is to be delivered in schools across the county following the success of the pilot programme in Stevenage. Services for Young People will be holding its annual Young People’s Awards events as part of the National Youth Work Week celebrations this November. To mark this year’s National Youth Work Week, which is running from 6th to 12th November, Services for Young People have planned a range of activities and events across Hertfordshire. Services for young People helps a Hertfordshire business develop its workforce and enables a young person to secure their ideal apprenticeship. This September, Services for Young People hosted celebratory events across the county to recognise the achievements of young people who successfully completed their Bronze and Silver Duke of Edinburgh’s Awards More than 90 young people visited the Services for Young People stand at the annual Herts Pride community event at Cassiobury Park in Watford this August. Positive feedback about Services for Young People’s holiday activity programmes highlights their impact on young people's confidence, health and wellbeing. This Sexual Health Week we are raising awareness of the sexual health support available at our young people's centres. St Albans Youth Council members are excited to be involved in shaping the future development of recreation spaces for children and young people in Harpenden and St Albans. Services for Young People is working in partnership with the charity ‘Herts Welcomes Refugees’ to support young refugees in Hemel Hempstead and the surrounding areas. Practical Partners, a collaboration between Services for Young People and GoVolHerts, will offer a new form of support for care leavers during their transition to independence. The project aims to equip care leavers with important life skills, paving the way for a successful future. Young people attending Services for Young People’s projects last month came together to celebrate Pride Month and express their unique identities through music and art. We are looking at ways to redesign some of the services we offer to young people in Hertfordshire. We want to hear from young people, parents/carers, professionals and other stakeholders about what is most important to them. You will receive support from Services for Young People at every stage. Are you interested in working with young people to develop their skills and conﬁdence to enable them to succeed? We have vacancies available in our local teams across the county. Services for Young People provides youth work groupwork and one-to-one support. Hertfordshire County Council Services for Young People Site by Pluspurple
