A surprise wisconsin elopement

Yep, you read that title right, A SURPRISE!! Ali + Luke were planning on a June 20, 2020 wedding, but in the early stages of COVID-19, decided to reschedule to November. I was planning on photographing their new November wedding date when all of a sudden I received an email from Ali on JUNE 18th! 48 hours before they got married! The email went a little something like, “Hey Tarah, TOTALLY ok if you are not available, but we are getting lunch at our original venue on Saturday to celebrate our would-have-been wedding and we were joking around with the idea of surprising everyone and just getting married and now I think we are going to do that, are you available?” I was gearing up for a surprise Wisconsin elopement! In true Tarah fashion I responded with a big, “HECK YEA LET’S MAKE IT HAPPEN.”And so we did, or rather they did Ali’s uncle got ordained online 48 hours prior, her 3 best friends helped set up a tablescape at the winery to make it feel more like a wedding, and EVERYONE WAS SURPRISED. The best part? The guests thought they were surprising Ali + Luke with a fun celebration lunch becuase Ali’s friends made sure they all came, but Ali + Luke were actually surprising them back.
