Publisher Great Idea Games announced the launch of their satirical visual novel, International Affairs today on Valve Corporations digital distribution platform, Steam. Set in the ultra-exclusive world of the UN, International Affairs challenges players with the task of romancing powerful world leaders while attempting to uncover an urgent and sinister threat to global security. With fully branching paths, sparkling dialogue, and a dazzling array of globetrotting hunks to choose from, the prospect of achieving world peace has never been so sensual!
