6 custom products that can beat stress beyond stress balls

www.proimprint.com · https://www.proimprint.com/blog/6-custom-products-that-can-beat-stress-beyond-stress-balls/

Stress has become part of the hectic life of the modern age. Around 75 % of adults in the world say they often experience high to moderate levels of stress due to various factors like job pressure or financial issues. Marketers can handout stress busting promotional products for their consumers to show how much they care for their happiness and mental health. Not very keen about the customary stress busting ideas of stress balls? Don’t you worry, we have a whole lot of custom gifts that are as effective as stress balls to beat stress. In this post, we have listed a few promotional products that will help your customers relieve stress –beyond the classic stress ball and you would be wondering why you didn’t think about these before! 1.Puzzles Let your customers take their puzzle cracking skills to family picnics, weekend parties or office with this delightful range of custom puzzles. Choose from a delightful range of exciting models like master pyramid puzzle, slider puzzle or mini trap 3D puzzle among others. Your name and logo imprinted on these custom toys will get a lot of attention from anyone who sees it who may even be tempted to try it for a while. This fun giveaway will get shared among friends and family members of your audience, which will further enhance your brand popularity. These fun toys will be a cool corporate gift idea too as these will help the stressed-out employees to let off some steam while at work. 2.Hot/Cold Eye Mask A refreshing hot/ cold eye mask will make a great way for your recipients to stay cool and relaxed amidst their back breaking schedules or during long trip. It will make a great handout that will help your customers relax and unwind after a long day and to be active on the day ahead. 3.Monkey toys This goofy toy will leave everyone in office in splits for sure. These solar powered dancing monkey toys can make desktops interesting and will make your brand the talk of the town literally. This delightful toy will make a great handout during business events and tradeshows as they will never fail to make people laugh. Promote your message in a light-hearted manner and leave your audience happy and stress free with these custom toys that are born crowd pleasers. 4.Chocolate Studies show chocolates release happy hormones and make people relaxed. Offered in a range of flavors and price rates, chocolates will make a sweet way to share your message with your audience. Your audience will surely be thrilled at the ideas of beating stress with the classic comfort food of chocolates. 5.Adult Coloring Books Coloring books for adults not just help to beat stress but can also stimulate brain and enhance the coordination skills. Coloring books are loads of fun too. Put your brand on and see how these logo items will make your brand closer to the hearts of your customers in no time. 6.Scented candles Hand out the luxury of a relaxing spa experience right in the cozy confines of their home with these custom scented candles. Offered in various popular flavors, scented candles will soothe the frayed nerves and will help them stay relaxed after a long day. It makes a soothing gift choice during trade shows, or store promotions as your audience will be really pleased to get these popular logo items for sure.
