781-821-0615 Cristina Hajosy Photography Photographing Your life Since 1993 This selection of images combines three photo shoots over a period of three years. First the maternity shoot and finally the arrival of a beautiful baby girl! Cristina’s photographic approach is different for every couple. She focuses her creativity and her craftsmanship on your specific needs. Color, black & white, or both? Traditional formals? Or more photojournalistic candids? By building a working knowledge of your tastes and preferences, Cristina can help you to choose the styles and techniques that she will use to photograph your wedding day. Your wedding pictures will be with you through all your years together. Selecting a photographer who understands how you want to remember your day will ensure that all your memories are picture perfect. “Location Portraiture”, as practiced by Cristina Hajosy removes the portrait from the distractions of unfamiliar surroundings and places it in the comfort of your home or at a special location that you enjoy. Photographing in familiar surroundings creates a relaxed atmosphere which indulges the idiosyncrasies that make each of us who we are. A location that has special significance provides a personal connection, unlike posed studio shots in front of tired backgrounds. Keeping everyone comfortable and at ease, creates fun, interesting and telling portraiture. Using techniques developed over 20 years of professional practice, Cristina combines the tools of the portable studio with natural, available light. The result is a natural image, free of the stiffness of traditional studio portraiture. We invite you to browse the image galleries presented here to see for yourself the magic of location portraiture by Cristina Hajosy. Capturing that special something in each of us is the essence of what Cristina offers. Simply moments in time made timeless…
