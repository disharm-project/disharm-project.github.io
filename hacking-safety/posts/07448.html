The one that made a belly landing near Beauraing in Belgium on February 20th, 1945 (pilot: Young and waist gunner Roy) bears serial number 44 6583. The crash did not take place in Florennes or near this airfield. This Flying Fortress is not the one with serial number 44 8179 “aka Yankee Wolf”. Sorry if my short comment is not correct.
