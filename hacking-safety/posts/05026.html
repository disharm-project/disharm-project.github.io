Hello, @Rainwalker. I appreciate trying the factory reset; once you logged into the modem, have you set up the WiFi network? And what is the status of the first light from the left side? If the first light from the left is not lit, then it's possible, as you guessed, the wireless portion of the gateway modem is not operational. We can look into it and advise you on the solution.
