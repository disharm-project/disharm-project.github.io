The classic east-facing porch catches the morning light, and the west-facing backyard basks in the afternoon/evening sun when you are home to enjoy it. This home is close to Queen St, in the heart of the bustle of the Beach, and all its amenities. Kew Beach PS, St Denis Catholic, and Glen Ames PS are all within a short walk. The stately beach home commands attention through classic curb appeal, and the backyard yields views as it is perched up with presence and grandeur. This home has all the important check boxes that hold value. The land size and location alone make it a special one. Go shopping, stop for ice cream, strap on rollerblades, and go for a swim. Or, sit and watch the water, or the people, with a cool drink in hand.
