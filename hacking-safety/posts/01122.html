Keeping historic Williamstown beaches cleaner The historic old colonial area of Williamstown exudes charm. 3016Beachpatrol based in Williamstown is there to help out their beaches. Positioned on the corner of the Port Phillip Bay and the mouth of the Yarra River, Williamstown gets more than its fair share of careless littering. We meet once a month for one hour to clean up one of our beaches. We are often joined by other groups with the same goals. Follow us on social media. We look forward to seeing you! If you live in the area and want to contribute to keeping your beaches clean then Sign up with 3016 BeachPatrol to become a member. As a member you will get BeachPatrol and Love Our Street is a volunteer organisation of people who want to keep their local beaches free from rubbish. We also serve to raise awareness of this large problem to other people in the local area with the expectation of educating them about proper rubbish disposal. BeachPatrol Australia acknowledges the Traditional Custodians of the lands where we perform our cleaning, we pay our respects to their elders past, present and emerging, and we look to contribute to caring for country. © Copyright 2024 BeachPatrol Australia Inc.
