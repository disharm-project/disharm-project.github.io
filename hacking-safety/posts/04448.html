In colored outdoor lighting, RGB refers to the three primary colors of light: Red, Green, and Blue. These three colors are combined in various intensities to create a wide range of colors. The RGB color model is based on the additive color mixing principle, where different combinations of red, green, and blue light can produce millions of distinct colors when projected or displayed together – and that is where the nightly illumination magic happens. For those truly interested in the technical science behind it, in RGB lighting systems from Outdoor Lighting Perspectives of Nashville, each color is represented by a value ranging from 0 to 255 for each of the three primary colors. By adjusting the intensity of each color component, you can create any color in the visible spectrum. With our expert guidance and easy-to-use controls, RGB LED permanent eave lights allow for dynamic and customizable color effects, making it popular for creating visually appealing and versatile lighting in an instant. As creative, memorable, and inspiring as the country music melodies that are born here in Nashville, lighting up discerning homes in this area is a natural complement. Our custom-colored roofline lighting predominantly utilizes LED (Light Emitting Diode) technology. LEDs are energy-efficient, long-lasting, and capable of producing vibrant colors. They are also flexible, making it easier to install them along curved or angled rooflines. Color Options And Control Options. Our OLP colored roofline lighting offers a wide range of color options, allowing you to create different effects and atmospheres.
