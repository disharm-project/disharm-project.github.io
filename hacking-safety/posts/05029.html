It's been a while since we drew at Uus Laine, time to pay them a visit again! :) Get ready for 2 ,5 hours of bliss full drawing with our beautiful model, some funky beats and fun people! ♥

All skill levels are welcome! After the drawing session and during the break we encourage you to to share your works with the group! You can bring your own gear. But we also have sketch materials provided by Kunst ja Hobi. There are artboards and molberts to draw on and there is a pile of sketch papers for the forget full minded! DISCLAIMER: This is not an art-class, there is no instructor, but a fun and friendly opportunity for you to draw, with your fellow friends and artists. See you at Uus Laine ! 35 tickets available. Online ticket sale : 8 Euro / Door ticket: 10 Euro cash

𝗜𝗠𝗣𝗢𝗥𝗧𝗔𝗡𝗧: ♥ We aim our events to be covid-free. So if you feel ill, have just a tiny cough or have been in contact with someone sick lately, please be so kind to wear a mask ! Let's keep ourselves and everyone involved safe! ♥♥
