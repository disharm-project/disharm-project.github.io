Open Mon-Sat, 9:30am-5pm | Wheatland Tours Mon-Sat, 10am-3pm

Collection: The Scrapbook Collection Call No. : MG-434, Box 095 Title: Scrapbook of Leaman Place, Paradise, Gordonville and Intercourse, 1911-1916 Dates:1911-1916 Extent: 1 volume Creator: Compiled by Emma Mylin. Repository: LancasterHistory.org (Lancaster, Pa.) Shelving Location: Archives South, Side 22

Description: This volume contains newspaper clippings of obituaries, weddings, high school graduations and local and social news. There are articles about the closing of the Paradise Select School, the 175th anniversary of the congregation at Old Leacock Presbyterian Church in 1915, the retirement of Gordonville Postmaster Amos Hershey, the Liberty Bell train traveling through Lancaster County in 1893 and Witmer’s Bridge. Conditions for Access: Please fill out a call slip for viewing. Conditions Governing Reproductions: May not be photocopied. Please contact Research Staff or Archives Staff with questions. Language: English Source of Acquisition: Gift of Catherine Glass, 4 March 2009. Emma Mylin lived to the age of 99. She was Ms. Glass’ great-aunt. Subjects: Liberty Bell. Old Leacock Presbyterian Church (Pa.) Witmer’s Bridge (Lancaster County, Pa.) Obituaries. Gordonville, Leacock Twp. (Lancaster County, Pa.) Intercourse (Pa.) Lancaster County (Pa.) — History, local.
