Aqua crisps are a new, original snack with your health in mind. This crunchy chip has the look and texture of a pork rind but is completely vegetarian and gluten-free. The unique recipe allows us to introduce a number of flavors. Through our patented process, crispy delicious crisps were born. We are debuting our original flavor. With the combination of vegan bacon powder and other natural spices you will find a satisfying and familiar flavor that is cholesterol free and very low in sodium. What you won't find is something that tastes bland or "too healthy". Aqua crisps are a snack lovers dream come true. Our goal is to sell the dried product to restaurants, hotels, schools, institutions and bars: anywhere with a deep fryer. We have developed a new snack that will universally be enjoyed. The dried product is nonperishable and takes less than a minute to cook. We have spice blends for retail or feel free to invent your own. We can also produce your own unique blend for you.
