It’s Wag Your Tail Wednesday 🐕! I ❤️ my Costa Del Mar Caballito sunglasses! I’ve had these since 2019, and I wear them everywhere. They are perfect for the sand and water. The frames have maximum-coverage to reduce light leak and a small lens front to fit a slightly narrow head. The lenses provide 100% UV protection, premium polarized 508g scratch proof clarity and are blue-mirrored, which is great for harsh sun Unfortunately, Kyle lost his sunglasses a few weeks ago when we were working at the boat. He had them hanging from his shirt and he bent over re-tie our dock lines. Down, down, down they sank very quickly. Ugh! It was pretty frustrating, but we learned that we needed to get glasses straps to prevent that from happening again. Always ready for sand, surf or open water, Costa’s Caballito sunglasses are the perfect companion of the explorer who spends his time jumping bridges into deep, clear water or the beachcomber on the hunt to make a statement at the local’s hideaway. Sporting flexible integral hinges, polarization, a heavy duty nylon frame and Hydrolite™nose pads, these Costa sunglasses are for those always looking to explore unknown waters.
