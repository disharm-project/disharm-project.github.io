Recognizing and preventing harassment in your workplace

theworkcycle.com · https://theworkcycle.com/recognizing-and-preventing-harassment-in-your-workplace/

3 of 38 sentences flagged

While it is important to know what forms of workplace harassment occur, it is equally important to know how to recognize and prevent them. By knowing when you are being harassed or have a problem with your manager or coworkers, you can take action before things get out of hand. Harassment is illegal whether it occurs in the home, on the street, or in a work environment. While the most common types of harassment happen purely verbal and physical, there are also other more severe forms, like sexual and physical harassment. All forms of workplace harassment, including sexual and physical harassment, are illegal and put both an employee and the company at risk, not to mention the legal ramifications of not stopping it. Harassment is something that can be subtle or it can be offensive. For example, if someone makes repeated comments about your appearance, whether they are derogatory or complimenting, or if you are made to feel inferior because of your gender, age, or race, you may have a case. If you are a woman and have been subjected to this type of treatment, you may have a case. Men who experience this kind of behavior may also have a case. The first step in fighting back against harassment is to document everything. Keeping track of what is happening and report it to your manager will help you get the help you need when you feel like you are being harassed or are in danger of being harassed. This may even mean that you can file a complaint against a co-worker, especially if you are in a situation where you have had previous complaints lodged against you. This can be the beginning of a case against your employer. In addition, documenting your complaints and your concerns can be helpful for future issues with your employer and/or business. If, for instance, your co-worker was accused of sexual harassment, you can use your report to prove that they did not commit this type of sexual act. Your documentation can also be useful in getting a raise, promotion, or other benefit at your place of employment. The more you know about what types of complaints are valid and what is not, the more likely you are to be able to defend yourself when it comes to your employer or company. When you have decided that you are experiencing harassment and you know what has taken place, you have to address the behavior in the business’ culture. by speaking to your manager about what has occurred. Even if the offending behavior is considered acceptable in the business community, your manager should not tolerate it anymore. Harassment has to stop right now. Whether you are the victim or the perpetrator, you have to speak up now. Whether you have experienced verbal or physical harassment, you need to report it and put a stop to it. You have to stop the problem now before the situation escalates, or before the harassment gets worse. Being aware of your rights in this situation and taking steps to protect yourself is one way that you can be sure that your business or company is in a safe environment. It’s important to understand what harassment is, but it is also important to know what is appropriate in your business and what isn’t. Harassment is not always a bad thing, but it does have to end. This is an opportunity to take a stand on behalf of yourself and your company and make sure that you are doing everything that you can to prevent future instances from occurring. If you have to leave your business or industry because of it, then you owe it to yourself to make sure that your company is free from any further abuse or harassment. You should be happy about the fact that there is good news on the horizon for victims of harassment. Because of the advances in technology, there are now a number of tools available to help you put a stop to workplace harassment in your workplace. There is now a wide variety of reporting and legal services that can help you handle your case from the comfort of your home. With the resources that these services offer, you no longer have to put up with workplace harassment. These services can make sure that you are safe in your workplace so you can return to your work life without any further stress or worry. Don’t let workplace harassment take over your life, and don’t wait for it to get out of hand.
