Posted: 01-Aug-24 Location: Manitowoc, Wisconsin Type: Full Time Salary: $125,000-$157,000 Categories: Years of Experience: MPU is seeking Power Plant Manager applicants with a strong background in power plant operations. The primary responsibility of this role is the ability to implement strategic initiatives and to achieve desired results aligned with the organizational mission, vision, values, and fundamental areas of focus through a collaborative role on the Leadership Team. This role is responsible for electric power and steam production through proper operation and maintenance of the electrical power plant. Supervision is exercised over production and maintenance activities within the power plant. The Power Plant Manager is also responsible for maintaining maximum operating efficiency of personnel and equipment in the power production plant. Examples of Duties and Responsibilities: Communicate and support organizational positions and goals with employees. Collaboratively perform strategic planning. Direct the operation and maintenance of all electrical production facilities. Oversee operation of the power plant and facilities within regulatory requirements. Support and promote a strong power plant safety culture. Sample of Required Knowledge, Skills and Abilities: Establish and maintain priorities, understand goals, implement action plans, and produce results. Work effectively in a collaborative team environment. Supervisory skills with an Everyday Leadership approach. Possess a growth mindset with a curiosity to learn. Provide coaching and development to team. Pay Rate: $125,000-$157,000 annually MPU is fortunate to serve our community by providing valuable services to our customers. Please reach out to jobs@mpu.org to request a full job description. About Manitowoc Public Utilities

Manitowoc Public Utilities (MPU) is a community-owned electric, water, and wastewater treatment utility serving approximately 18,000 accounts in the City of Manitowoc, Wisconsin and employing about 115 full time employees. During the last century, MPU has recognized its responsibility to provide reliable service, safe drinking water, and environmental stewardship – all at a competitive cost. MPU will continue to build on this foundation in the future with our vision to be a thriving and innovative regional utility service provider that exceeds customer expectations.
