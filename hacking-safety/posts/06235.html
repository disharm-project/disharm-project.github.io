Mottcell batteries to solve the disadvantages of the solar energy industry 159832

2023-07-25

One of the most obvious defects of solar energy is that it cannot generate electricity without the sun. Now, Tesla (Tesla), BYD (002594, stock) (BD) and Bosch (Bosch) and many other companies have launched a new generation of lithium-ion storage systems. It is similar to electric car batteries, except with electricity generated by residential solar systems. It's like putting a large battery in your home and storing the electricity from your rooftop solar system in it for rainy days. The advantage of this system is that it can power your home with lighting and appliances for 1-2 days. It can also be controlled when using electricity. In areas with floating electricity prices, where prices are higher during peak periods, the average household can store solar power during valley periods and use it during peak periods, like a hot afternoon when air conditioning is buzzing. A large number of solar cells can help power companies integrate renewable energy into the grid. Solar energy is intermittent, which means it will suddenly flood into the grid and suddenly disappear, causing power instability. Storing the solar power eliminates such fluctuations. In October, California regulators asked power companies to increase their renewable power storage capacity to 1.3 gigawatts by 2020, greatly boosting the development of solar power storage.
