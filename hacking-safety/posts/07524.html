Rope lab winter ball

heyplura.com · https://heyplura.com/events/rope-lab-winter-ball

5 of 30 sentences flagged

Create a kinky Winter Wonderland with us! Winter-themed scenes and costumes are admired but not required – think blues, whites, icicles, frost, warm hats, ice royalty, snow sprites, and so on! We'll provide the space, hardpoints, mood lighting, a fantastic house photographer, and music; you bring your bondage supplies and lovely presence. Photography is encouraged at this event – with the consent of everyone in the photo, of course. In addition to a spacious tying area on the sprung dance floor, there is social space and excellent viewing in the raised seating area. This is a laid-back and friendly event focused on rope play. While it is not a formal educational environment, we will have knowledgeable folks to answer questions. We welcome your bondage "lab time" experiments! While coming with a partner is not required, please be aware that most attendees arrive in pairs or groupings or with tying plans already made. If you're looking for pick-up play, this event is likely not the best fit. You are welcome to come and watch! The Bloom chat can be a great way to connect with folks ahead of the event. The event is from 8pm - 11pm. Please plan to arrive before 9pm. Our full photo policy can be viewed here. This event is for adults age 18+, ID required. All attendees must abide by our code of conduct, event guidelines, and photo policy. Please also read our release form, which attendees must sign at the door. Please carefully read our COVID guidelines to determine whether attending this event is within your risk profile. Venue details: Capacity is limited to ensure space and sufficient hardpoints. Little Boxes is large and airy, with 2-story ceilings and square footage capacity far exceeding our attendance cap. While the venue provides some mats, supplies are limited. If having a mat is crucial to you, we recommend bringing one. We hang a few pieces of bamboo, but again, if this is critical to you, we recommend bringing your own. Attire: Anything from nudity to sweats to elaborate costumes are welcome! The temperature in the space can vary; we recommend bringing layers. The entry fee goes to pay for the space, compensate our photographer, and cover expenses for this and other Twisted Windows events. The fee is fully refundable up to 5 days before the event using the link at the bottom of the order email. Within five days of the event, tickets are transferrable but not refundable. Please contact us to inquire about reduced price or free entry, accessibility, or any other event questions.
