France Télévisions – Sports Editorial Published on 07/31/2024 5:40 p.m. Updated on 07/31/2024 7:17 p.m. The French handball team during its group match against Norway, on July 29, 2024 at the Paris Olympic Games. (LAURIN AMELIE / KMSP / AFP) Beaten by Denmark and Norway, the French men’s handball team is aiming for its first victory in the competition on Wednesday. What there is to know Finally launch its Olympic Games. Largely dominated by Denmark (37-29) on Saturday, then by Norway (27-22) on Monday, the French team must absolutely find the Olympic recipe if it wishes to retain its title. “A bit with their backs to the wall”. After the defeat against Norway, Guillaume Gille felt that his Blues were starting to enter “more and more into a form of emergency”. The one who voluntarily chose the toughest group to avoid meeting these opponents too early in the elimination phase is perhaps losing his bet. Qualification still within reach of the Blues. France is still far from being eliminated. With four teams qualified in each group (of six teams), the Tricolores still have their destiny in their hands, but no longer have the right to make a mistake. They must make a clean sheet and hope for a misstep from Norway to take back second place from the Scandinavians and face a more affordable opponent in the quarter-finals. Reassure yourself about your game. The threat of elimination averted for the moment, the most worrying thing for the Blues remains the level displayed both offensively and defensively.
