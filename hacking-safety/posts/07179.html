ARTS 249 - Digital Artmaking (2,2) 3 CreditsPrerequisite(s): ARTS 105 - Two-Dimensional Design & ARTS 110 - Basic Drawing I .Students will explore creative expression in art beyond the traditional media of painting, drawing, photography and sculpture by using digital technology. Students will combine traditional artmaking with the use of digital imaging software applications and printing techniques. Emphasis is on creative thinking and problem-solving. Students will also explore theory, history and ethics involved with the computer as an art tool. Students must purchase some art supplies. Offered every other year in even numbered years.
