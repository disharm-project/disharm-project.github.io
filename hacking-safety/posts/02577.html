Washington News Today

Sharing is caring! For a long time, we’ve been forced to watch as Biden’s made a fool of himself. All along the campaign trail, he made gaffe after gaffe, forcing his handlers to hide him most of the time. Once in office, they did their best to keep him away from the limelight. But he’s the “president,” right? And with approval numbers as bad as his, they can’t keep him hidden away forever. So, they trolled out this man for an event with a few CEOs. It started with him making little sense. From YouTube: President Joe Biden said he’s “looking for a job,” during his meeting with CEOs at the White House on 1/26/2022. We agree with you, Joe, you need to get another job or just retire. But it only got worse from there, when he mumbled about driving a truck. From YouTube: President Joe Biden said, “driving that, eh, you know, uh, was up there, uh……I don’t know, man,” during his meeting with CEOs at the White House on 1/26/2022. Then, there was some serious confusion after Biden called on a CEO, then interrupted him. From YouTube: A confused President Joe Biden interrupted a CEO after calling on him and did not know who was supposed to be speaking, during his meeting with CEOs at the White House on 1/26/2022. Yikes. How hard can you facepalm after watching a video? This was only a sampling of what went on during a recent event between Biden and a few CEOs. It’s pretty common for a president to meet with important leaders in various industries. Biden has done very few. Probably because his policies have been destroying the U.S. economy. This event seemed to be about “climate change,” so there was little discussion about helping Americans get back to work and earn more money. How ridiculous! But this event was exposed for the farce that it was the moment Biden opened his mouth. He seemed confused, disinterested, and incapable of communicating. We don’t have to wonder why Biden’s handlers work so hard to keep him out of public view. Many critics have long suggested Biden isn’t well. Some have even claimed he shows symptoms of mental decline. Democrats have denied this, yet they refuse to provide us with a cognitive test. Meanwhile, Americans are forced to watch this tired old man stumbling around the White House. And wonder whether our nation can survive a leader this mentally incompetent. How many more videos do we have to show before Congress gets its act together? Does Biden need to totally come apart at the seams before we get some answers? Source: YouTube, YouTube, YouTube
