Your derriere, like other areas of the body, can change over time, leaving you feeling dissatisfied with your appearance, losing firmness and shape – a natural result of a variety of factors such as age, pregnancy, weight fluctuations, menopause, and gravity. If your shape has become noticeably reduced or otherwise appears less than flattering, Spa Imperial offers a non-surgical butt lift treatment option to restore your perkier bum so you can confidently flaunt your favorite jeans again. One procedure that can add fullness and shape to the backside without anesthesia or incisions is known as the non-surgical buttock lift. This procedure uses the injectable dermal filler Sculptra to round out the buttocks with limited pain and downtime. Sculptra contains poly-L-lactic acid, which works differently in the body than standard hyaluronic acid dermal fillers like Juvederm, not adding volume right away, but rather stimulating the body’s own production of collagen to produce youthful volume naturally over time. While the improvements take time to develop, the results also last longer, with most patients enjoying their non-surgical buttock lift for up to three years or more. But the benefits go beyond more volume: the stimulation of collagen in the deeper dermal structure also softens and rejuvenates the skin on the outside, creating a smoother, more attractive contour. You may also see a reduction in the appearance of cellulite as the collagen production thickens the skin and minimizes the dimpled look. You might consider a non-surgical buttock lift if you would like to rejuvenate your backside but don’t want to undergo surgery and lack sufficient fat for a Brazilian Butt Lift.
