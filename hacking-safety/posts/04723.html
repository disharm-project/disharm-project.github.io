Request Appointment What is skin tightening? Beauty Scripts’ dermatologists adopt non-surgical laser skin tightening treatments to target a specific area of the face or body and firm the skin. Laser skin tightening procedures use single-wavelength light energy directed on in the dermis, starting the production of new collagen. As the collagen production boosts, the skin and damaged cells start regenerating, revealing more firm and tighter skin. Some variants of these treatments can also help smooth out the appearance of cellulite. How does skin tightening work? Beauty Scripts’ skin tightening treatment is an FDA-approved, non-invasive procedure that uses highly sophisticated technology and clinically tested products to tighten the lax skin on the face and body. Our laser treatment functions by directing concentrated heat energy on lax or damaged skin. As the tissue is heated, the dermis and connective tissue experience some wounds that are wired to start healing immediately, revealing plumper and smoother skin with rejuvenated skin tissues underneath. How is a non-surgical skin tightening better? Among all the surgical and non-surgical skin tightening treatments, Beauty Scripts’ laser skin tightening is a safe and non-invasive procedure that has multiple benefits. Primarily, the biggest advantage of the non-invasive treatment is the minimal to zero downtime for recovery needed. Additionally, these treatments are safe to be performed on virtually every area of the body without leaving any surgery or scars. Moreover, the results are almost instantaneous with a noticeable “lifting” effect that can last for up to 1 year. Who is an ideal candidate for skin tightening? Laser skin tightening treatment is ideal for individuals who have mild to moderate skin laxity and do not require any drastic measures such as tummy tuck surgery or liposuction. Hence, anyone on their weight loss journey or women experiencing saggy skin postpartum can consult with Beauty Script’s dermatologists regarding this treatment. However, it is suggested that pregnant women should postpone this procedure until their delivery. How much does the treatment cost?
