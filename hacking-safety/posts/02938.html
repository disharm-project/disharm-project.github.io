Slim Line LED 'Enhanced Brightness' Round Courtesy Lamp - White LED - Stainless Steel Bezel - 12V Attractive, energy efficient lighting for interior and exterior use. Hella Marine round LED courtesy lamps can be installed almost anywhere on board. Over 3 x brighter than regular Soft Light White and Warm White LED courtesy lamps. Advanced lens technology provides a cool and even spread of soft light without glare or eye strain. Hella Marine LED courtesy lamps consume a fraction of the energy of traditional bulb lamps and run very cool due to their low current draw of less than 0.5W (less than 0.04A @ 12V). This provides safer installation into light weight materials where the heat of incandescent lamps may cause damage to the surrounding structure. Each lamp is completely sealed, highly shock, impact and vibration resistant, perfectly suited for the demanding marine environment. Enhanced brightness lamps include a surface mounting ring with 3 x stainless steel screws. Stainless steel and white rim lamps include a white spacer, black rim lamps a black spacer.
