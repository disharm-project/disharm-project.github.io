Thank you, in advance, for completing this application to connect with A Little Help. Our volunteers support older adults ages 55+ in Denver, Jefferson, Larimer, Mesa, and parts of Douglas, Arapahoe, Adams and Broomfield Counties. If you are unsure of the types of help our volunteers can offer, please take a look at our how we help page.
