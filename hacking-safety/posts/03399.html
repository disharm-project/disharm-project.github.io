Home All News WhatsApp Channel Contact Events Shop More A Milton man has been arrested after a sexual assault investigation. On Wednesday, May 29th, the Halton Regional Police Service (HRPS) arrested 26-year-old Avery Hill. He is charged with: Sexual Assault Assault Causing Bodily Harm Assault Forcible Confinement Hill is in custody awaiting a bail hearing in Milton. The female victim met Hill through a dating app. Police think there may be other victims. If you have information about this case, contact Detective Constable Angela Dahdaly at 905-825-4777 ext. 8790. Anonymous tips can be given to Crime Stoppers at 1-800-222-8477 (TIPS) or online at www.haltoncrimestoppers.ca. Sexual assault includes any unwanted sexual touching. It ranges from unwanted touching to assault involving penetration. There is no time limit for reporting a sexual assault. You can report it to the police no matter how long ago it happened. Victims of violence or sexual assault can contact the Halton Regional Police Service Victim Services Unit at 905-825-4777 for support.
