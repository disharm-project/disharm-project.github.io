The Department of Anthropology at The University of Alabama has a diverse research program in the following specialties: Learn more about current research projects in the department by visiting the Facilities & Labs page, or browse our directory. Biocultural Medical Anthropology, a specific approach within the more general subfield of medical anthropology, strives to understand why people grow and develop as they do and why they may be at risk for health problems.
