In today’s digital world, consumers are bombarded with a constant stream of information, making it difficult for businesses to grab their attention and stand out from the competition. Here are just a few reasons why: 1. Video is more engaging than text. The human brain processes visuals 60,000 times faster than text, making video an incredibly effective way to grab attention and hold it. 2. Video is more shareable than text. Social media users are more likely to share a video than a blog post or article, which means that videos have the potential to reach a much wider audience. 3. Video is more memorable than text. Research has shown that people are more likely to remember information presented in a video than information presented in text. This means that a well-crafted video can help your business stay top of mind with consumers. 4. Video is more versatile than text. Videos can be used in a variety of ways, from product demonstrations and customer testimonials to explainer videos and thought leadership content. This makes them a versatile tool for reaching consumers at different stages of the buying journey. Overall, the power of video for B2B demand generation cannot be ignored.
