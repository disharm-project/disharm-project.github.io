25 alternative names for grandma and grandpa

www.familyeducation.com · https://www.familyeducation.com/family-life/relationships/grandparents/25-alternative-names-for-grandma-and-grandpa

1 of 42 sentences flagged

Whether grandparents want to choose their own name or wait for their grandchildren to choose what to call them, the options are endless. Some families have traditional names that are passed from generation to generation, others are looking for something new and trendy; whichever the reason, the options are endless. Some of the best grandparent nicknames come from grandkids’ inadvertent mispronunciations, like Bumpa or Grum, creating a whole new name that often sticks. Picking the perfect name doesn’t have to be stressful and can actually be fun. You can try out many different options from formal to trendy or traditional to wacky. Related: 12 Great Activities for Grandparents and Grandchildren Below you will find a list of 25 alternative names for Grandma and Grandpa, a mix of many different backgrounds and formality. Some come in pairs, but that doesn’t mean that they need to, feel free to mix and match based on what feels right. You never know what will stick! Choosing the perfect name for grandparents can be a delightful yet challenging task for expecting parents and grandparents alike. It's a title that will carry a lot of love, memories, and legacy. Whether you're looking for something traditional, modern, or uniquely whimsical, our list aims to inspire the perfect choice for those special individuals in your child's life! Rutger Bruining, FamilyEducation’s Family History and Heritage Expert and CEO of StoryTerrace, gives us a little insight into family history with names for grandparents. [1] He says, “Having documented the stories of countless grandparents through StoryTerrace, one thing we have learned is that nicknames for grandparents often stem from events or things that happened in their past. The interesting thing is that many people never realize this because they don’t know their grandparents' history before they were parents or grandparents. The names and stories behind them are as varied as every family. Each and every one of these unique names we give our grandparents will have a story behind it which adds to its importance to every family. Discussing this is a great way to connect with family members and understand more about their lives.” Yes, a grandmother can definitely be called Gigi! While the most common grandparent nicknames are Grandma and Grandpa, there are many other options that have become popular in recent years. One of these is Gigi, which has a sweet and playful sound to it. Some people may wonder where the name Gigi originated from. It is believed to be derived from the French word "grandmère" which means grandmother. It is also possible that Gigi came from the nickname for the name Virginia, as many grandmothers are given this name. If you're looking for a groovy and unique nickname for grandma, consider calling her Moonbeam, Granola, or Sunshine. These whimsical names exude a free-spirited vibe that perfectly captures the essence of a hip and trendy grandmother. Embrace the hippie culture with this cheerful and unconventional moniker for your beloved grandma! Interested in family history and genealogy?
