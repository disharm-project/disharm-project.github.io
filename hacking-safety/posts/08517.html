Digital advertising

www.kulturedigital.com · https://www.kulturedigital.com/digital-advertising/

1 of 50 sentences flagged

A well-built pay-per-click (PPC) campaign is the BEST way to ensure that your website (or landing page) is showing up at the top of the search results. By using available data to target the most valuable keywords, your brand, product, or service will stay competitive. The main benefit of PPC advertising is that you only pay when someone actually clicks. Imagine if you only paid for the people who actually read your newspaper ad or contacted you after hearing your radio spot. In other words, digital advertising just makes more sense. Consistent branding can elevate a brand to the next level. A fantastic digital campaign bringing potential customers to a perfectly designed website with a killer video has exponentially more power when combined. If you remove one of those pieces, the branding falters. When you get all the facets working together, it’s far more valuable than the sum of its parts. Digital advertising is a key piece to the digital puzzle. In many cases, a digital campaign is the first step to driving conversions and sales. Are you ready to find those potential customers online with targeted and adaptable campaigns? A study done by Nielsen Catalina Solutions, a leader in measuring advertising performance, found that the sales return on a targeted campaign ranges from 3 to 5 times the investment. These findings show that by correctly leveraging the available data, a well-built digital advertising campaign yields amazing return on investment. One of the greatest strengths of digital marketing is the amazing ability to measure the results. You know how many people clicked, opened, referred, shared, and so much more. When a digital advertising campaign is correctly connected to your website, you also have the ability to track the entire journey. This way you can see which campaigns are working and which need to be updated. Firstly, imagine if you had a billboard and could change the message for each vehicle that drove past. Secondly, imagine that you could turn it off for some people who you didn’t think were potential customers. Finally, and most importantly, imagine only having to pay when someone actually read your message AND followed through by contacting you. That’s the power of digital advertising. EVERYONE is online. Do you want proof? Where are you RIGHT NOW? Next time you’re out in a crowd, look around and see how many people are looking at their phones. They are most likely on social media, looking up reviews, or watching videos. Your potential customers are already there, and so is your competition, so using online advertising is vital to your success. Having the ability to track every digital campaign gives you amazing power. You are able to make changes and tweaks on the fly. Instant updates can be made to adjust each ad depending on the data coming in. This flexibility allows you to stay in front of your potential customers and is unparalleled in any other advertising medium. Your business has a story to tell, and there is no better way to tell that story than with a phenomenal video. Video is the best way to get people to connect to your company on a personal level. Your website is the foundation of your online marketing activities. If you look at all the marketing channels and techniques, what they actually do is bring visitors to your website. Make it good. Digital advertising with both text and display ads is the most cost effective way to reach your audience. Since you only pay per click, your budget goes a lot further than it would with traditional ads. There are 8 main types of digital advertising: display ads, remarketing, search marketing, email marketing, social media ads, video ads, mobile ads, and native advertising. Digital advertising is important because it allows you to reach customers wherever they are: at home, at work, at play, and while traveling. Virtually everyone is online and you can only reach them via digital marketing. Digital advertising finds potential customers on social media, Google searches, YouTube, and email. The difference is the medium in which you find potential customers. In traditional marketing, it's via television, billboards, magazines, and newspapers. With digital marketing, it's via websites, social media apps, and video. With traditional marketing, you spend money on billboards, radio spots, television commercials, and print ads. You then sit back and hope that it all works and drives new business. With digital marketing, everything is trackable, therefore more cost effective. You can see what's working in real time and spend accordingly.
