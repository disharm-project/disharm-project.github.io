Unite with other professionals passionate about improving patient care, public awareness and the professional standing of optometry. Explore MOA/AOA membership opportunities supporting all areas of optometry. As a member of the Minnesota Optometric Association (MOA) you will be able to take advantage of all that membership has to offer, including: Free Subscription to Weekly E-Newsletter — provides you with innovative ideas and insightful articles and upcoming events. AOA Membership — Your membership in the MOA also gives you an automatic membership in the American Optometric Association (AOA). Practice Opportunities — The MOA maintains a list of practice opportunities, which is housed on this web site in the career center. As a member you can have ads placed on the website for free. Legislative Advocacy — The MOA serves as your eyes and ears by retaining lobbyists that maintain an active presence at the capitol in St. Paul, and helping volunteers to coordinate grassroots legislative contacts. Through promotion of proactive legislation and reaction to other legislation introduced, the MOA's goal is to create an environment that allows optometrists to practice to the highest level of optometric education with the restrictions on patient care. Health Plan Advocacy — Just as with legislative advocacy, the MOA maintains constant communication with the major health plans in the state. The goal is to create an environment that allows optometry to practice to the highest level of optometric education with the fewest restrictions on patient care. Education with Discounted Registration — The MOA is committed to offering the latest and highest quality continuing education in the region and nation. All of our educational offerings bring in regional and national experts to make sure Minnesota O.D.s are ahead of the curve with the latest clinical information. MOA members received discounted registration at all MOA-sponsored education events Credit Card Processing — The MOA has a partnership with Wind River Financial who provide a great credit card processing service at a reasonable price special to members of the MOA. Office Supplies discount program — Through an association program with Office Max/Office Depot members get a discount on office supply purchases both online or in the store.
