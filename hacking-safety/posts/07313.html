Welcome to my practice, where I specialize in providing supportive and affirming psychotherapy for ADHD neurotypes. If you've been experiencing challenges in your work or relationships, you're not alone. Many of my clients seek support in navigating these areas and are eager to cultivate new perspectives and skills. Your brain is a remarkable beauty and inherently whole just as it is. However, it's undeniable that the world around us often fails to fully accept and accommodate our unique strengths and vulnerabilities. Letting go of 'neurotypical' standards for time management, goal setting, and self-care while adopting ADHD suited ones can feel like a daunting task, but it's also an opportunity for profound growth. Embracing approaches tailored to your neurotype isn't just about transforming your present circumstances; it's about acknowledging and addressing any lingering wounds from the past. As your therapist, I am committed to guiding you through this journey of self-discovery and healing. Together, we'll explore the patterns that may be holding you back and uncover the possibilities that exist in the here and now. With a focus on self-awareness and insight, our therapeutic work will move towards empowering you to make new choices and find as much closure as possible with unfinished business from your past. The path of personal growth and fulfillment is uniquely yours, and I'm here to support you every step of the way. *My practice is committed to cultural humility, lgbtqia+ affirmation, and offering a safe space for immigrants.
