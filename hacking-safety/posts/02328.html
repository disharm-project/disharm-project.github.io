3. Hershel Greene The Greene family patriarch was, well, kind of a dick in the comics. While extremely religious, he swore constantly and always seemed to have a chip on his shoulder. In both mediums, he was killed by the Governor at the prison, but it was a moment that definitely had a much bigger impact on the TV series. A part of why Hershel was so great on the show was that his character adapted many of the qualities of the comic’s version of Dale, such as being a wise, kind-hearted and, eventually, one-legged. After saving the lives of many beloved characters, including Carl and Glenn, Hershel became a fan favorite for many viewers.
