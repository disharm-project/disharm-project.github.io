Recovery Corner, LLC’s mission is to provide men with a safe, clean sober living environment. Allowing Residents, the opportunity to develop life skills in a structured environment giving them the resources and tools required in their new journey of sobriety. Men must be actively working a 12-step program, have a sponsor, be clean and sober and adhere to all house rules. Recovery Corner, LLC is the last step for men to transition into society while residing in a structured sober living environment, taking it one day at a time. Recovery Corner, LLC’s vision is that any man working a 12- step program, who seeks a clean and sober life is provided a safe place to live. A stepping stone while transitioning into becoming a productive member of society free from active addiction. Recovery Corner does not discriminate against race, national origin, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, or age.
