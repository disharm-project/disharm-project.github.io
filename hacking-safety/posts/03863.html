Introducing the official Kansas City Chiefs Kamado Grill Cover by FANMATS, the ultimate accessory for every fan and grill master! This high-quality grill cover not only protects your beloved grill but also proudly displays the official Chiefs' logo, making it a must-have for game day gatherings and cookouts. It also makes a great gift for any football fan. Measuring 27 inches in diameter and 40 inches in height, this grill cover is designed to fit most Kamado-style grills. For added convenience, this grill cover features a velcro strap which holds the cover snugly against your grill, preventing it from being blown away by the wind. With this official Chiefs grill cover you can combine your love for grilling and your passion for the Chiefs, all while ensuring your grill stays protected and ready to cook up a winning feast on game day.
