Police fc consolidate top spot with victory over karungi

swiftsportsug.com · https://swiftsportsug.com/2023/10/23/police-fc-consolidate-top-spot-with-victory-over-karungi/

1 of 11 sentences flagged

Gladys ZAWEDDE Isaac Ogwang and super substitute Herman Wasswa scored for Simon Peter Mugerwa’s men consolidating themselves on top of the table. Ogwang put the Cops in the lead as early as the eighth minute off captain Samuel Kayongo’s well delivered cross. Wasswa came in for Ogwang in the 82nd minute only to score in the last minute of the game off Kayongo’s delivery. The win did not come on a silver platter despite a flattering scoreline as echoed by Police FC coach Simon Mugerwa. “I would like to appreciate my players for the great performance. Kaaro Karungi were resistant but we remained composed which resulted in scoring the goals we wanted”. “A great performance it has been as we maintain our position on top of the table.” Police remains unbeaten and they top the table with 13 points from five games They will visit Booma FC next on Monday 30th at Masindi Stadium. Kaaro Karungi’s next opponent will be Blacks Power on Sunday 29th October at St George’s Arena in Ibanda.
