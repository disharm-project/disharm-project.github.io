Our theme for Faith Formation 2024-2025 is “Blessed.” We'll explore the Beatitudes, which are found in the Gospels of Matthew and Luke. We’ll begin each monthly Faith Formation gathering by attending the 9 a.m. Sunday Mass together as a group, then moving to the school gym for a delicious breakfast and more faith-enrichment activities. We’ll also have monthly resources to send home to help families practice their faith (please see below!). Everyone is invited to these monthly “intergenerational” gatherings: children, parents, siblings, friends, grandparents, godparents, aunts, uncles, cousins, parishioners, visitors, you name it ... all are truly welcome! Please note: All participating children/youth must be accompanied by at least one parent/guardian/adult who is registered for the program and is on site during sessions. Faith Formation registration is $50 per family. You can pay the registration fee in one of three ways: 1) make an online payment through Vanco (scroll down to "Faith Formation Registration"); 2) write a check (made out to "Blessed Sacrament Church") and mail it to Blessed Sacrament Church, c/o Faith Formation & Youth Ministry, 607 Central Ave., Albany, NY, 12206; or 3) drop off your payment in person at Blessed Sacrament's church office or bring it to our first Faith Formation session in September. Please know that financial assistance is available if needed. If cost is an issue, contact our Faith Formation office at faithformationbsc@gmail.org or (518) 438-5854, ext. 119. Payment arrangements will always remain private and confidential. Rev. Daniel Quinn, Pastor Rev. Stephen Yusko, Parochial Vicar ​ Lisanne V.
