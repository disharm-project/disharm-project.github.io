At McLean Funeral Directors, we are always finding ways to provide superior service to those we serve. That is why we’ve created our own service desk to assist you and your family. We have developed relationships with local hotels, restaurants, florists, car services and other companies. From making a reservation at a local restaurant after the visitation or memorial service to assisting with picking up a family member at the airport or ordering a special bouquet of flowers, we are here to help. At your service is our in-house service staff; generally available to assist you with your special needs Monday through Friday from 8:30 to 5:00 PM.You can call us at 1 800-217-9901 and discuss your needs in advance. For your convenience, we have also provided on-line access to a number of services that you might find helpful. By using these links, you can make hotel reservations, rent a car, order flowers and get directions to churches and the funeral homes -all from our website.
