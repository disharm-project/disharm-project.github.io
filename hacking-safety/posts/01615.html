Group rides

Are you ready to join a group of fun and passionate cyclists? Come ride with us! We love getting together as a group and hitting the local trails every Wednesday night at 6 pm (weather and trail conditions depending). Our group loves exploring new trails, so we change our route weekly. Check out our calendar or follow us on Facebook to see where we'll be riding next. You never know where we'll end up, but one thing's for sure - it'll be a blast! Whether you're a seasoned pro or starting, we welcome all abilities to our group. We split into several groups based on fitness and abilities so everyone can enjoy the ride at their own pace. After the ride, we usually like to grab a bite to eat and hang out. It's a great way to get to know your fellow riders and enjoy some post-ride relaxation. Join us and make some new friends while having fun on two wheels!
