How to stop topping the golf ball

We've all watched our tee shot dribble along the ground barely getting past the tee boxes. But Why? See the Video Fix everyone is talking about! You step up to the first tee feeling confident. But then you watch your tee shot dribble along the ground barely making it past the tee boxes. How embarrassing! Do you find yourself constantly topping the golf ball? Or maybe you're thinning the golf ball more often than you'd like? If so, then this blog post is for you! We'll explore some of the most common reasons why golfers top the ball, as well as some tips and tricks for how to correct this annoying problem. So let's get started! There are a few reasons why golfers tend to top the ball. Let's take a look at some of the most common ones: 1. Poor Posture: One of the most common causes of topping the ball is bad posture. When you slouch over too much or you're squatting, it throws off your balance and makes it difficult to make solid contact with the ball. You can also have too much knee flex. Instead, you want to create the right spine angle at address and maintain it throughout your swing. If you want help maintaining your spine angle then check out the best golf alignment sticks. It's the one training aid in almost every pros bag. 2. Reaching for the Ball: Another common cause of topping the ball is standing too far away from it. When you take your stance, you want to make sure that you're not too close or too far from the ball. If you're too far away, you'll end up reaching for the ball resulting in that dreaded topped or thin ball. 3. Poor Balance: As mentioned above, poor balance is another major contributing factor to topping the golf ball. When your weight is shifted too far forward or backward, it throws off your whole swing and makes it difficult to make solid contact with the ball. Instead, you want to make sure that your weight is evenly distributed throughout your body so that you can maintain your balance throughout your swing. Think about holding your finish through the downswing until you see your shot land. 4. Lifting your Head: You're so anxious to see your shot that you lift your head before impact. Think about staying low through impact and the release of the club. A golf impact bag can help you create the feeling of solid ball striking. 5. Gripping the Club too Tightly: Many golfers grip their clubs too tightly, which can lead to all sorts of problems, including topping the ball. When you grip the club too tightly, it causes you to tense up your arms and limits your range of motion, making it difficult to make a smooth swing. Instead, you want to grip the club lightly but firmly so that you can maintain a relaxed grip while still maintaining control over your shot. 6. Lack of Arm Extension: Yet another cause of topping the golf ball is lack of arm extension - commonly referred to as the "chicken wing." This happens when your arms collapse during the swing, causing you to lift up through impact. The result is a loss of power and accuracy and a topped ball. To correct this, you need to make sure that you extend your arms fully during your swing and follow through after impact. You want to feel like you're chasing the ball down the line through impact. We've explained why you top the golf ball and given you some tips on how to eliminate the problem. But a video is worth a thousand words, so check this out. If you find yourself constantly topping or thinning the golf ball, don't worry - you're not alone! These are common issues that plague many golfers. But by taking a closer look at your posture, grip, and overall swing mechanics, you can start making corrections and see improvement in your game in no time!
