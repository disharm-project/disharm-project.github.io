iPhone 7 & 7 Plus release date – The entire world is flooded with the rumors about the next iPhone release which may take place in September or October this year. The new iPhone could either be called iPhone 6S/6S plus or iPhone 7. If the world most popular tech company decides to release two iPhone together, we may expect the announcement of iPhone 7 and 7 Plus together in Q4 of this year. However the other rumors suggest that, the tech giant will come up with iPhone 6S and 6S plus instead of iPhone 7. There is no clue about the what the new iPhone will be called but it is assured that whatever it may be called, the new iPhone will be a super advanced phone having ultra fast processing speed and tons of new features. There is more chances that Apple Inc will shift to next number ( iPhone 7) and the new iPhone could be called iPhone 7. Based upon the rumors and the trend that the techie has been following, I expect that iPhone 7 & 7 Plus release date will be someday between September to second week of October. The company always plans release of new iPhone prior to festive season to maximise the sales. The people buys different stuff for Christmas and the New year that the company always try to take advantage of. The sales figure of iPhone and other idevices skyrocket during this time and the company makes big profit. The other possible reason for iPhone 7 & 7 Plus release date to fall between Sep-Oct is trend. If we notice the trend of release of last few iPhone, the company prefers to keep release dates during these months only. Given below is the release dates of all the iPhone starting with first one. Based upon the above release dates, it can be predicted the iPhone 7 & 7 Plus release date may fall in Q4 of this year if the company doesn’t come up with other variants of iPhone 6 this year. As long as the specification of iPhone 7 and iPhone 7 Plus is concern, there will not be any major different. The only difference between these two variants will be the size. As seen with iPhone 6 and 6 Plus, the iPhone 7 Plus will be slightly larger in size as compared to iPhone 7. The size of iPhone 7 Plus could go upto 5.8 inch however the KGI securities analyst Ming-Chi Kou believes that the company will stick to 4.7 and 5.5 inches. It has been noticed that the predictions by Ming-Chi Kou come out to be true most of the time so the iPhone 7 & 7 Plus will possibly have same sizes as iPhone 6 and 6 Plus. The phone will have 12 MP primary camera and 2 MP front facing camera for self shoot. Talking about the memory, both the phones could have upto 256 GB internal memory that may increase the price a bit. The phones will run latest iOS 9 having 1.5 Ghz dual-core CPU coupled with A9 chipset and upto 3GB of ram to minimise the lag time to zero. Since both the phones are ultra advanced and carrying such a great hardware, they could be priced a bit higher. It is expected that they will fall under the price range of 1000 to 1200 USD based upon internal memory sizes. The price may also vary depending upon the the country you are living in.
