In the wake of the documentary Surviving R. Kelly, two more women have come forward with claims that R. Kelly targeted them for sexual abuse as teenagers. Since the Lifetime docuseries aired in January, calls to #MuteRKelly have strengthened. Kelly has since been dropped by his record label and reportedly has left his Chicago recording studio. Police in both Atlanta and Chicago are investigating claims that R.
