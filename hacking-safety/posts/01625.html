The next The Lord of the Rings LEGO set has been released. The Lord of the Rings: Bara-dûr set was an instant success & a collectors piece. Which is why we have created a Limited Edition display case perfect for the next LEGO set from Middle-earth. Our Limited Edition The Lord of the Rings Bara-dûr display case will transport you to the Plateau of Gorgoroth in Mordor where Barad-dûr is found. The display will keep the Eye of Sauron protected as it imposingly watches over Middle-earth from the highest tower spreading fear. Our Limited Edition premium display case has been designed specifically for set 10333 and is made of clear premium acrylic, making our cases the stand out choice.
