About us

www.pickleball-shop.de · https://www.pickleball-shop.de/about-us/

The team behind pickleball-shop.de consists of players who put their many years of experience from the sport into the equipment offered to achieve the best result for customers. We have made it our mission to select the best material from the multitude of pickleball suppliers to enable our customers to improve their game and take it to the next level. We have a select range of different top brands in the store and try to meet every type of player. From the simple wooden paddle to the top carbon paddle, you will find everything. Be it an indoor or outdoor ball, we have the leading manufacturers in our program. If you are not sure about the difference between the different types of balls, please contact our team of experts before buying. Professional pickleball nets as mobile nets or also as fixed nets. The mobile nets are set up in no time and very easy to tighten. We have been selling pickleball equipment for 6 years and have tested almost every material ourselves or had it tested by experts. So we can say for sure if a paddle has control or power and which ball is best for which surface. We don't guess...we know ;-) No problem - call us and an expert team of players will advise you.
