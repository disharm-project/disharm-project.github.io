SHIPPING TIME CALCULATOR: 1-2 business day packaging time + shipping transit time chosen at checkout = estimated delivery time! SLIME CARE GUIDE STICKY SLIME? It is normal for slime to arrive sticky. This may happen due to warm weather and certain slime textures are more likely than others to be a bit sticky. To restore it to its normal consistency, simply add a small amount of activator and knead until you achieve your desired texture. Be careful not to add too much activator, as this will result in a stiff slime. Click Here to Watch a Tutorial on How to Fix Sticky Slime STIFF SLIME? It's common for slimes to arrive stiff due to cold weather. Most slimes can thaw out within a couple hours if you leave them in a warm place or by warming it up with your hands. In more extreme cases of stiff slime you can try the following solutions: White Glue Base: Mix small amounts of lotion in slime and knead until your slime has reached your desired consistency. Clear Glue Base: Keep slime in an airtight container in a warm place for a few days to allow the slime to soften up. You can also try adding small amounts of glycerin which will soften the slime. MORE TIPS: Always store your slime in its airtight container when not in use. Regularly activate slime to prevent it from melting completely. Wash your hands before and after use to keep your slime clean. ACCIDENTALLY GOT SLIME ON THE CARPET? ON YOUR CLOTHES? Don’t worry!
