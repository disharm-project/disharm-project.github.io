Horatio mcgann

There's a quaint little man Called Horatio McGann Who wears a hat made of fruit And a watermelon suit! Now this odd little man Named Horatio McGann Has a shirt made of sweets And chocolate shoes on his feet! For this strange little man Born Horatio McGann Has socks made of crisps And liquorice gloves on his fists! So this nice little man Known as Horatio McGann Is never short of food But comes home in the nude!
