Sorry, we don't have images or video for this item. Focuses on the experiences of several couples who have experienced the joys and problems associated with having twins. Includes comments by an obstetrician who explains the reasons why complications often attend the birth of twins, as well as footage showing an actual multiple birth. Our collection comprises over 40,000 moving image works, acquired and catalogued between the 1940s and early 2000s. As a result, some items may reflect outdated, offensive and possibly harmful views and opinions. ACMI is working to identify and redress such usages. Learn more about our collection and our collection policy here. If you come across harmful content on our website that you would like to report, let us know. This work has not been digitised and is currently unavailable to view online. It may be possible for approved reseachers to view onsite at ACMI. Learn more about accessing our collection
