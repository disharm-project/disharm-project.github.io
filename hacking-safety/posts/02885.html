This thread is a place to discuss quests for Treadmillasaurus Rex. Here is a list of the current quests available for this hat-loving game...Percent Daily Values are base...Reach 20,000 caloriesDifficulty: MediumKing of the TreadmillReach 50,000 calories. Difficulty: HardHat TrickCollect three hat level ups in one game. Difficulty: MediumTen Spins, None of the Calories. Reach ten spins before you gain 1,000 calories. Difficulty: Medium Got 40k twice in a row then it started going downhill. When I got those 40k runs it didn't seem like it took any longer than five minutes, but after that it felt like it was taking 10+ minutes just to get above 20k. Kept getting a lot of useless stuff from the wheel. Also, I dislike how the wheel doesn't give you extra lives. It'd make playing way less frustrating. This game, must've took 30+ tries to get 50k, but I achieved it earlier today. Once I got it, I realised that I didn't get it earlier because I was moving around too much. I'd have ideal positions where jumping I wouldn't get hit by the spikes, but I kept moving because of nervousness. During my winning run I decided not to move from spots where I'd live for extended amounts of time and got 50k. Think I had two lives left by the time I got it too. There were plenty of times where I thought I was gonna die by the spikes, the t-rex would be so close to them, but I only died once before reaching 50k and it was because of a laser. I wonder if anyone has actually gotten the "Win The Game" Slot on the wheel. Over the course of atleast 30 games I've never seen it hit that spot once. I think that's there just for show. I've played several games where I've gotten 50+ spins and that never came up. I'm as bad as everybody else, got like 30k+ a few times. Oh, this game is so frustrating I'm only able to get to 40k+. I'm not giving up, though. However, I don't know why if I die I tend to respawn over a spikeball or close to a laser and I don't have time to react. And I die... again. It would be great if the wheel gave you extra lives. I havent even got 10k... I'm absolute (insert negative word here)Randomness is the way forward After reviewing a thread off of another site, Jmtb02 stated that the "Win the Game" slot is impossible to land on. I've given up on the 50k Quest. Over 50 attempts and the best i've hit is 31k. I got 49k on it a couple of days ago. I was so upset at how close I was that I just turned my computer off. Doubt it even registered a high score for me, but apparently it's not hard for a lot of people to get 50k+ so my score would've been meager anyway xD finally got the 50000,god i hated this game. Just a little tip i noticed whilst playing for those struggling to rack up large amounts of points due to excessive wind/treadmill increases: The spinner spins for every three jumps you make, so try not to jump too haphazardly, especially at the beginning when things are slow; it is more effective to run on the treadmill and jump only to avoid spikeballsHope this helps! This quests is very hard! Laughing so hard for my obvious luck. I got 73k on first attempt and I was rank 500 of all time and I was like: others really suck at this game. But then I came here and all I see is people complaining about the quest I will try to play sometime again it so I can see how much I can get without luck. You must be logged in to post a reply!
