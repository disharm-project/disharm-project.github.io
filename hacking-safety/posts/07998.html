Les 6 plus belles cascades a la reunion

In this article, you will find the top six most beautiful waterfalls on Reunion Island where you can swim and enjoy the beauty of Reunion Island nature. Reunion is very famous for its circuses: Salazie, Mafate and Cilaos, with its ramparts and its national park classified as a UNESCO World Heritage Site. This award is the pride of Reunion Islanders and of the many travelers who have come from all over the world to enjoy the tourism in Réunion. Reunion Island has many waterfalls spread all over the island that will delight young and old alike, during your stay in Reunion. Whether it's for a swim in rather cool water or just to immortalize the place, discover our selection of the most beautiful waterfalls on Reunion Island below. The Grand Galet waterfall also called Langevin waterfall, is located in the town of Saint Joseph and is made up of multiple waterfalls. It is the most famous and surely the most beautiful of the island of Reunion. Discover the Grand Galet waterfall

Niagara waterfall is located northeast of the Reunion Island. It is part of the town of Sainte-Suzanne, its spectacular waterfall from a height of 25 meters rushes into a large basin well hidden at the bottom of sugar cane plantations. Discover the Niagara waterfall

Located in the northeast of Reunion near the Salazie circus, The Iron Hole is one of the most beautiful geological curiosities of the island and is home to the largest waterfall in France with waterfalls of over 300 meters. Discover the Iron Hole

The Peace basin is a water point formed of basalt, located in the town of Saint-benoit filled with several waterfalls and a torrent. The most important draws its source from the Roches River which flows several kilometers below. Discover the Peace Basin

L'Anse des Cascades is a natural site in the municipality of Sainte-Rose east of Reunion Island. You can admire its waterfalls in a magnificent green setting very popular with tourists and locals. Discover theCascades Cove

The Cormorant basin located to the west of Reunion Island, not far from the seaside resort of Saint-Gilles-les-Bains and of Hermitage beach, is part of the municipality of St.Paul. Discover the cormorant basin waterfall

There are a multitude of waterfalls on Reunion Island. This list is only a small glimpse of the most famous, if we were to really make a list of the most beautiful we should include all of them, because they each have their own charm and beauty, in this atmosphere. typical of Reunion Island.
