A man and woman got out of a car and snatched the child as he walked down Moss Lane, they then took him into nearby bushes next to Riley's Snooker Club on Albert Close, assaulted him and drove off leaving him by the road side. Police say the boy is 'extremely traumatised' and they are appealing for witnesses to the incident which took place on Saturday, April 9 between 5pm and 5.30pm. The attackers got out of a silver car described as similar to a Vauxhall Omega, in poor condition with scratches along the left wing. The man, who was driving, is described as white, between 30 and 40-years-old, of slim well-muscled build, with short grey hair and he wore black trousers. His co-attacker is described as a white female with tanned skin, around 30-years-old with elbow length, brown, messy hair and a small nose. She was wearing a black top and black trousers with a multi-coloured knee length skirt over the top. Detective Inspector Simon Collier from Whitefield CID said: "We need to hear from anyone who may recognise the man and woman responsible for this despicable attack. Their descriptions are extremely detailed and I'm sure there must be someone out there who knows who they are. "The 11-year-old boy is extremely traumatised from what he has been through and it is vital we catch the people who have done this to him. "The car is quite distinctive so we would ask anyone who may have seen the car to contact us as soon as possible." Anyone who may be able to help the investigation should contact Whitefield CID on: 0161 856 4647 or Crimestoppers anonymously on: 0800 555 111.
