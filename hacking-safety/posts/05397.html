WATCH LIVE GERMANTOWN (WPVI) -- Philadelphia police are looking for a suspect who robbed a cell phone store at gunpoint in the city's Germantown section. Police say the suspect approached a 26-year-old employee at gunpoint and demanded cell phones and cash. He got away with 25 phones and an unknown amount of cash before fleeing the scene.
