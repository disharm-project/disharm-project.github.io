20 things to watch on netflix

While we’re all practicing social distancing and spending much more time at home than before, I thought it would be fun to share some of my favorite things that we’ve watched on Netflix over the last couple of years. I don’t know about you, but it’s nice to have a little escape from the uncertainty of what is currently happening in our world with a little entertainment at the end of the day. You Unbelievable Mindhunter Narcos Bloodline House of Cards The Sinner (season 1 was our favorite) Gilmore Girls The Office New Girl Arrested Development Schitt’s Creek The Pharmacist Killer Inside: The Mind of Aaron Hernandez Making a Murderer, Season 1 The Staircase Evil Genius Cheer Love is Blind* Somebody Feed Phil I’ll Have What Phil’s Having Chef’s Table Any of Sebastian Maniscalco Standup * I feel like I need to add this disclaimer here. This show is absolutely ridiculous. I don’t watch the Bachelor, but I imagine it’s even more absurd than that. It’s clearly not reality, but it is 100% mindless and entertaining, if you enjoy reality shows.
