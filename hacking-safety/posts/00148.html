How to choose a right fishing rod

Fishing has always been a favorite activity one can invest his time in. It is considered the best exercise for people for every age group. To increase the interest and excitement of this activity it is important that you have a good and right fishing rod. As it all depends on the fishing rod whether you will be able to catch a huge number of fishes or not. A fishing rod can make or fail your day for sure. With good fishing skills and techniques, you can attain the purpose for which you have visited the beach. Of course, skillful fishing is not at all an easy task; it requires patience, knowledge, and technique. So here in this post, you are going to know how you can select the best fishing rod for a better experience. Measurements and Specifications Choose a length The way to measure the length of the rod is from the end to the butt. Fishing rods are not available in many sizes but they come from four feet up to fourteen feet. The larger rods have the potential to be disassembled into various pieces for proper transportation. The length of the rod depends on what and how you have to fish. The rods can be differentiated into two forms: · Short Rods Short rods are shorter in size and cast shorter distances and they are basically used for lightweight tasks. · Long Rods

As the name suggests, it cast for longer distances and are the best for surfcasting. It means when you are fishing from a boat or pier rather than a beach. 2. Decide the weight The weight of the rod is indicative of the weight it can normally carry and also tells about its holding capacity. Exactly the weight can be understood by the weight of the bait it is designed to cast. 3. Understand the action By the word action, it is understood that the action of the rod is the point where it bends. The measurement is of different types as it can vary like some are ultra-light or very heavy. The lighter action is indicative of the bend that is closer to the butt of the rod and the heavy point is indicative of the bend that is closer to the tip. Decide the material Fishing rods are usually made of graphite, fiberglass, or a combination of both materials. · Graphite rods are lighter and stiffer and more brittle than fiberglass rods. They are more sensitive, making them a bit more expensive than the fiber rods. · Fiber rods are heavy in weight and flexible in comparison to graphite rods. They are strong and difficult to break.
