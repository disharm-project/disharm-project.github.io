We’re going digital! Our 2020 Annual Summit in partnership with Mastercard will be virtual from October 6-8 with the theme “Shifting the Paradigm: The Female Economy 2020 and Beyond.” The event will bring together major players from both the tech and financial services ecosystems at an unprecedented scale to create a space for collaboration and advancement for all. The 2020 Summit will include a special focus on FinTech and FemTech, as we explore the latest innovations from Mastercard and other members and ecosystem players eager to advance all segments of the female economy. Backed up by proprietary research this three-day best-practice event will focus on topics such as: Many of our members have invested in and acquired FinTechs, and/or have launched digital banks and integrated various platforms to offer women unparalleled services. With the 2020 Summit, we are inviting the FinTech sector to learn what it takes to be gender intelligent and to create partnerships with leading incumbents for a better future. Detailed information on the 2020 Summit will be announced in June. Stay tuned to our social media channels and watch your inbox for details on how to register.
