ENT (Ear, Nose, & Throat), also called otolaryngology is the field of medicine that deals with diagnosing & treating diseases concerned with the ear, nose, throat, head, & neck. Surgery is usually elective & carried out during the day; an average half-day in theatre that might comprise three operations, which may range from simple procedures that take 15 minutes to more complicated surgeries lasting up to twelve hours. While most days involve direct clinical care, be it outpatient work or surgery, some time should be allocated to teaching, clinical governance, & departmental & multidisciplinary meetings. National Health Service(NHS) consultant salaries are the same for all specialties but vary between Scotland (highest), England, Northern Ireland, & Wales (lowest) & increase with service (up to nineteen years). In 2020 the salary bands range from £77,779 to £109,849. A “purely” private clinic is rare in the UK. On average, otolaryngologists can make a profit of an additional 98% of their NHS salary by working in the private sector. In 2018, private otolaryngologists made a profit of £107,000; this is higher than most specialties, only behind orthopedics (£128,000) & radiology (£117,000). “In India, various reputed organizations offer attractive salary packages to ENT Specialists”. As an ENT technician one can start with Rs. 20 -25 thousand per month.
