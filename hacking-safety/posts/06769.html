Rayne@bigwavedrops.surf

In San Francisco, between killer waves and epic rides, Dr. Joseph Hardeman, a fellow big wave rider and audiologist, spotted a problem. His surf crew was constantly battling ear issues. Not cool. While grinding at UC San Francisco Medical Center, Dr. Joe turned his frustration into action. Teaming up with some UCSF brainiacs, he began tinkering with formulas, trying to craft the perfect solution. Tested in epic wipeouts, gusty winds, and icy waters, BigWave Drops are as hardcore as the conditions they were born in. The SF surf community was psyched. Dr. Joe's ear drops became so sought after that he literally had surfers chasing him down SF streets for a restock. Seeing the stoke and the need, it was time to bring BigWave Drops to the masses. Now crafted in a certified production facility in Ventura California, Dr. Joe's vision has gone global, helping water athletes everywhere. Ride the waves, and let us take care of your ears. "I was inspired to create BigWave Drops ear care products to keep my friends in the surfing community active in the water. Our mission is to keep everyone doing what they love ear-pain free" Joseph Hardeman, Au.D. received his clinical doctorate in Audiology from the University of California, San Diego in 2016. He completed his clinical residency at University of California, San Francisco and participated in the Allen Ryan, Ph.D. laboratory utilizing a Department of Defense grant to complete research on the fundamental mechanisms of the inner ear, brainstem, and brain. He has experience in research and clinical applications of acoustics, hearing, balance, electrophysiology, and neuroplasticity. We're hear for you. Rayne@bigwavedrops.surf
