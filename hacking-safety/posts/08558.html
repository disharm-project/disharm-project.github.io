I found out my husband was cheating from a tiktok video

Our reader, Sally, is 40 years old and never thought a simple recipe channel could change her life completely. She found out that her husband of 10 years had cheated on her many times and kept it a secret. Sally wrote to us, asking for advice. Here’s her story, which surprised even our experienced editorial team! Sally’s family was a happy one, and even the tragedy didn’t ruin it. Sally, a 40-year-old woman, didn’t expect anything bad to happen in her life, but her husband surprised her with a bitter truth one day. She wrote us a letter sharing her shocking story, making us believe that secrets eventually come to light. Sally began her letter, saying, “My husband Peter and I have been happily married for over 10 years. We’ve faced many challenges together, including the loss of our eldest son, Josh, in a car accident. Peter was my rock during that time, or so I thought.” She continued, “I believed our tragedy brought us closer, and I never imagined Peter could betray me. We always trusted each other completely. We never checked each other’s phones or private things. Trust was the foundation of our family, but one day that trust was broken.” Sally gave more details about Peter, saying, “Peter is a workaholic who loves his job. He recently got promoted, which meant more business trips. He told me about this before the promotion, so I was prepared for him to be away more often. I was okay with it because I knew how much his career meant to him and always tried to make him comfortable at home and on his trips.” She added, “Peter always expressed his love for me, and we never lacked emotional connection. He often brought me gifts from his trips, which I later realized were bought out of guilt.” One day turned the life of the woman upside down. Sally continues her story, saying, “Two weeks ago, Peter went on a long and exhausting business trip. I wanted to make him feel special when he got back home. I decided to cook something nice for him as a surprise. I’m not a great cook, so I often look for recipes online. I don’t follow any specific channels; I just find something that works with what I have.” She goes on, “This time was no different. I searched for recipes and found a TikTok account for housewives who cook simple but delicious dishes. The account belonged to a woman who made short cooking videos. I watched one video and froze in shock because I saw a man in the background, facing away from the camera. I looked closer and realized it was my husband, Peter! I then looked through more of the woman’s posts and discovered that Peter had been acting like a ‘dad’ to this woman’s three sons. In one video, I even found out that they were planning to have a baby together.” Sally was deadly shocked. Sally wrote, “I didn’t confront Peter right away. I needed time to process everything. I decided to watch him closely, and soon I realized Peter was living a double life. He would tell each of us that he was on a work trip while he was actually with the other. Until my shocking discovery, I had no idea there was a problem in our marriage. I was so shocked that I contacted a divorce attorney. The attorney told me to keep what I had learned to myself at first. Later, he said I could confront my husband, which I did.” Sally revealed, “Even with the TikTok posts from Peter’s mistress and the evidence from my lawyer, my husband refused to admit his guilt. He even tried to make me think I was crazy. But eventually, he confessed. He begged for my forgiveness, but I refused and insisted on a divorce. After he moved out, I decided to visit his mistress. To my surprise, she seemed to be expecting me. She even boasted about her affair with my husband. I found out that my husband had bought a house for her. While talking to her, he always accused me of being lazy and a gold digger. I don’t know what to do. I want him back and want to accept his apologies, but I also feel like I will never be able to forgive him for what he did to me.” Your email address will not be published.
