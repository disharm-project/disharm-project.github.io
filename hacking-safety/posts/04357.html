Who This Protective Styles Regimen Is For? If you rely on either half or full lace frontals to create the hairline you desire, this 6-piece regimen will ensure your hair and scalp remain moisturized, conditioned, and healthy. Just remember, even if it’s a protective style, your lace front should only be installed for 5 to 6 weeks at a time, or it can damage your hair and scalp. Our sulfate-free shampoo allows you to easily cleanse your scalp, natural hair, and lace frontal every 7 to 10 days. It’s infused with Peppermint to soothe your scalp and stimulate new growth and Biotin to keep your hair healthy and strong. Apply between your parts or tracks, and gently massage it into your scalp—then from root to tip. Rinse thoroughly, repeat, and rinse again. Our multi-purpose conditioner is ideal for preparing your hair for lace frontals, protective styles, post-shampoo conditioning, deep conditioning, and hydrate your hair before wrapping it at bedtime. It’s infused with Coconut Milk, Aloe, and Sunflower Oil to keep your hair hydrated, conditioned, soft, and healthy. Instantly soothe irritation and itch with our Peppermint, Aloe, and Shea Butter infused serum. The nozzle is designed so that you can easily distribute the serum directly to the areas of your scalp that are irritated. It’s a must to soothe irritation after your lace frontal is installed. Apply our growth oil to your clean and conditioned hair before and after installing your frontal—then 2 to 3 days a week thereafter. It’s infused with Coconut and Sweet Almond Oil to seal and protect your hair and scalp. Remember, a little bit goes a long way. Our Aloe and Shea Butter infused serum should be used every other day, especially the week after your frontal is installed. It will soothe tight scalp, preserve moisture, heal damaged hair, and stimulate new growth. You can also apply it to your hair as an overnight mask before wrapping your head at night. Keep the edges of your natural hair soft, smooth, frizz-free, and flake-free with our lightweight cream. It will smooth your hairline and stimulate new growth in damaged areas. If your hair has thinned out, massage it in for 60 seconds before smoothing and styling.
