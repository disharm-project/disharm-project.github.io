We have purchased two new females from the Halbeath dispersal this month. Halbeath Kerry L425 is a February 2011-born Bosullow Elmark G209 daughter out of Halbeath Kerry H333, a Blelack Telestar A240 daughter. She carries a milk figure of +17, with a terminal index of +38 and self replacing index of +45, she was purchased in-calf and due in January to Ducanziemere Edwin for 5500gns. Our other purchase at 5000gns was Halbeath Kim K416, she is a 2010 born daughter of Idvies Elmonarch F289. Her dam is Halbeath Kim F216, a Halbeath Knight C069 daughter. She has also been running with Duncanziemere Edwin. We currently have a range of 2019-born bulls for sale. They are all performance recorded, semen tested have great calving ease and high growth rates.
