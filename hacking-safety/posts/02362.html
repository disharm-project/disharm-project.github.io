Because of the Indian Cinema, there is a misconception that a plastic surgeon can completely change your looks. That is not true, but instead, plastic surgeons are involved in repairing reconstruction and replacing tissues like the skin, musculoskeletal tissue, and maxillofacial structures. The scope of plastic surgery is not just limited to the face, but they are also very helpful in dealing with the hand, breast, genitals, legs, and other body areas.
