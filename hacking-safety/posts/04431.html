Today (Sept. 6), Dairyland Seed, a part of Dow AgroSciences, announced 22 new corn hybrids for the 2018 growing season, eight of which use Enlist weed control technology. “We’re really excited about releasing such a robust variety of products for 2018,” says T.J. Strachota, Dairyland Seed marketing leader. “These new products allow our farmers more choices in management decisions and will work for a more diverse set of operations.” These new varieties were plot tested last year and selected for their high-performance and proven capabilities, according to the company. In particular, Strachota has high performance expectations for DS-9090RA, DS-9804RA and DS-7106PRE.
