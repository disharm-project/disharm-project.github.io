ABOUT ME GALLERY BOUTIQUE GOURMET TREATS CONTACT More Hi everyone! Today, I'm sharing this simple DIY Burlap and Lace Mason Jar Vase tutorial. It's very easy to put together. I had made a couple of them with glass jam jars which I have been collecting over a period of time. These were used as guest table centerpieces for an event. For the event, I used fresh chrysanthemums. A bunch of fresh Baby's breath flowers would look absolutely gorgeous. Just like my other projects on my blog, I have put together a YouTube video so you can watch to get a better idea of how I achieved the finished look. I will make sure to link it below. Mason jar Burlap / Jute Ribbon White Nylon Lace Hot Glue Gun Jute twine I wrapped some jute twine on the rim of the jar and tied the ends into a bow. This part is not shown in the video.
