Ready to Apply? Advance your career as a public health practitioner, educator, or researcher with IU Online’s Graduate Certificate in Sexual and Reproductive Health. Impact your community with comprehensive training in the promotion and management of sexual and reproductive health. Course Delivery: 100% Online

Total Credits: 15

In-State Tuition Per Credit: $518.62

Out-of-State Tuition Per Credit: $621.98

Cost of attendance may vary by campus. View the total cost calculator As a student in the Graduate Certificate in Sexual and Reproductive Health, you will work with internationally renowned IU faculty members and certified sexuality educators with the American Association of Sexuality Educators, Counselors, and Therapists (AASECT) to explore research in sexual and reproductive health, create and evaluate curricula, review the epidemiology of sexual health, and build skills for seeking external funding. The curriculum aligns with AASECT content knowledge areas. You also study the major systems used to survey sexual and reproductive health and explore the legal and ethical foundations of sexual and reproductive health surveillance. You learn how to apply for and manage extramural funds for sexual and reproductive health and practice activities. You’ll graduate with an Indiana University certificate respected by employers worldwide—and you can work on yours anytime and anywhere. Plus, you’ll enjoy personalized support services throughout your academic journey.
