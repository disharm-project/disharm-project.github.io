Welcome to the official Black Magic II Wiki! This wiki is currently under construction, and is designed to help anyone that has any questions about Black Magic II, or to help newcomers understand the game and it's mechanics, or simply for anyone! Our goal is to document all the gameplay, features and details behind the game. Feel free to add anything about the game! To start, follow the directions on the right side of this page. Black Magic II: Phantom's Causatum, or simply Black Magic II, is a Roblox fighting game by QQ Studios inspired by numerous fighting games such as Street Fighter, BlazBlue, and Under Night In-Birth. We also have a Discord server, where you can receive gameplay help from other members of the community, showcase your art skills, and catch the latest on Black Magic II's development.
