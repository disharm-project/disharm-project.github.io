Welcome to Little Red Wagon, a fun place to challenge your creativity! Each Wednesday, one of The Girlfriends will post a new challenge that we hope will inspire you to try something different with your scrapbooking supplies. Then on the following Tuesday, we will feature our favourites from those who played along. Have fun and enjoy the ride! One day a group of girlfriends got together to play with their papers, stamps, markers, ribbons, buttons and the rest of their seemingly endless supply of other 'have-to-have' scrapbooking and card making goodies. It was so much fun to talk, share, laugh and create with each other that they decided to create a blog that would motivate, inspire and challenge them on a weekly basis to use some of the supplies they have acquired. They also thought it would be fun to have all the other girlfriends out there join in and share their creations based on the weekly challenges. So, grab your wagon full of goodies (we know you have one! ), get creative and have fun! Just a note.... occasionally we will be unable to leave a comment on your entry if the Girlfriend visiting does not have an account for the method you used to link up. Don't worry though - you'll still be in the running for our Featured post... and I'll bet we loved your creation even if we couldn't tell you! If you publicly follow this blog and/or leave a comment on anything posted here, and/or add your project to the Linky, you do so in the knowledge that your username and profile or blog are visible to all who visit and in so doing have published your own personal details. You have also consented to our use of that personal information should your work be selected as one of our Featured projects.
