The charges that Norman is facing come from a drunken rampage. Norman started the rampage by kicking open the gate of a trailer park and approached a woman who was sitting outside her house. Norman punched her on the side of her head and damaged her house before continuing his rampage. Norman went to a neighbor’s house and fell through the home’s open door, which caused one of the residents to pick up a hammer so he could leave. Normal left the trailer park and yelled that he will be coming back to burn the trailer down. After leaving the park, Norman went to a nearby apartment complex, where he knocked on the doors of residents daring them to fight him. A resident shut down his door in the face of Norman and locked it down. Norman was angered with the act of the resident and replied with ramming his shoulder into the door, causing damage to the door and the frame that was holding it. After ramming into the door, Norman let himself into an apartment and screamed at 2 men inside the house to fight him. He chased the men and threw a lamp at them. Before leaving the apartment, Norman grabbed a slice of pizza from the apartment and chased a man into a bedroom. The other man in the apartment tried to call the authorities, but Norman snatched the landline from his hand and hit him in the back of the head. The men escaped from Norman by running outside but the drunken man didn’t stop there. Norman chased the men around the apartment complex for some time before he got knocked out by a chain fence. The men called the police so Norman could be arrested. The police arrived and found Norman in an intoxicated state while he was knocked out. Norman was handcuffed and was sending out death threats against the police officers. The victims of Norman’s rampage sustained minor injuries, but the woman that he punched in the head has a brain tumor. The woman said she would be required to go through a medical exam to make sure she was alright. Norman was arrested, threw in the Escambia County Jail. He is currently jailed on a $262,500 bond.
