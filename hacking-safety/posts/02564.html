Plantasia Tropical Zoo has all the ingredients to make your family day out in Swansea a special one. From mischievous monkeys swinging in the trees, to creepy crawlies lurking in the undergrowth, every animal in Plantasia brings the feel of a mysterious and beautiful rainforest, waiting to be discovered by you! So step inside this world to make some incredible discoveries with your family today at Plantasia Tropical Zoo. Plantasia is also home to a huge display of plant life from all continents of the world. The tropical indoor climate allows all sorts of exciting and extraordinary plants to flourish. Look out for aloe vera, black pepper, weeping figs, avocado, bird of paradise, coffee beans, bananas, Persian limes and so much more. If you're feeling brave, head into the danger zone, where you'll see poisonous plants up close. You can also find out what it feels like to be stuck in a sticky plant and you can even put your head inside a giant Venus fly trap. Don't worry, it won't take a snap out of you, just make sure you snap a selfie instead! Close out your visit to this tropical zoo with a nice bite at the Canopy Café before you embark on your journey home and be sure to keep Plantasia in mind for your next day out in South Wales. Whatever the weather or season, Plantasia Tropical Zoo will transport you to an otherworldly rainforest for a day. What could be better than that?
