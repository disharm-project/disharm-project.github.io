6 products We are proud to present to you our Big & Tall collection! Created for all the guys out there who struggle to find comfortable riding wear that keeps them safe and looking sexy whilst fitting your broad, tall or wide bodies! If you have a big & or tall frame then this is the collection for you. At Moto Establishment (Moto Est. ), you can browse our Big & Tall collection and know that most of the gear in this collection is suitable for your body proportions, taking the guess work out of finding the right gear for you. The riding pants in this collection are all made from material with give or have been specifically made to fit longer legs, broader shoulders or wider waist lines. The riding jackets in the collection are all made with flattering materials on bigger bodies, available in plus sizes from XL to 5XL or have lots of adjustment buckles to make the jacket fit your body type. The lifestyle products in this collection like the t-shirts are all suitable on a larger frame or curvier proportions. The other riding gear, including motorcycle gloves, are ones that run in larger sizes or are made to fit larger hands, yes we have plus size gloves, large helmets & big size boots. Included in our collection are some great brands such as 78 Motor Co., Black Arrow, Clutch, Corazzo, Grifter, Hedon, Liberta, REV'IT! & Tobacco. There are loads of options to suit Big & Tall size people & to suit your particular style. Although we have a wide range of options, rest assured that every piece of motorcycle gear is made to a high quality to ensure longevity so you will be riding in your perfect Big & Tall-friendly motorcycle gear for years to come.
