Make someone’s day, every day, with a career at Givens. Our team members have diverse backgrounds and experiences but unite with their passion for serving older adults in Asheville. A focus on residents, helping them feel at home and vital to the community, is what sets our team members apart. Givens fosters an inclusive, meaningful work environment with an equal opportunity employer while offering competitive salaries and excellent benefits. We also offer extended training and support for continued growth and job satisfaction. Visit Careers on Givens Communities to learn more about career opportunities and current job openings. Residents live fulfilling lives in a peaceful, rural setting. Hear from their experiences on how Givens Great Laurels provides fellowship among friends in a secure, comfortable community. I am thankful to be living my retirement dream here, in the beautiful mountains of Western North Carolina. It’s a very nice safe place to grow old. I call it my little piece of paradise. I love Givens Great Laurels. It means a lot to me to get to live here. It is a safe place. The best thing about Great Laurels is the staff who always give a friendly greeting and ask how I am doing. We are called by name and treated with respect and compassion.
