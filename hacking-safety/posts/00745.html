Gizmo the dog who went missing in us in 2015 found alive after 9 years

July 30, 2024 Admin News 0

In a heartwarming turn of events, a Las Vegas woman has been reunited with her pet chihuahua, Gizmo, who went missing nine years ago. The unexpected reunion began with an email that brought Judith Monarrez to her knees in tears. Back in 2015, a faulty gate in the backyard of her family’s home allowed Gizmo, then just 2 years old, to slip away unnoticed. Despite a desperate search that involved posting fliers and reaching out to Facebook groups, Ms. Monarrez’s efforts to find Gizmo were in vain. However, she never lost hope of reuniting with her beloved pet. Now 37, Ms. Monarrez has moved into her own home, earned a master’s degree, and started a teaching career. Yet, the memory of Gizmo remained close to her heart. Her persistence finally paid off when a woman found Gizmo and took him to an animal hospital. There, his microchip was scanned, triggering the notification that would change everything. Despite his changed appearance and behavior, Ms. Monarrez knew instantly it was Gizmo when they reunited at the vet’s office. “I can’t forget those yellow-amber eyes. I can’t forget them, ever – they’re Gizmo,” Ms. Monarrez told KTNV. Judith Monarrez called the emotional reunion “a miracle” and credited Gizmo’s microchip for making it possible. “Hindsight is 2020. I’m so glad I registered his microchip,” she said. Gizmo’s return has brought mixed emotions, as he has developed fears and health issues during their time apart. He was found in an alarming condition, with severe matting, loss of teeth, dental diseases, and a debilitating eye infection. “He was severely matted. They had to shave him down,” Ms. Monarrez told the outlet. “He’s missing a lot of teeth. They told us that he had teeth falling out of his mouth when they dropped him off.” Now, Monarrez and her family are focused on addressing Gizmo’s health issues and are dedicated to “showering him with all the love that we were holding onto for all those years.” To help cover the substantial medical bills, estimated to exceed $4,000 for initial visits alone, the family has set up a GoFundMe page.
