Bell Air Moving knows that relocating your business can be very stressful. So why not let our commercial movers take the load off your shoulders. Whatever your business premises: offices, warehouse, shop, factory or garage our personalised service can be tailored to your needs. To ensure minimal disruption to your business we’ll work with you; so that you can get on with your job while our business movers do theirs. To make your business relocation as smooth as possible our corporate moving experts will organise every aspect of the move – allocating however many vehicles and moving staff needed to complete the job in the most efficient and timely way possible. Available upon request: Certificate of insurance will be sent (emailed or faxed) directly from our Insurance agent to Management Company /Condo Association. The total number of hours is rounded up to the next quarter hour. E.g., if the total move time (labor + travel) was 3 hours and 10 minutes, the charge will be for 3 hours and 15 minutes. TOTAL COST = (total labor hours + travel time charge) * moving rate + packing materials (if any). E.g. : A customer is moving from Dedham MA to Natick MA at the rate of 135 $/hr. The move started at 8:00AM and finished at 12:00PM. The travel time charge was 45 minutes (that is 15 minutes to get to the pick-up location + 30 minutes to return to the office from the moving destination). In addition, 6 rolls of tape were used (we provide 2 free rolls, any excess tape used is charged to the customer). LABOR = (Labor + Travel) * rate = 4 hrs. + 45min. (travel Time) x 135 = $641.25 MATERIALS = 4 rolls of tape * $3.00/roll =$12.00 TOTAL MOVING COST = $641.00 + $12 = $653.25 All Services Navigation Other Contact Us © Copyright Bell Air Moving Inc. is fully licensed, bonded and insured.
