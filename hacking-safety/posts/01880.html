This vertical air blower is planned for inflate a sky dancer, large fabric sleeve representing a character (or crazy sleeve) to signal an event. Of course, this inflator can be used in other occasions. One of our customers, for example, uses them make giant soap bubbles ! - Powerful- Very light- Secure to the folding foot- No assembly - All sky dancers from 6 meters are adapting to this blower, from 43 to 45 cm. - We recommend placing the blower on a floor mat, pallet or other insulator from the ground. Indeed, Dust and pebbles are sucked into the ground and can damage the engine However, by being raised, the blower will be less likely to suck the canvas at restart. - Indeed, when the sky dancer is stopped, the canvas is placed next to the blower. At restart, it is possible that the canvas, too close to the bottom of the blower, is sucked from below ! There is a risk of tearing the sleeve, and may damage the engine and its propeller. Indeed, the engine will not support a heavy rain. If it is running under a small fine rain that lasts, then the moisture will not reach the engine. On the other hand, in case of heavy rain, or at a standstill, the blower should be sheltered ! Warning, this blower model don't have bright spot ! This is not a disadvantage because many of our customers already adapted on the the old, a more powerful spot, hanging on the grid on the top of the blower. If you fin that this blower is too powerful, you can open your sky dancer, especially at the top (hole in the head) and the junction arm / trunk. If, on the contrary, the blower does not seem to be powerful enough (for example, a sky dancer very high of 8 meters), in this case try to block air outlets, for example by knotting the ends of the arms.
