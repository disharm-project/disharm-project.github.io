Three men in a nationwide manhunt were being sought Thursday, July 5, 2018. According to Bowling Green police, the three are suspected of kidnapping and raping teenage sisters aged 13 and 14 in Ohio. The crime allegedly occurred at a Days Inn, where the girls were staying with their parents at the same time as the suspects. David Ramos Contreras (pictured above) was found in Lubbock hiding under a bed on Thursday, July 5, 2018. He was arrested and will be extradited back to Ohio to face charges. According to CBS News, the other two suspects (seen below with Contreras) have been identified as Arnulfo Ramos and Juan Garcia Rios Adiel (left to right in picture below) and are considered to be violent and dangerous. It's being reported that all three were in the U.S. illegally.
