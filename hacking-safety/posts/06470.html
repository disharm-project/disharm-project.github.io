Dear Families, Welcome to our website, which is designed to inform our community about important school information, such as academics, co-curricular, calendar, events, and staff contacts. As I enter my ninth year at Curtis, I continue to be impressed with the positive energy and enthusiasm from our students, the high level of commitment and expertise from our staff, and the caring and involved support from our families. Our Core Values -- Learning, Respect, and Responsibility -- guide our decision-making and beliefs as we strive to personalize the middle school experience, provide opportunities each day to grow and improve, and balance academic work with clubs, sports, laughter and fun. We are committed to developing strong partnerships with our families and we look forward to working with you. Don’t hesitate to ask questions, provide feedback, and share ideas and concerns, so that we at Curtis can best understand and support your children . We hope you utilize our school website and we look forward to a fantastic school year.
