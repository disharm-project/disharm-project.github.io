Merrell Chameleon 7 vs Moab 2… which pair is better for your hiking trips? On one corner, there’s the Merrell Chameleon 7 Stretch, a pair of hiking shoes that come in three colors. On another, there’s the Merrell MOab 2, another pair that targets hikers and enthusiasts alike. To help you with the answer, here’s a comparison report. Also read: Speedcross 4 vs Speedcross 3: Which Salomon to Get? Contents [hide] Use this chart to easily spot the difference in features. Let’s quickly discuss the key differences in features of the Chameleon 7 Stretch and the Moab 2. This feature is perhaps one of the deciding factors of hiking (or outdoor) shoes. After all, when you’re out there in the woods, you’ll be exposed to the elements, and the last thing you want to do is to get your feet wet. Otherwise, the risk of suffering from hypothermia is very high. As can be seen in the chart above, the Moab 2 is equipped with waterproof materials that help ensure that your feet stay dry when hiking. It also helps that this pair has a bellows, closed-cell tongue that keeps moisture at bay. Unfortunately, the Chameleon 7 does not have the same waterproof features. The Chameleon 7 Stretch has an elastic one-pull lace system, and the Moab 2 has a lace-up system. There are advantages and disadvantages of both, so your choice may be dictated by preference. The lugs of the Moab 2 are deeper than the lugs of the Merrell Chameleon 7 Stretch. Also read: Merrell Chameleon 2 vs 5, Which is Better for Hiking? So… which, in our opinion, is the better pair of hiking shoes? Here’s what we have to say. Get the Merrell Moab 2. Its waterproof features are a big plus.
