Unlocking the Power of Vehicle Auctions: A Step-by-Step Guide You will be required to create an account using your Name, ID, valid email address and Telephone Number. Your profile contains information on the vehicles you have bid, bid amounts and your successful bids. Once you have registered and logged in you can view the vehicle you would like to buy and place your OFFER AMOUNT.Each vehicle has a bidding fee which you will be required to pay in order to complete the bidding process. You will key in your OFFER AMOUNT and phone number and then click submit to receive an MPESA prompt and complete the payment of the bidding fee. Your bid will be successful upon payment of the required bidding fee. The bidding fee is none refundable . You can bid for as many vehicles as you like but only one bid is accepted per vehicle. The bidding window closes after 14days. Vehicles are sold on ‘As is, Where is’ basis. Offer letter shall be issued to successful bidder(s) who will be required to pay the full amount within 24 hours. Final allocation will be on a first come first served basis. The Bank will confirm receipt of your payment and immediately issue you a letter authorizing release of the vehicle from the yard. You will thereafter collect the Logbook and necessary documentation to facilitate transfer of the motor vehicle.
