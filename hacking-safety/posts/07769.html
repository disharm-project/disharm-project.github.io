Pencils Pens & Markers Surfaces Wondering what to do these school holidays? Concerned your child isn’t tapping into their creative side enough? Then check out Eckersley’s with its wide range of quality kids arts and craft supplies. From kids safe paint and paint by numbers, to books, chalk, clay, face paint and glitter glue, your kids will have endless creative fun with Eckersley’s. Eckersley’s stocks only the best kids arts and craft supplies, with brands including Chroma, Derivan, EC, Educraft, Jasart and FIMO. You can pick up kindy brush packs and junior aprons for the little ones, chalkboard paint for your child’s play area, and stackable plastic palettes ideal for distributing paint. Eckersley’s maintains a strong involvement in the arts and education sectors and so we know what crafts kids are actively involving themselves in. We proudly supply art and design materials directly to schools across Australia as we believe that kids should start their art and design journey young, and so we stock products we know will get them hooked.
