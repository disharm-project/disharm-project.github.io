Start your morning off right with our Breakfast Variety Pack, available from Monroe Systems for Business! This delightful assortment includes one box each of Little Debbie Oatmeal Creme Pies, Honey Buns, Donut Sticks, and Drake's Coffee Cakes. Perfect for any time you’re craving something delicious, these treats are sure to please. Oatmeal Creme Pies: The pioneer product of Little Debbie, Oatmeal Creme Pies feature two soft, chewy oatmeal cookies baked with whole grain oats and molasses, layered with delicious cream. Each box contains 12 oatmeal creme pies, perfect for a sweet start to your day or an afternoon pick-me-up. Honey Buns: Little Debbie Honey Buns are a soft, golden pastry baked with a touch of honey and cinnamon, then finished with a sweet glaze. Each box contains 6 honey buns, making them a delightful treat for breakfast or a mid-day snack. Donut Sticks: Enjoy the classic taste of Little Debbie Donut Sticks, coated with a light sweet glaze. These cake-style donut sticks are perfect for dunking in your morning coffee or as an on-the-go snack. Each box contains 6 individually wrapped donut sticks. Coffee Cakes: Drake's Coffee Cakes are a moist, classic treat topped with plenty of sweet, cinnamon-flavored streusel. Each carton contains five twin-wrapped pastries, ideal for sharing or enjoying solo. Individually wrapped for convenience, these snacks are great for children in school, college students, or as a quick treat at work. Their versatile use makes them a pantry staple, whether for breakfast, lunch, snacks, or dessert.
