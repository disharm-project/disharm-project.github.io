Mumbai: All Deepika Padukone needs in her 70s is a happy family and a peaceful life! The leggy lass, who recently wowed at the 70th Cannes Film Festival, in an interview with France 24 said, "When I am 70, I will hopefully have a little home somewhere with a beautiful view, lots of children and grandchildren and a good life. I want to have a peaceful life." Further sharing the experience of her Hollywood debut ' xXx: Return of Xander Cage,' the Mastani of Bollywood said, it was because of her co-star Vin Diesel that she had all the amazing experiences. "Let me set the record straight, he ( Vin Diesel) is in love with me. He is adorable. He is the most amazing person I have ever met. He is generous, he is kind and he is the reason I have had all the amazing experiences. He is a friend for life," she said. The 31-year-old actress grabbed all the eyeballs with her graceful two-day appearance at the Cannes Film Festival. While she wore a jewel-toned maroon creation by Marchesa Notte on Day 1, Day 2 was marked with a minimalistic chick green Brandon Maxwell thigh slit gown. On the work front, Deepika will next be seen in Sanjay Leela Bhansali's 'Padmavati,' alongside Ranveer Singh and Shahid Kapoor.
