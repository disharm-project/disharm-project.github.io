Early childhood yoga program

Are the kids struggling with their emotions? Do they have separation anxiety? Not able to transition to calm and settle? Bringing passion and our expertise to our kids yoga programs. Introduce Kids Yoga and Mindfulness to your Childcare Centre. Children practicing regular yoga has shown to improve focus, balance and co-ordination, resilience, self-regulation, mind-body awareness and physical fitness. Classes focus on giving students the tools to learn to relax, reduce stress in their lives, promote positive thinking and improve co-ordination, allowing them to learn to listen to their own body. Children’s Yoga for Kindergarten and Early Education Centers. Kids will love yoga! Offering Classes for children aged 1 to 5 years. Our kids yoga programs and Classes are play based, fun and interactive. Kids yoga programs follow and are linked to the Early Years Learning frame. Tranquil Kids yoga programs are designed and age appropriate with yoga poses for moving the kids bodies, fun breathing activities – for mindfulness. With a perfect blend of yoga poses, creative movement, games, dance and music. For example we may shake our crazies out, hop like a bunny, jump like a frog, hiss like a snake, roar like a lion. New weekly themes and yoga stories like the hungry caterpillar, going on a bear hunt, we use our imagination to move our body in yoga poses. Using props for practicing and enhancing the learning with deep breathing with pom poms, straws, breathing ball and bubbles. Tranquil kids yoga offers weekly, fortnightly, monthly or one-off classes. Classes are generally but are flexible. 1-2yr – 10/15 min 3yr – 15/20 min 4 yr – 20/25 min 5yr Kindergarten – 30/40 mins You can have multiple classes in 1 hour. These are parent paid or Centre paid funded programs. Introduce Kids Yoga and Mindfulness to your Centre.
