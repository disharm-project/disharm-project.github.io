Illustration created by Canadian artist Loogart featuring Ottawa's famous landmarks in their simplest and iconic forms. It is off-white, uncoated, and the superior choice for art enthusiasts who appreciate world-class quality to enjoy artworks most luxuriously. While the poster and frame are packed separately in one box, final assembly is required by the end recipient.
