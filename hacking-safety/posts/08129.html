Kidney stones are a common disease, and generally kidney stones can be helped by diet. So, what can kidney stone eat good, kidney stone daily should not eat what food? Let's take a look at what can be a good introduction to kidney stones! 1. Drink plenty of plain water. Drinking more water can dilute the urine, and the concentration of calcium ions and oxalates will be reduced, and calcium oxalate stones will not be formed. Studies have shown that increasing urine volume by 50 percent can reduce the incidence of kidney stones by 86 percent. 2, black fungus black fungus also contains a variety of minerals, can produce a strong chemical reaction to various stones, stripping, differentiation, erosion of stones, so that stones shrink, discharge. For the initial stones, keep eating black fungus 1-2 times a day, pain, nausea and other symptoms can be relieved in 2-4 days, stones can disappear in about 10 days, for larger and more solid stones, the effect is poor, but such as long-term consumption of black fungus, can also make some people's stones gradually smaller and broken, discharged from the body. Often eat black fungus, but also to prevent the occurrence of thrombosis and other diseases. 3, dairy products most kidney stones are about 90% calcium and oxalates. Because dairy products are high in calcium, doctors used to advise all kidney stone patients to eat less of these foods. But recent studies have shown that eating dairy products can actually reduce the risk of kidney stones, because calcium helps the body get rid of other substances that can easily form stones. 4, honey adhere to take 30 to 40 grams of honey every day. Honey contains various trace elements needed by the human body, and is easily absorbed, and daily use can provide all kinds of nutrition and energy needed by the human body. Put the pot on the fire to boil, simmer for 30 minutes into porridge. Serve as a staple food 3 times a day. It can tonify lung and kidney and remove stones. 6, the work of the seed porridge: 50 grams of seed, 150 grams of rice, 30 grams of sugar.
