Law wordpress template is perfect for a lawyer’s blog or lawyer firm website. It’s also perfect for a judge’s blog or anyone working in the law field. Overall this is a simple two column wordpress template which supports sidebar widgets. Header graphich of lawyer wordpress theme shows a judge’s desk and chair with american flag in the background. Main content area is white and sidebar is located on the right side. Law wordpress theme was designed by cryminallawyergroup.com Important Note: Please make sure you enable your wordpress widgets – otherwize the theme will not display properly. You can find the widgets by loging into your wordpress admin area, then click on the Appearance tab on the left sidebar, then select Widgets. From there you have to click the “Add” link next to each component you want to see on your sidebar.
