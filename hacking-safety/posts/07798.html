EAST TO WEST Della Terra travel trailer 300DB highlights:

Dual Entry Fireplace Double-Size Bunks Large Slide Bath and a Half 19' Power Awning

Load up the family, clothes, gear and food, and head out for a fun long-weekend at your local state park! As you easily load through the dual entry doors, you will find lots of storage throughout in the dual wardrobes in the private bedroom and bunkroom, underneath the booth dinette, within the overhead cabinets, plus the exterior pass-through storage allows you to bring along larger items and outdoor games and gear. You will sleep like a royalty in your private bedroom up front while the rest of the family enjoys the double-size bunks, a jack knife sofa, and even the dinette once easily setup for nighttime. You'll enjoy several more conveniences such as a full and half bath, a fireplace underneath the entertainment center, and loads of storage both inside and out. Each Della Terra travel trailer by EAST TO WEST is constructed with an enclosed and heated underbelly and enclosed holding tanks so you can camp all year long. There is also a power tongue jack with an LED light for easy set up and a power awning with LED lights you can rest under after you've unpacked. The Explore Comfort Package comes with marine grade woven PVC flooring in the slide outs with no carpet, an 81" interior ceiling height, upgraded Congoleum designer linoleum flooring, and many more comforts.
