On the other hand, the Denver Nuggets have proven they’re the better overall team, rocking a 5-2 record against the spread (ATS) in their last seven games overall, which includes a blemish due to the loss on Sunday. Now, let’s talk about the Nuggets’ dynamic duo: Nikola Jokic and Jamal Murray. Jokic went off for 41 points in Game 2, while Murray dished out 10 assists. But the Heat’s hot shooting isn’t sustainable, and Denver’s defense is bound to tighten up.
