Genius shoe brand Mime et moi is every brides’ dream come true. These innovative and fashionable, height adjustable shoes will see you go from high heels to flats in seconds with a simple heel change. From an elegant kitten heel at the ceremony to a chic stiletto at the reception to a stylish flat to dance the night away, these versatile shoes are perfect for your big day! Mime et moi has an extensive selection of wedding styles in a range of colours including silver, gold, dusty rose, white and nude. The shoes can be combined with seven heel shapes including a stiletto, block or superblock in high, mid and flat styles. The shoes of the German brand are manufactured in a family-owned factory in Spain with a sense of beauty, comfort, and functionality. The masters of the trade work with great precision and attention to detail according to the rules of traditional shoe making. Some production steps are carried out with delicate handwork while others use modern technology. Your Mime heels come in a specially designed Mime et moi bag to fit discretely into all types of handbags.
