Fantastic Flowery wordpress theme is a dual skin (or actually tripple). There are three icons in the top right corner and by clicking on them any visitor can change the design’s color. Available colors are gray, green and brown. Below the switch/icons is the search function. Pages links are in the header. Main content area is on the left side (black text on white background). The sidebar supports wordpress widgets (don’t forget to enable WP widgets in the admin). The main background is full of artistic shapes.
