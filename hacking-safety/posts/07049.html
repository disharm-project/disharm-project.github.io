Redline Hockey Academy Inc FEI/EIN 85-2512406 Jason Shoats President, Tara Hollen VP

Dear Business Owner: Let me take this opportunity to introduce to you, REDLINE HOCKEY ACADEMY. We are a Non Profit newly established hockey school from south Florida area, strongly in support of the development of all youth hockey players that strive to excellence, as well as encouraging positive involvement in society. Our goal is to provide an instructive environment to ALL kids, that otherwise couldn’t afford the ice time or equipment, as well as forming a competitive team that enjoys success locally and regionally. We are offering an opportunity to local businesses like yours to become Team Sponsors. Your contributions will be targeted exclusively to lessen the growing costs of the team, enabling all members to fully participate in team training programs, Redline Hockey Academy clinics and tournaments for every kid that otherwise wouldn’t be able to afford to be on the ice. Redline Hockey Academy is guided by a team of volunteer coaches, that dedicate their time to make sure every kid has an opportunity to learn the skills to further their training , and become productive members of the community. Our primary revenue source is registration fees paid by families and donations received from generous individuals and businesses. Since these funds do not cover all of teams operating expenses , our players families often must raise their own funds to pay for equipment , training fees, travel, and tournament entry fees. The average cost per player per year to participate in all activities is $3500 particularly difficult for families with more then one player and or modest means. In order to continue to make this rewarding experience for our youth , we are reaching out directly to area businesses, family, and friends, and offering the opportunity to help by becoming a team or club sponsor. While any sponsorship level you select will be greatly appreciate , a donation of $500 or more will earn you a proud team sponsor plaque with a photo of the team to display on your wall, a valuable marketing tool as well. Please take a moment to review and select the sponsorship package that best fits your business and budget. Respectfully yours, Tara Hollen Team manager REDLINEHOCKEYACADEMY.COM redlinehockeyacademy@gmail.com
