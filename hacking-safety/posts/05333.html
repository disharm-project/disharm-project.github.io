(Photo by Daniel Prentice) DANIEL PRENTICE Rav­aged by the pas­sage of Hur­ri­cane Beryl re­cent­ly, Grena­da is set to re­ceive much-need­ed help from the Women’s War­riors Well­ness Foot­ball Com­pe­ti­tion, which kicked off on Sat­ur­day with the host De­fence Force edg­ing the Grena­di­an women’s team 2-1 at the Hase­ly Craw­ford Sta­di­um in Mu­cu­rapo, Port-of-Spain. It was the sec­ond of two match­es on the day, and it gen­er­at­ed hun­dreds of sup­port­ers, all bring­ing canned items to help their re­gion­al neigh­bours in this time of need. The Grena­di­ans, un­daunt­ed by the dis­as­ter, still dis­played a de­sire to win the con­test with Ron­niel­la Bubb giv­ing her team the ad­van­tage in the 21st minute. Hav­ing seen their men’s team pull off a grit­ty dis­play to hold the So­ca War­riors to a 2-2 tie in the World Cup Qual­i­fiers in June at the same venue, the Grena­di­an women tried to em­u­late that by tak­ing the lead. How­ev­er, the De­fence Force, led by sev­er­al na­tion­al play­ers in their ranks, got the equalis­er in the 30th minute from the boot of Aaliyah Prince. The goal sent the teams to the break with the score at one apiece and it led to a bat­tle in the sec­ond half which was of tremen­dous en­ter­tain­ment ben­e­fit to the fans that made their way to the fa­cil­i­ty. Five min­utes af­ter the restart, how­ev­er, the Army Coast Guard women got a sec­ond item that turned out to be the win­ner. Lau­rel Theodore, an­oth­er na­tion­al play­er in the De­fence Force ranks, got the in­sur­ance goal in the 50th minute to send her team off to a per­fect start in Group A of the com­pe­ti­tion. The oth­er team in that group is Play­fit Acad­e­my who was sched­uled to be­gin their quest for the ti­tle against the Grena­di­ans in the first game of a dou­ble­head­er last evening at the same venue. In the sec­ond match, Group B cam­paign­ers Queen’s Park Crick­et Club’s Women’s foot­ball team was to face the T&T Foot­ball As­so­ci­a­tion’s High-Per­for­mance team. Al­so Sat­ur­day, Club San­do WFC got a hat-trick from Chelsea Ralph in a con­vinc­ing 4-0 vic­to­ry over the High-Per­for­mance team. Ralph opened the scor­ing in the 16th minute and Kha­didra Debe­sette gave them a two-goal cush­ion from the penal­ty spot in the 23rd minute. The goals opened up the flood­gates ear­ly, as Ralph then helped her­self with two more goals in the 71st and 73rd minute to com­plete their dom­i­nance. Yes­ter­day, Devin El­cock, one of the or­gan­is­ers of the ini­tia­tive said he was pleas­ant­ly sur­prised by the de­cent turn-out in such a short space of time in putting on the event. “A lot of peo­ple brought canned items to help the Grem­na­di­ans get the re­lief that it needs. I was hop­ing for a bet­ter turn-out yes­ter­day and for the com­ing match­es, as the coun­try comes out in sup­port of their re­gion­al neigh­bour,” El­cock said. On the field, how­ev­er, El­cock said he was al­so shocked to see the qual­i­ty of foot­ball that the Grena­di­ans dis­played which was much high­er than what we see in the Women’s Foot­ball League of T&T (WoLF).
