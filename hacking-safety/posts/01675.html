The Alberta-based Maxwell Realty was founded in Calgary in 1999 by Realtors® Ron Stanners and Dick Oakes. Ron Stanners, broker at MaxWell South Star, and Ron Oaks, broker at MaxWell Canyon Creek, have built MaxWell Realty into a major regional real estate player. Over the past 14 years, MaxWell Realty franchises have sprouted all over Alberta and British Columbia with several offices in Calgary including MaxWell Canyon Creek, and MaxWell South Star, as well as, MaxWell Realty Edmonton, MaxWell Realty Group in Lethbridge, MaxWell Mountain Realty in Canmore, MaxWell Canyon Creek in Okotoks and MaxWell Realty Invermere to name a few. MaxWell Realty has a built a reputation for providing exceptional real estate service, really caring about its customers and giving back to the community. MaxWell Realty is a huge supporter of Kids Help Phone with agents giving a portion of each real estate deal to the organization. Since 2003 MaxWell Realtors® have donated over $306,000 to Kids Help Phone, Alberta Region. For home buyers and sellers looking for a major local real estate company that promotes great customer service and integrity consider looking in your own backyard with MaxWell Realty. MaxWell Realty
