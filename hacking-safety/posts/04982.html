By Kentucky For Kentucky | June 01, 2016

Get ready Lexington, the NoLi CDC's Night Market is back! We're taking it to the streets from 6-10 p.m. this Friday, June 3rd, along the 700 block of Bryan Ave. in Lexington for the ultimate block party. Hell yeah! It's an epic Northside evening featuring live local music, delicious craft beer from West Sixth Brewing, cool local vendors (like us), food trucks, 5,000 kick-ass Kentuckians (and a few tourists), fresh air, hip outdoor lighting (makes for great Instagram photos) and live Tinder-ing (love). We're a presenting sponsor, along with our friends at West Sixth Brewing, which means we'll have the most badass Kentucky for Kentucky Pop-Up-Shop you've ever seen with fresh new gear and favorites like Y'ALL gear, Bourbon gear, Southern Socks and more. We're also opening up the store for extended hours on Friday night so y'all can check out even more cool stuff. Free Y'ALL koozie with every purchase! Come thirsty, hungry or naked … The Night Market will have you covered! WHAT: NOLI NIGHT MARKET WHEN: 6-10 p.m. FRIDAY, JUNE 3 WHERE: 700 BRYAN AVENUE, LEXINGTON, KY 40505
