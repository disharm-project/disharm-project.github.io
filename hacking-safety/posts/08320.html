[The Kit Inbox] KNOW: BLACK FRIDAY CYBER REPORT For the first time, digital sales passed the $2 billion mark this Friday, reaching $2.4 billion in sales, up 25 per cent from last year’s $1.8 billion. This year saw new trends as mobile use doubled as a way for consumers to shop, transactions spiked at 11a.m. compared to 6p.m. over the past years. November 11, also known as Singles Day in China, saw American’s spending $1.29 billion, one of the reasons being given for why Black Friday sales came in below predictions. [WWD] KNOW: BLACK FRIDAY BREAKDOWN The National Retail Federation released a report on this weekend’s spending trends. Traffic dropped 5.2 per cent to 133.7 million unique holiday shoppers from 141.1 million in 2013, though 55 per cent of holiday shoppers still hit the road or went to their computers to buy. On average, consumers spent $380.95, down 6.4 percent from $407.02 last year. A strong economy, highly competitive environment, early promotions and 24/7 access to online shopping were listed as reasons for the downturn.
