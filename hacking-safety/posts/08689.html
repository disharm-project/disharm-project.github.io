The suspect fled Wheelwright Drive in the stolen Mini Cooper The latest Huddersfield news and updates straight to your inbox We have more newsletters The latest Huddersfield news and updates straight to your inbox We have more newsletters A young woman was subjected to a terrifying car-jacking in Dewsbury last night. And in a brave attempt to prevent the thief from driving off in her Mini Cooper she desperately clung to the side of her car and was dragged a short distance before falling into the road. The drama unfolded seconds earlier when the 22-year-old woman was driving along Halifax Road at around 10pm and pulled over in a side street to answer her mobile phone. But as she stopped in Wheelwright Drive, near the former Birkdale High School, she was immediately confronted by a man who opened her car door and dragged her from the vehicle. She struggled with the man who then got in her car and drove off. The victim suffered bruising to her head and arms and phoned police. Det Insp Ian Thornes, of Huddersfield CID, said: “This was clearly a distressing incident for this young woman. She has taken the appropriate steps to stop her vehicle to take a call on her mobile phone and pulled in to a side road. “As she has done so she has been approached by a man and forced out of her vehicle. “A struggle broke out between the pair but the suspect drove off in her vehicle. “She managed to alert police and suffered minor injuries in the struggle but did not require hospital treatment.” The suspect was white and wore a red baseball cap, plain grey hooded top with white cord ties with the hood up and grey jogging bottoms. Mr Thornes added: “We are keeping an open mind. The road is secluded and it may well have been an opportunistic crime.” The stolen car has since been recovered. It was not known whether it was damaged. Anyone with information should contact Kirklees CID on 101.
