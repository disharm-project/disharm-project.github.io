What are your pets’ names? Bailey and Maxx What is the breed of your pet? Greyhound/Lab/Shepherd Mix & Silver Lab How were your pets’ names chosen? Bailey was easy it rolled off my tongue easy when she was naughty and Maxx was the only name we could agree upon! What is a fun or quirky fact about your pets? Bailey loves riding in the car with her head out the window sucking wind and Maxx just loves annoying Bailey as much as he can! Both are “Give Paw” masters! Do you have a favorite walking route, etc.? What is it and why? We enjoy the boardwalk downtown! And Wrightsville Beach when it’s allowed! Do your pets have a special talent? If so, what is it? Maxx just learnt to swim (on accident as he dove headfirst in to a pool, quickly followed by his momma) and Bailey has a way of making her poop disappear. We are pretty sure she is eating it but we still love her none the less! If your pets were a famous human, who would they be? Bailey has to be Snow White because she is a princess and Maxx would be Hannibal as he is still in the biting phase which we hope ends soon! Submitted by Alycia Marshall
