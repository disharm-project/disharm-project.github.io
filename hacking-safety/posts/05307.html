Less than three weeks after Poor Things took four Oscars, including best actress for Emma Stone, Kinds of Kindness Trailer for their new film has been released. According to its official synopsis, Kinds of Kindness, which shot in New Orleans in late 2022, is a “triptych fable, following a man without choice who tries to take control of his own life; a policeman who is alarmed that his wife who was missing-at-sea has returned and seems a different person; and a woman determined to find a specific someone with a special ability, who is destined to become a prodigious spiritual leader.” You May Also Like: The Penguin Trailer: Teases Next Chapter in Matt Reeves Lanthimos’s latest film also stars Margaret Qualley, Jesse Plemons and Poor Things’s Willem Dafoe. In a style reminiscent of the Coen brothers, the film features Willem Dafoe and Margaret Qualley, who both starred in “Poor Things,” alongside Joe Alwyn, known for his role as Stone’s husband in Yorgos Lanthimos’s 2018 hit “The Favourite.” You May Also Like: ‘Bad Boys 4’ trailer: Will Smith, Martin Lawrence return Lanthimos and Stone collaborated previously on the 2022 silent short film “Bleat.” During her speech on March 10th, Stone expressed her gratitude to Lanthimos, acknowledging him for “the gift of a lifetime in Bella Baxter. I am forever thankful for you.” The ensemble cast also includes Jesse Plemons, Hong Chau, Mamoudou Athie, and Hunter Schafer. Lanthimos co-wrote the film with Efthimis Filippou, with whom he had previously collaborated on acclaimed projects like “The Lobster,” “Killing of a Sacred Deer,” and “Dogtooth.” The film is set for release on 21 June in the US and 28 June in the UK.
