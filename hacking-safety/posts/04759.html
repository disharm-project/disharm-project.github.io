The Fall 2015 palette is rooted in multi-faceted, androgynous colors that can be worn to portray effortless sophistication across men’s and women’s fashion; it is the first time we are seeing a truly unisex color palette. This season displays an umbrella of accord that weaves earthy neutrals with a range of bold color statements and patterns to reflect a landscape of hope, fun, fantasy and all things natural. The colors are evocative of a love for nature and a timeless appreciation for warmth and security, which are conveyed through naturally inspired colors that remind us of things that are real and protective. Moodboard Fall / Winter Color 2015: the importance of neutrals continues to evolve with Desert Sage, reminiscent of the sky on a gray, overcast day, comes Stormy Weather. Just as the sun comes out after stormy weather to bring us cheer and a glimmer of hope, Oak Buff is a mellow. An olive green once thought of as strictly safari or military, Dried Herb, is the solution. Marsala is a winey red-brown that adds finesse and savoir faire to the palette. Biscay Bay: a lush and elegant teal. Moodboard Fall / Winter Color 2015: a nod to the ‘60s and ‘70s, Cadmium Orange evokes a sentiment of optimism, fun and fantasy. A play on the ‘60s with a twist of today, luxurious Cashmere Rose is a tactile and soft pink hue that renders exactly what it promises. Thoughtful, contemplative and composed, Reflecting Pond is a cooling blue that adds dimension and intrigue to the Top 10. Amethyst Orchid: intriguing, vibrant and somewhat sensual, this enigmatic shade is an extraordinary hue that is unique, bold, creative and exciting.
