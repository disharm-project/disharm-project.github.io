Pushbullet

softrare.space · https://softrare.space/en/windows-en/pushbullet/

1 of 11 sentences flagged

← Previous All posts Random post Next →

Pushbullet is a simple program for Windows that provides support for quick transfers between your personal computer and an iPhone or Android smartphone. Basically, it facilitates instant data exchanges across the connected devices, as well as sending messages and URL links. There is also support for mirroring phone notifications to the desktop. The main purpose of this software solution is to send images, documents, archives, multimedia or any other items from your PC to your mobile device and vice versa. Thus, you can link your Android or iPhone over the Internet, and transfer files or messages. It carries out the tasks as long as the app is installed on both your computer and the smartphone. If you are the kind of person to spend too much time in front of your stationary PC, you are able to link your phone and receive all the notifications on your desktop. It is possible to view SMS, alarms, reminders, notes, todo lists, updates, or anything that sends a warning to your mobile device. Additionally, you have the option to write messages to any contact in your phonebook. All you need to do is select the desired number, type what you want and hit send. If you would like to route mail in bulk, you may try a special utility called DRPU Bulk SMS.
