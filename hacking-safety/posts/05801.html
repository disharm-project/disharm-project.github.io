Arctic Leaf is excited to announce that we are now a Buy with Prime partner, an offering that helps e-commerce businesses align their online store with the trusted name of Prime! This will enable our clients to simplify their fulfillment to Amazon Prime customers. Buy with Prime empowers merchants to grow their e-commerce business on their own sites. They can attract Amazon shoppers with the trust of Prime and increase shopper conversions by an average of 25%* with fast, free delivery, transparent delivery times, and a checkout experience that millions of shoppers love. With Buy with Prime, merchants receive shopper information, including order and contact details, which they can use to provide customer service and nurture relationships with customers to build their e-commerce business.
