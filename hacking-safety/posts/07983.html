Ever since they first entered our homes in 2011, Property Brothers stars Drew and Jonathan Scott have become icons of the home renovation space, with fans flocking to the brothers for inspiration on how to design and renovate their own homes. Now, it just became easier than ever to bring some of that Property Brothers flair home, thanks to their new line, Scott Living by Drew & Jonathan, found exclusively at Wayfair. This line includes furniture, mattresses, rugs, window treatments, wallpaper, textiles, bedding, and more, making it one of the most extensive launches we’ve seen in a while (not that we’d expect anything less from the brothers). “Scott Living is designed for real life,” Drew said. “We designed these pieces with the function you need, the look you want, and the durability you expect.” Our favorite part? Every piece in the collection is also backed by the brothers’ 30-day Love It Guarantee, a no-questions-asked return policy based on Drew and Jonathan’s commitment to ensuring that everyone is happy with anything they buy for their home. As expected, the line has been flying off shelves since it launched, with many pieces already back-ordered until next year! But don’t worry — there are still tons of great pieces you can shop right now. Below, we rounded up 10 pieces from Scott Living that we’re loving and we think you’ll love, too. Be sure to get your hands on them before they sell out! With clean, modern lines and eye-catching tufting, the Cypress Sofa is a stunner. The perfect blend of classic and contemporary, this sofa features pocket coils and high-density foam seating for a lounging experience that's as comfortable as it is stylish. And if you like the look of the matching ottoman, it also comes in a set. With the Wilshire Bar Cabinet, cocktail hour never looked better. Complete with bottle storage, stem holders, and ample shelving, this piece will have you hosting a relaxed soiree in no time, or, if you prefer, give you the perfect night in. Looking for a fun headboard? Go for velvet with the Hawthorn Panel Headboard. Featuring both a headboard and matching bench, this set screams glamour with its rich emerald velvet upholstery and vertical channel stitching. The easiest way to instantly transform a space is with a great rug — and what's more fun than a gorgeous geometric rug? With a modern, tile-inspired design, this low pile rug offers sumptuous softness and rich colors with the dependable durability needed for busy households, making it a stylish choice for high-traffic areas. We love a good mid-century modern-inspired piece, and the Hawthorn Sideboard is just that. Perfect for everywhere from entryways to living spaces, this sideboard will add a warm, rustic MCM feel to any room while offering the chicest form of storage. For the most part, bookcases aren't shown the love they deserve — that's why we're loving the stylish design of the Brentwood Bookcase. This piece offers four shelves plus a double-door cabinet (complete with black leather loop pull handles) for a fresh, modern display for everything from your favorite reads to your beloved tchotchkes. Look! At! This! Color! The Urbana Accent Chest is the definition of eye-catching thanks to its cool blue hue and funky open-square knobs. With three stacked drawers and lovely gold accents, this is one piece we can see turning into a beloved family heirloom. Tired of the same old greys and wood tones when it comes to beds? Bring a pop of color into your space with the Brentwood Upholstered Platform Bed. Covered in a fun royal blue velvet, this low profile bed features plush vertical channeling, a slat roll support system, and pine colored legs for a piece sure to make a statement. If geometric rugs are your thing, this is further proof that Scott Living is your place to go. This gorgeous indigo rug is covered in a chic hexagon design that adds style and dimension to your space. Best of all, it's woven with an expertly crafted blend of stain-resistant, environmentally friendly recycled premium polyester and the light-catching sheen of rayon viscose, for a rug that's both stylish and sustainable. Looking for a fun, modern table? Then this funky iron side table has your name written all over it. Chic and sturdy, this contemporary gold-toned piece features a unique leg design with a tray-style top that makes it great for entertaining.
