Oat milk hot chocolate

www.healthylifetrainer.com · https://www.healthylifetrainer.com/oat-milk-hot-chocolate/

1 of 59 sentences flagged

Published: Jun 19, 2023 by Radwa. 2 Comments This creamy Oat Milk Hot Chocolate recipe is a vegan, dairy-free beverage made with simple ingredients and will be ready in less than 5 minutes. It's my best treat on a cold day. This is the drink I need when looking for some warm and sweet comfort on a rainy day. Sometimes, I double this recipe and store it in the fridge to enjoy hot chocolate for 2-3 days, fuss-free! Just a sip from this drink, and I will already feel the exploding warmth covering my cold body with that sweet delight of chocolatey goodness. This Oat Milk Hot Chocolate offers a superb taste and texture. The taste is chocolaty, rich and mildly sweet, while the texture of this drink is creamy, thick, and rich that even just with a single sip, you will love it! I prefer to have a cup of this beverage in the morning when I wake up or in the afternoon as I peruse my computer work. Alternative milk: Oat milk is my favourite dairy-free milk to make lots of vegan beverages; it is creamy and has a slightly sweet flavour. However, you may use other non-dairy milk such as coconut milk, soy milk or almond milk. Oat Milk. This non-dairy milk provides a rich texture for this recipe. You may replace it with other dairy-free milk, such as coconut milk. I prefer to use unsweetened oat milk. However, if you use sweetened almond milk, you can skip the honey in this recipe. Cocoa Powder. I use unsweetened cocoa powder so that my hot chocolate tastes chocolatey. Semi-sweet Chocolate. I use semi-sweet chocolate because it is less bitter than dark chocolate. Honey. A sweetener, use maple syrup in case honey is unavailable; both will work fine. Pinch of Salt. Salt serves as a balance between the different flavors in this recipe. Marshmallows and Chocolate Sauce. Soft marshmallows and sweet chocolate sauce: perfect toppings for your hot chocolate, I used vegan products. “See the recipe card for full information on ingredients and quantities.” One: In a saucepan, whisk oat milk, cocoa powder, vanilla extract, salt and honey over medium heat for 2-3 minutes, until combined and smooth. Two: Once the mixture starts to bubble, add the chocolate and whisk until fully melted. Three: Turn off the heat and pour the hot chocolate in cups, garnish with marshmallows and a drizzle of chocolate sauce, then serve. I like to serve this oat milk hot chocolate with chocolate oatmeal cookies! Aside from that, a slice of chocolate biscuit cake is also an excellent choice to serve with this drink. You may also enjoy drinking this hot chocolate with Oreo cheesecake bites since its indulgence and the creamy texture and unique flavor goes well with hot chocolate. Or serve with Victoria Scones, Cookies and Cream Cinnamon Rolls or Pumpkin Pancakes. Yes! Only if you used vegan marshmallows and chocolate! Absolutely. White chocolate is still chocolate, isn't it?
