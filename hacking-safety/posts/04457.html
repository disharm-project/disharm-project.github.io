The event motto connects the existential themes of farewell, displacement and flight in the past and present. In the process, the thread runs from Heartfield’s exile to that of other displaced artists. Video artists and filmmakers Marcel Odenbach and Gavin Hodge, composer Helmut Oehring, Angela Lammert and Michael Krejsa will talk about old and new conflicts of flight and places of retreat. 3 pm John-Heartfield-HausSchwarzer Weg 1215377 Waldsieversdorf Film, Talk & Music With Marcel Odenbach, Helmut Oehring, Gavin Hodge, Angela Lammert and Michael Krejsa Music: Adumá-Saxophonquartett In German Free admission Further information Health and Safety Regulations
