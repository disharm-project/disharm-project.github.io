Burnaby is the third largest city in BC by its population surpassed only by Vancouver and Surrey. With a population of 232,995 as of 2016. It is situated just east of Vancouver. It consists of thirteen urban villages and four town centres – Metrotown, Edmonds, Lougheed, and Brentwood. Schools – School District #41 operates K-12 schools, international education, community schools & adult education. Simon Fraser University and the British Columbia Institute of Technology. Transportation – Translink Buses, the Expo Line and the Millennium Line. Metropolis at Metrotown is the largest mall in BC, with high end shopping, Parks & Recreation – include Central Park, Robert Burnaby Park, Kensington Park, Burnaby Mountain, Still Creek, the Brunette River, Burnaby Lake, and Deer Lake. Burnaby Mountain Golf Course & Driving Range – Great meeting place and an excellent restaurant as well. The Shadbolt Centre for the Arts – is a venue for both performance and teaching in the arts. Single-family houses for sale are mostly in West Newton and North Burnaby. There are condos, single-family homes, townhouses, detached and semi-detached family homes in most areas. Burnaby is snowballing with its proximity to Vancouver. There are homes for everyone, whether you are a young family, single or a retiree, there is a home for you.
