To read the full description, click here. ____________________________________________ Position Available: Social Work Internship Department: International Community Health Center Location: Akron/Cleveland, Ohio SUMMARY Asian Services In Action, Inc.’s International Community Health Center (ASIA-ICHC) is a federally qualified health center that provides services to all regardless of inability to pay. ASIA-ICHC predominantly serves low-income, Limited English Proficient (LEP) immigrant and refugee populations. ASIA-ICHC provides opportunities for social workers to work with underserved populations by assisting the organization’s Mental Health/Behavioral Health program. A Licensed Independent Social Worker (LISW-S) provides qualified supervision and guidance to interns who work in the Behavioral Health/Mental Health program. The intern’s work schedule can be flexible in order to avoid any conflicts with their current study. DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES Following an initial period of training and shadowing opportunities, the intern will participate in all core clinical services offered through the Mental Health/Behavioral Health program at ASIA-ICHC. These duties will be tailored to the developmental level and training interests of individual students and may include individual therapy, group therapy, case management, policy advocacy, community engagement, needs assessment, and psycho-education. QUALIFICATIONS The position reports to the Licensed Independent Social Worker Supervisor. We are seeking a candidate who has strong technical and soft skills, high energy, maturity and potential for leadership. APPLY/CONTACT Please send a cover letter and resume to HRManager@asiaohio.org and title the email subject line as “ICHC Social Work Internship: [Your Name Here]”. Visit apexfund.wpengine.com for more information. No phone calls, please. Asian Services In Action, Inc. promotes equal employment opportunities for all applicants and employees. ASIA, Inc. will not discriminate against any applicant for employment on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, veteran status, age, sexual orientation, marital status, or the presence of any sensory, mental or physical disability in any employment practice, unless based on a bona fide occupational qualification. Women, minorities, and disabled are encouraged to apply. Apex Fund is a microenterprise lending program meant to promote small business startups in Northeast Ohio. 330-391-7446
