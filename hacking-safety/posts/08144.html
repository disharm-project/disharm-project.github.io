July 21, 2024 When it comes to documenting the earliest days of your baby’s life, choosing the right photographer is essential. As a Wexford newborn photographer, we had the absolute pleasure of capturing baby Cecelia’s newborn session, creating timeless memories for her family to cherish. For Cecelia’s session, we used some of our favorite muted color palettes, incorporating whites and creams to create a classic and timeless look. We also added touches of pinks and purples, bringing a subtle yet beautiful pop of color to the photos. We always love adding these colors to our collection of favorite props with earthy tones and textures, including some from nearby Kensington Court Antiques. It was an honor to photograph Cecelia, especially since we had previously taken her older brother’s newborn photos. Seeing families grow and being a part of their journey is one the best parts of our job! This time around, we were able to capture the special bond between Cecelia and her older brother, as well as the whole family together. It’s hard for us to believe how fast our past newborn clients have grown up! Selecting a newborn photographer is a significant decision. You want someone who not only has beautiful photos but also a comfortable and safe environment for your baby. As a Wexford newborn photographer, we pride ourselves on providing a warm and welcoming atmosphere for both the newborn and their family. We aim to capture the genuine emotions and connections that make each family unique. Our studio, conveniently located in Wexford, offers the perfect setting for newborn photography. With a range of props and backdrops, we can create a variety of looks to suit your style. The peaceful and comfortable space ensure that both parents and babies feel at ease during the session. Worried that your little one won’t sleep? Check out this post. As seasoned newborn photographers with over a decade of experience, we understand the importance of these early moments. And of course, each session is tailored to your family’s needs. From the initial questionnaire to the final edited images, our commitment is to provide you with a seamless and enjoyable experience. If you are looking for a Wexford newborn photographer to document your baby’s first days, we would be honored to be a part of your journey. Let’s create beautiful, lasting memories together.
