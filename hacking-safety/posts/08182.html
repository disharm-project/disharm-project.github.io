Education Related Topics Bible Discussion Topics for Groups: Religion is part of our life. It is something that has been around for ages. There are times you look for bible study topics or bible discussion questions. The bible has so many debate topics. If you are searching for Bible Discussion Topics for Groups or Bible Debate Topics then you are in the right place. This page will provide you with a list of Christian debate topics that you will find useful when searching for something to debate about. Find a collection of Bible Discussion Topics for Groups. Contents Can you sin and still make it to heaven? The Godhead What is original sin? Recreation How to know God’s will for your life? What put(killed) Jesus on the cross, was it sin, or was it God, or another reason? Practical lessons of life that can be learnt from the sermon on the mount What is the right balance between understanding the importance of faith and works? The Second coming of Christ Nature of Christ True sanctification Should we as Christians fight the fight of faith or fight the fight of sin(Romans 7)? The purpose of the Holy Spirit What is the seal of God vs Mark of the beast? Did Jesus experience God’s wrath on the cross? Last Day events Sermon on the mount Did Christ come to the world to deal with God’s wrath on man because of their sin, or did Christ come to the world to take away sin, or Another better reason? Lessons we can learn from the old testament tabernacle/sanctuary Considering that there are a vast amount of christian denominations, does it matter what denomination I belong to? Jesus said, “And this gospel of the kingdom shall be preached unto all the world for a witness, and then shall the end come”. Which kingdom is being spoken of here? Relationships Understanding bible prophecies What is this mystery of which Paul writes to the Ephesians and to the Colossians? What’s the best bible interpretation to use? Did Jesus commit suicide when he died on the cross? What has Jesus been doing since he ascended back into heaven? Did Jesus resurrect himself? Who is Jesus? Did the system of animal sacrifices, circumcision of the body, feasts provide salvation? Did a person have to participate in that system to be saved? God’s Love Original Sin Did Christ possess the nature of Adam after the fall or before the fall? What does the bible teach about good health? Is it possible to talk to the dead? The Covenants in the bible Same Sex Love? Is Oral Sex a Sin? Steve George is Blogger, a marketer and content writer. He has B.A. in Economics from the University of Washington.
