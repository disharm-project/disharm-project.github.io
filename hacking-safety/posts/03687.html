Rhonj jackie goldschneider ends beef with teresa giudice

tvshowsace.com · https://tvshowsace.com/2023/10/28/rhonj-jackie-goldschneider-ends-beef-with-teresa-giudice/

1 of 48 sentences flagged

After each doing some growing up, it appears that Jackie Goldschneider and Teresa Giudice have ended their beef with a blossoming friendship. If Real Housewives is known for anything, it is the drama. and Real Housewives Of New Jersey is certainly no exception. The popular Bravo show has provided countless hours of fiery entertainment with no-holds-barred fights and backstabbings. One of Real Housewives Of New Jersey’s most infamous moments was a beef between Jackie Goldschneider and Teresa Giudice in Season 9. With nothing more than an unsubstantiated rumor, Giudice accused Goldschneider’s husband of cheating on her. Understandably, tempers flared, and the two women found themselves at odds. Eventually, Giudice would offer something of an apology to Goldschneider. “Sorry about like, you know, like everything that went down, and I never want to do anything to hurt you guys in any way,” Giudice said in her apology. Even with the apology, the two remained distant afterward. Despite their contested past, it appears that the two have buried the hatchet. After being a core cast mate on Real Housewives Of New Jersey for four seasons, Goldschneider’s role was seemingly reduced out of the blue. This sparked speculation about the reasons behind what appeared to be a demotion. However, in a December 2022 interview with People, Goldschneider opened up about her recovery from an eating disorder. “When Bravo approached me about casting for Season 13, I obviously was very excited to be a part of the show again because I love the show,” Jackie said. “But my recovery was not in the place that I expected it to be. It was moving very slowly. I was still being triggered by stress and I still needed a lot of intense therapy.” Thank you for your courage in sharing your story, @JGSchneid. After taking time to focus on herself, Goldschneider says that her entire outlook has shifted. For her, that even includes letting go of her beef with Giudice. A move that signaled growth in both women. In an interview with Hello, Goldschneider talked about that growth. “We both grew up. I think our lives changed a lot in the past three years and we found ourselves at this point as different people, and we were drawn to each other,” she told Hello. “Once we opened ourselves up to that friendship, we really like each other.” “I mean, I just got off the phone with her before I picked up the phone to call you. I’m very open now, my mindset has changed. Therapy has been life-changing for me and not just from a food standpoint but for self-esteem. I used to do a lot of things because I felt like that would keep my friends happy and would make them like me, and I’m a completely different person now. If somebody doesn’t like me for being who I am then they can take a hike. Once I stopped worrying about that. I was able to back away from unhealthy relationships and really fill my life with people who made me feel good.” Earlier this fall, Goldschneider’s book The Weight Of Beautiful made its debut. The book detailed her decades-long battle with anorexia and her struggle with maintaining her public persona. In a move that signaled a blossoming friendship between the two women, Giudice took to social media to offer her support for Goldschneider. In a September 26 story added to Instagram, Guidice took a picture of Goldschneider’s book The Weight Of Beautiful. Giudice then included the caption, “So happy for you @jackiegoldschneider. Can’t wait to read it.” Giudice then posted a short clip of her inside a car with her daughter, Gia. Speaking to the camera, she offered congratulations to Goldschneider. “Jackie I’ve come to see you in Ridgewood. I’m here with Gia. Congratulations.
