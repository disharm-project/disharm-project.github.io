Bandcamp: September 13, 2013 Substack / LABELabel: July 31, 2023 This is another album that stands as a memorial to a lone dead app. Thicket was a fun, touch-based noise maker with a number of different modes. I was beta testing an updated version with more modes, which I explored in depth on this album. There is a “classic” version of the app available on the app store, but it’s not really the same. This album is full of haunting minimal noise. Abstract electronic ambient noise lovingly crafted on my iPads for your mental viewing displeasure. Composed and produced by Qid Love. For just $3 a month, you can own and download my entire iOS-produced back catalog and automatically receive all new releases. Even better, you’ll receive artist albums and compilations from other members of Apptronica and the larger iOS music community as well. That’s 201 albums in your collection today, for just 3 bucks.
