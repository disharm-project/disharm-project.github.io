Location: Milpitas, California Position: Seasonal Store Associate Application Deadline: [01/09/2023] Company Overview: NIKE, Inc. transcends traditional expectations by not only outfitting the world’s finest athletes but also by fostering an environment where potential is explored, boundaries are shattered, and innovation knows no limits. We seek individuals who can embrace growth, embrace creativity, and contribute to a culture that celebrates diversity and rewards imagination. Our brand is seeking achievers, leaders, and visionaries who are willing to contribute their skills and passion to a dynamic and ever-evolving playing field. Retail at NIKE, Inc.: At NIKE, Inc., our retail teams represent the forefront of our brand, embodying our product knowledge and delivering exceptional customer service. With stores across the globe, each location showcases a unique perspective and cultivates a community of sport and style enthusiasts. A career in NIKE, Inc. retail offers an avenue for creativity and ambition, enabling growth alongside industry-leading athletes, colleagues, and retail partners. Position Details: About the Role: NIKE Milpitas is seeking a Seasonal Store Associate to join our team and contribute to delivering world-class service to our consumers. If you are passionate about fostering memorable shopping experiences and embody our core values, we welcome you to join our vibrant retail community. Key Qualities We Value: Consumer-Centric: You are friendly, a team player, and committed to providing an exceptional customer experience. Achiever: You are coachable, goal-oriented, and eager to learn. Your innovative and influential nature sets you apart. Integrity: You maintain a high level of professionalism, demonstrate integrity and reliability, and consistently uphold the right standards. Passionate Advocate: You exude passion, energy, drive, and positivity. Your authentic approach resonates with customers and colleagues alike. Responsibilities: Demonstrate a genuine passion for NIKE products and services. Learn and contribute to various store roles, including Service, Visual Presentation, and Stockroom. Provide exceptional service to all consumers, ensuring a memorable experience. Collaborate with the team to execute daily retail operations, enhancing service and store efficiency. Foster positive relationships with consumers and colleagues. Participate in and support store events, actively engaging with the store community. Display reliability and flexibility by accommodating varied work hours and days to meet business needs. Maintain knowledge of different store departments and assist with cashier duties, shipping and receiving, stocking, cleaning, and visual displays as required. Qualifications: Must be at least 18 years of age. Effective communication skills. Physical capabilities include bending, twisting, squatting, reaching, climbing ladders, and standing for extended periods. Thrives in a fast-paced environment, managing multiple tasks efficiently. Collaborative team player with exceptional customer service skills. Preferred: One or more years of customer service and/or retail experience. Flexible scheduling availability, including weekdays, weekends, evenings, and holidays. Rewards and Benefits: Joining NIKE Retail means being part of an exciting team environment. You’ll have access to the latest Nike products and apparel, along with a competitive compensation and benefits package. Our commitment extends beyond customers; we offer opportunities for professional development and a culture that champions growth. Equal Opportunity Employer: NIKE, Inc. is dedicated to fostering a diverse workforce. Applicants will receive consideration without regard to race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, sexual orientation, gender identity, gender expression, veteran status, or disability. Application Process: If you’re ready to embrace the challenges of a career in NIKE Retail and contribute to our mission of inspiring athletes worldwide, we invite you to apply for the Seasonal Store Associate role at NIKE Milpitas. Please submit your updated resume and a cover letter that highlights your relevant experience and alignment with our values. Note: Only shortlisted candidates will be contacted.
