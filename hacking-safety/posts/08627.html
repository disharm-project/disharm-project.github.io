Erectile dysfunction, commonly referred to as ED, is an unwanted health condition experienced by countless men in the Plano, Frisco and Dallas, Texas area. ED can range from the total inability to achieve an erection to a tendency to sustain an erection for a brief time. Causes of ED vary from patient to patient, with no single cause. Dr. Mark Allen often prescribes the AMS Spectra Concealable Penile Implant, a penis implant for erectile dysfunction, in patients with ongoing erectile dysfunction so they can regain their intimacy and self-esteem. While there are real physical and psychological reasons for ED, there’s no single cause. Common causes of ED include: It is commonly understood the incidence of erectile dysfunction increases with age. 5% of 40 year old men and between 15-25% of 65 year old men experience erectile dysfunction. In the older generations, erectile dysfunction usually has a physical reason, such as disease, injury or side effects of prescription medications. Also, any disorders that may cause nerve damage or impairs blood circulation have been known to cause erectile dysfunction. Yet, erectile dysfunction and age do not have to be connected in all men. Erectile dysfunction is treatable at any age and numerous men who have sought ED treatment are returning to their normal sexual activity because of successful treatment. Dr. Allen commonly prescribes the AMS Spectra Concealable Penile Implant for patients who have chronic erectile dysfunction. This is an ED treatment device that is a sterile, non-pyrogenic, single-use implant intended for use in the treatment of chronic, organic, erectile dysfunction in men who are determined to be suitable candidates for implantation surgery. The prosthesis consists of a pair of cylinders that are surgically inserted into the penis. The prosthesis is positioned up for intercourse or down for everyday activities. Once inserted into the penis, Dr. Allen will consult and coach the patient on how to function everyday with this device, and how to use it properly for sexual intercourse.
