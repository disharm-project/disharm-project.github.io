AR-15 5.56/.223 16"M4 MAGPUL MOE DEFENDER RIFLE KIT, left hand. This rifle kit contains all components to complete your AR-15 Rifle build in various colors (choose on top). The rifle kit includes left hand upper receiver, left hand BCG, and left hand lower parts kit. Comes WITHOUT 80% lower receiver. All options and modifications to your kit are available on top of this page. All components are Mil Spec and made in the USA. Barrel: 16” 4150 Chrome Moly Vanadium Chamber: 5.56MM Twist: 1 in 7 Thread: 1/2x28 .750” Gas Block Diameter Carbine Gas System Left Hand A3 upper receiver Forward Assist Ejection Port Cover A2 Flash Hider Magpul Moe 2 piece handguard LH Bolt Carrier Group AR-15 Charging Handle AR-15 Mil Spec Lower Parts Kit, left hand Magpul Moe 6-position Mil Spec Stock Barrel: 16” M4 government profile barrel, 4150 Chrome Moly Vanadium with 1:7 twist, M4 Feed Ramps, phosphate processed with a carbine gas system. Barrel is finished off with Magpul Moe 2 piece carbine length handguard, top rail picattiny gas block and A2 style flash hider (no rear sights). Upper: 7075-T6 M4 upper is flat top, precision machined and hard coat anodized. These uppers are made for us in the USA by a mil-spec manufacturer. Comes with forward assist and dust cover, complete. Tmarks on rails. Bolt Carrier Group: Left hand 5.56/.223 complete bolt carrier assembly, chrome lined, made from 8620 steel. Bolt is made from 9310 steel and stalked per mil spec. MPI Tested. Comes complete with cam pin, firing pin and firing pin retainer. Lower Build Kit: Classic AR-15 lower parts kit, left hand version, Mil-Spec. Includes everything you need to turn your 80% stripped lower into a complete lower. Stock: Magpul Moe 6-position stock, Mil-Spec. Completed with buffer tube assembly and spring. Please Note: Comes unassembled. Does not include sights and vertical grip. Standard kit fits perfectly all styles Mil Spec AR-15 lowers. Images may not be an exact representation of the product.
