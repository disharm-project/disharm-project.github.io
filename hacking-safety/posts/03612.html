But just how likely is is that we’ll see the usual summer holidays in 2020? Here's the latest travel advice: Spain and Tenerife are looking to slowly ease the nationwide lockdown in the second half of May (provided authorities stay "on top of the virus"), but travel there will only be permitted once the UK Government deems it safe to do so. The Foreign & Commonwealth Office (FCO) advises Brits against all non-essential travel worldwide.
