A salty ocean scented, DIY clay slime. The base is 4oz of thick clear, scented slime, tinted blue. There is 1 oz of white, scented slime for the beach. There are 2 pink clay conch pieces. The kit comes with pearl beads. Makes a great gift for kids and adults. Option to have slime individually wrapped for party favors is available. Just make a note in checkout. Please message for large volume of orders. Base: clear glue Activator: Borax Additive: lightweight clay, pearl beads Consistency: creamy, smooth, non-sticky. 80% slime, 20% clay Scented: Yes Size: 5oz total (4oz clear base + 1oz white glue) or 7oz (6oz clear base + 1oz white glue) The clay pieces come packaged in a separate, airtight deli container. The container can be used to store the extra slime, once it has been mixed. This slime is made to order to ensure clay freshness. Please allow 5 to 7 business days for processing. ************************************************************************** Customers living in the Regina, Saskatchewan/Canada area, there is curb side pick up available. Make this selection in checkout. ************************************************************************** Slime size is measured by volume, not by weight. Slime is packed to the fill line of the container. Due to the different densities of the different types of slime, the weight per container will vary. Slime can and will also change volume when played with. Containers are high quality plastic and come with a screw on lid. Store your slime in the container. Labels on the containers are waterproof and can easily be cleaned with some water and light rubbing. All slime orders come with Slime Care Instructions and a pre-filled activator pen. ************************************************************************** Warning Slime is not edible. Do not consume. Not suitable for children under 5 years of age. Slime may contain small parts that can be a choking hazard. Slime contains glue and Borax and may contain colorants, clay, soaps, lotions, shaving creams, metal leaf and scents.
