Here are the current offers for Tangerine credit cards: Further on, find all the details on these no annual fee credit cards. The Tangerine Money-Back Credit Card has no annual fee and offers cash back. With this limited-time welcome offer, you can earn an additional 10% cash back (up to $100) when you spend up to $1,000 on everyday purchases during the first 2 months. You can choose two to three categories that interest you (groceries, gas, furniture, home improvement, recurring bills, pharmacy, etc.) to earn 2% cash back! And there is no annual cash back limit. It is one of the best credit cards for renovations. In addition, the Tangerine Mastercard offers several insurance coverages for your purchases : Finally, during your first 30 days, transfer balances and pay only 1.95% interest on the transferred balance for the first 6 months. If you have an individual income of $60,000 or a family income of $100,000, you may want to consider the World Mastercard Tangerine. This credit card has no annual fee and offers cashback. In addition, the Tangerine World Mastercard comes with several insurance coverages for your purchases and travel : Finally, during your first 30 days, transfer balances and pay only 1.95% interest on the transferred balance for the first 6 months. With both Tangerine credit cards, you earn 2% money back on everyday purchases in up to 3 categories of your choice. They are the only ones in Canada to offer this feature! So it’s not limited to gas stations, grocery stores, drugstores, etc. like other cash back credit cards. So you could ask to earn 2% cash back on purchases at stores where you shop the most!
