Paint mixing balls made of rust-resistant stainless steel. Set of 40 Stainless steel bearing balls in 6.35mm to mix paints. These mixing balls are coming with a special coat to make them completely paint proof which will avoid the rusting process after long periods of contact with paints that may ruin the paint colors. They are not magnetic but they will be attracted by magnets. They are suitable either for personal or industrial uses in order to guarantee a proper mixture of all paint contents and therefore avoid having all pigments deposited in the bottom of the pot, especially after long-term storage. This set of Stirring balls in 6.35 mm is perfect for mixing dropper paint pots such as Green Stuff World and Citadel, among others. These ball bearings are more indicated for paint pots smaller than 40 ml The total weight of 1 ball is 1gr. Quality SS316L I use the balls to shake my old citadel paints that born without balls. These perform perfectly in paint pots. I have purchased others in the past that rusted during testing. I put these in through a rough test in saltwater.....no rusting.
