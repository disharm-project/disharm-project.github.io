To love fiercely is not about drama or martyrdom. It is not about coercion or manipulation or overpowering someone else or toxic obsession. It is not about loving someone else more than you love yourself.... what it IS about....is loving with complete abandon. What that means, is to be so completely willing to being in a state of love, you are willing to be responsible for your own well being, in all moments, regardless of the affect it has on the other person. I can't know how I will bless someone else by NOT doing what they think they want me to do! Read that again. I cant know how me saying "NO" to their request... can actually lead someone even closer to their true highest hearts desire. I cant know how me rejecting or hurting someone else can ultimately lead them into their highest good. All I can know is that if I surrender to other when I truly don't want to, in hopes they will love me more, or accept me more, or so I can receive their approval, or so they wont be angry.... ultimately is the least loving thing I can do for them. To be fierce, I must passionately honor my own well being, and practice rigorous honesty with myself. I must notice how I feel and get to know my innermost indicators of my personal well being.
