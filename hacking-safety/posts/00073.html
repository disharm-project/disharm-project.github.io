This course focuses on general CACFP recordkeeping requirements in lesson 1. In lesson 2 recordkeeping for Outside School hours. The course addresses program eligibility, operational requirements, enrollment, participant eligibility, and miscellaneous recordkeeping requirements. These online training courses are brought to you by the Virginia Department of Health (VDH). The USDA regulations for child nutrition programs require specific training for providers to begin, or continue participation. VDH is offering these online training options to meet the regulatory requirements.
