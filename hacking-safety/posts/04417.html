You get people within the congregation who project their past hurt on to you and screw you over at the first chance available, when it is never fully or truly about you, it is about their need to punish someone for someone in their lives in the past, people who they could not get to. However, it can work both ways, pastors can be bullies too. Pastors themselves can project their past hurts onto other members of the congregations, and take our their unforgiveness on others. A few years ago I went to this church’s introduction session, there was this guy who like to joke around, and yes, he was interruptive. However, the pastor could have handled it politely and assertively, by saying something like ” you are making it very hard for me by interrupting me, can you stop please” instead he said ” don’t you give me a hard time” Which was rude on his part, and I know he well enough to know he wouldn’t appreciate it if he is on the receiving end of it. Though the impression I get is that he wasn’t repentant about it. For me, in end of the day, pastor are not perfect, and we as congregation need to cut them some slacks. However, many pastors seem to take that as mean ” if I sin against you, it is because I am not perfect” in another word, I can’t and should not be made to take responsibility for it, hence I don’t have to make an effort to change, because being a pastor is too hard? No, that is not acceptable, if a pastor sinned against others, he should man up and say, ” I was wrong, I take full responsibility for it, and I am sorry. I will ask God to help me and make real effort to change, and if I do it again, please call me out on it and I will give you an apology ” if that is the pastor’s attitude, then I don’t care if he slipped again and again, because he has shown true repentance rather than just want to be left off the hook. as for your husband being attacked. All I can say is when we are being attacked we need to examine ourselves before God first to see if we are truly at fault, or at least partially at fault. and if not, then great. but we should not deflect those accusations immediately and become defensive.
