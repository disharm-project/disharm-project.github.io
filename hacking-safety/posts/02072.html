(801) 702-8475 Call Menu As we age, our bodies undergo a range of physiological changes, including hormonal fluctuations that can significantly impact our pelvic health. These changes can manifest in various ways, such as vaginal dryness, decreased tissue elasticity, altered blood flow, and a decline in pelvic muscle strength. These symptoms can often lead to discomfort, diminished sexual function, and urinary incontinence, affecting our overall well-being and quality of life. At Reborn Pelvic Health & Wellness, we understand the challenges women face as they navigate these hormonal changes.
