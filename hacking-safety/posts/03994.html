Women's specialty care Our diverse team of women’s health experts includes the largest group of OB-GYNs and the longest-standing midwife practice in the Twin Cities. When it comes to caring for yourself, knowing matters. We use the latest research and technology to get you answers quickly. With more than 20 clinic locations in Minnesota and western Wisconsin, personalized care and treatment is always close to home. Staying healthy allows you to focus on the things that matter most in life. Whether you’re working toward your dream job, dreaming of a little one or dreaming of retirement, we’re here to help you stay healthy so you can be there for all life’s moments. At HealthPartners and Park Nicollet, we specialize in women’s health. From preventive care to family planning to questions about heavy periods and menopause, we’re here to get you answers and provide the latest in research-driven treatment options. We have the largest team of women’s health doctors including OB-GYNs, midwives, maternal fetal medicine specialists and urogynecologists. Working together, we can provide you with any type of care you need at more than 20 clinics in Minnesota and western Wisconsin. We also provide care exclusively to women at the Park Nicollet Women’s Center and Jane Brattain Breast Center. At all of our locations, you’ll find the latest treatments, customized to meet your personal needs. We provide the full range of women’s health services for all of life’s stages. Whatever you need help with, we’re here for you. Our OB-GYNs are here to help you stay healthy for everything you want out of life including birth control, fertility care, family planning and menopause. Our practice of certified nurse midwives (CNMs) was one of the first in the Twin Cities. We've guided thousands of women through all stages of pregnancy and childbirth with a superior record of safety and patient satisfaction. Our team of board-certified OB-GYNs, certified nurse midwives, maternal fetal medicine specialists, nurse practitioners, lactation consultants and other specialists work together in your care so you can focus on welcoming your baby into the world. From preventive screenings to advanced diagnostic imaging, we offer exceptional treatment and guidance to support your breast health. Mammograms are the best way to get peace of mind about your breast health. We make mammograms convenient with online scheduling and same-day appointments at over 20 locations throughout the Twin Cities, the St. Cloud area and western Wisconsin. Our team of board-certified oncologists, breast surgeons, radiologists, nurse navigators, plastic surgeons and other specialists are experts in treating even the most complex cases of breast cancer at our state-of-the-art cancer centers. Our board-certified urogynecologists are experts in helping women to manage and improve conditions like incontinence and pelvic organ prolapse. When complications happen during pregnancy, our maternal-fetal medicine specialists work together to create a personalized plan for you and your baby. If you’re planning to breastfeed, we’re here with the education and support you need to make it a positive and rewarding experience for you and your baby. Our primary care doctors, OB-GYNs, certified nurse midwives and internal medicine doctors all provide expert care for everything from prevention to diagnosis to treatment. At many of our clinics, family medicine doctors, internal medicine doctors, OB-GYNs and midwives work closely together to make sure you get the care you need. This means you can choose the doctor who is the best fit for you, knowing a whole team of specialists is here to support you. To stay informed on a variety of women's health topics, sign up for our quarterly women's health e-newsletter, In Good Health. We accept most health insurance plans, including Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Minnesota, CIGNA, HealthPartners, Medica, Medicare, PreferredOne and many others. Not sure what your insurance covers?
