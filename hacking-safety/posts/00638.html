Th.D. in Missiology, Trinity College & Seminary (2016); Th.M. in Practical Theology, Korea Christian University (2015); M.Div., India Bible College & Seminary (2012), B.Th., Buntain Theological College (2003). Dr. Jeremiah is an ordained minister in association with the Trinity Missions and International Churches. He has been in the pastoral role at Presbyterian Mok Dong Chanyang Church, Soul Republic of Korea, as well as Vice Principal for the Mizoram Bible College and teaching ministry. He published Matu Chin New Testament under the Evangel Bible Translators International (2004-2023) and has begun work on the Old Testament translation project (2023-2042). He is married to H. Ramnunsangi who is also actively serving the Lord. They are blessed with a daughter, Rebecca Anry Lalrinnungi, and son, Christopher Anry Malsawmhlua.
