Choose from scandi-inspired wall murals for bedrooms to cool jungle wall mural designs, perfect for kitchens or bathrooms alike. Unlike wallpaper which has a repeating pattern, murals consist of one large design, similar to a photograph or painting. When deciding on our mural collection, our design team took inspiration from some of the biggest trends in interior design and our best-selling wallpaper designs. Our collection has been designed around the patterns you love. Edgy industrial ombre walls and rustic wood effect murals to fun bold kids wall murals for a colourful nursery. Find our favourites like a dinosaur wall mural or animal design for an eye catching statement wall. We have a look for every room, from kids wall mural options that grown-ups will adore too, through to intricate, sophisticated designs that are guaranteed to catch the eye. We digitally print all of our wall murals ourselves in the UK to ensure the print and paper is the highest quality. All of our murals are non woven which means they are quick and easy to hang by pasting the wall. This method also means it is easy to adjust as you go along and when you do decide on a change they peel off easily too.
