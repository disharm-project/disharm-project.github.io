Tomorrow's Forefathers, Inc. Equipping Families to be Strong in the Lord

Read More “Seeking Christ Conference at the Ark Encounter” Check our online store for discipleship resources, Bible study helps, recommended reading, and books encouraging families to be strong in the Lord. Read More “Store”

Read More “Testify Training Camp” Bright Lights is a discipleship ministry encouraging young ladies to Be Radiant in Godliness, Holiness, and Testimony. Read More “Bright Lights” The pattern we see in Scripture is for older women to teach younger women (Tit. 2:3-5). This can start by older girls teaching younger girls! Read More “Start a Group” It is our goal to give vision, encouragement, and training to families that they may be firmly grounded in the Word of God, be protected from the enemy’s deceptions, and be fruitful for Christ’s Kingdom. Tomorrow’s Forefathers is the “umbrella name” for our ministry. The strength of the church and the country starts in the home with godly fathers and mothers who are uncompromising in their commitments, and courageous in their testimonies for Christ. They have an awesome assignment from the Lord, and are the forefathers on which our future will depend. They need God’s wisdom and His mighty, outstretched arm of blessing. “Except the Lord build the house, they labor in vain that build it” (Psalm 127:1).
