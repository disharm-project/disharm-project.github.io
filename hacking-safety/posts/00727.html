Boost performance with a good sleep routine

by The Project Joyful Team | 21 Jul 2024 | Article | 0 comments In our fast-paced world, sleep often becomes an afterthought as we chase productivity and excellence in our daily lives. However, neglecting sleep can have dire consequences on our performance. Mild insomnia, defined by the Sleep and Healthy Activity, Diet, Environment, and Socialisation Study (SHADES) as getting less than 5 hours of sleep regularly, can lead to a staggering 58% drop in productivity. Moreover, such sleep deprivation not only affects work output but also poses significant health risks including heart disease and diabetes. To combat these issues, aim for restorative sleep lasting between 7 to 8 hours each night. Consistent sleep helps you wake up refreshed and ready to conquer your day. Key to achieving this is maintaining a regular sleep schedule, even on weekends, to avoid “social jet lag.” Variances greater than 2 hours between your weekday and weekend sleep times can adversely impact your health. Start by setting a fixed wake-up time and then deduct 8 hours to determine your optimal bedtime. Don’t forget to allocate an additional 30 minutes for your pre-sleep routine. Optimize your bedroom for sleep by reserving it strictly for rest and intimacy. Remove all distractions, including TVs and smartphones, which emit blue light and disrupt your circadian rhythm. Additionally, regulate your room temperature to the ideal range of (15.5-19.5 degrees Celsius) 60-67 degrees Fahrenheit and invest in blackout curtains to eliminate light and minimise noise. If you’re a hot sleeper, consider using breathable fabrics like cotton, linen, or silk for your bedding and nightwear. A pre-sleep routine can significantly enhance the quality of your sleep. Herbal teas and essential oils such as lavender or chamomile have calming effects that can help you unwind. Incorporate relaxing activities like meditation, reading, or light stretching before bed. Avoid heavy meals and stimulants like caffeine and nicotine in the hours leading up to bedtime. Creating a structured timeline for your evening can help streamline your activities and reduce stress. Make a list of tasks and activities you need to complete between getting home from work and going to bed. Allocate generous time slots to each activity to ensure you’re not rushed. For instance, spend time with family, prepare a light dinner, and then engage in calming activities like a warm bath with essential oils before finally hitting the bed. By customising and committing to a sleep routine for just 10 days, you’ll experience tangible improvements in your energy levels, immunity, and overall mood. Share your progress and experiences with us on Instagram and LinkedIn as you master the art of getting quality sleep. Investing time and effort into crafting the perfect sleep routine can be a game-changer, both in your personal life and at work. A consistent, quality sleep schedule of around 8 hours per night will not only boost your productivity but also enhance your overall well-being. Experiment with different elements in your sleep routine to find what works best for you, and don’t hesitate to make adjustments as needed Gain control of your sleep, and watch your productivity soar!
