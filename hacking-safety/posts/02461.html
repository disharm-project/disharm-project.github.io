About invicta

Two thousand years ago, Jesus proclaimed that He had come to set the captives free. Since 2014, Invicta Ministries, led by Mark and Jana Chase, has continued this mission to free people from the heaviness of spiritual oppression and the deep pain of emotional trauma from the past. We are based out of Fort Lauderdale, Florida; however, we serve Christian believers from all around the world. We minister the Gospel of Jesus Christ to bring salvation to God’s chosen people. We do this by making repentance the foundation of our ministry. Paul taught that without repentance, deliverance isn’t possible (2 Timothy 2:24-26). We minister deliverance prayer to victims of demonic spiritual bondage so that they may experience God’s healing in their lives. We minister inner healing to people just like you in order to heal the broken heart that resulted from the abandonment, rejection, neglect, abuse, molestation, and violation that happened in childhood and later in life. We want you to be the person that God created you to be: Invicta Ministries is the DWJD Deliverance Team of Fort Lauderdale, Florida. Invicta Ministries is under the auspices of Invicta means “unvanquished,” and for us, that is a wonderful way to describe our Savior and Lord, Jesus. He said to them, “Go into all the world and preach the Gospel to all creation. Whoever believes and is baptized will be saved, but whoever does not believe will be condemned. And these signs will accompany those who believe: In my name they will drive out demons; they will speak in new tongues; they will pick up snakes with their hands; and when they drink deadly poison, it will not hurt them at all; they will place their hands on sick people, and they will get well.” Mark 16:15-18 (NIV)

Here at Invicta, we teach that it is the spiritual calling, anointing, and responsibility of every Christian to enforce the victory of the cross by taking authority over the enemy and casting him out of our lives—in the strong Name of Jesus. We believe that God’s word—the Bible—is the final authority on all matters. We believe that there is a spiritual war taking place (Ephesians 6, 2 Corinthians 10, etc.) and that this war is a literal battle against demonic forces, not a metaphorical conflict. We believe that repentance, turning to Christ, and the forsaking of sin is the first step in the process of deliverance from demon spirits. According to the “Formula for Freedom” (James 4:7), we must submit to God, resist the devil, and then the devil will flee from us. Repentance is a major component of our submission to God. We believe in and operate in the Gifts of the Holy Spirit (1 Corinthians 12). We believe that God still uses individuals to heal and work in the miraculous. We believe that every follower of Jesus is to walk in His footsteps (1 Peter 2:21). We believe that walking in the footsteps of Jesus entails doing what Jesus did—and even doing greater things (John 14:12). We believe that it is God’s plan for His Church to live in victory over every force of darkness. And finally, we believe that this victory can only be achieved in the Name of Jesus and by the power and counsel of His Spirit. If you are currently suffering from spiritual torment, or are burdened with the pain of what they did to you earlier in your life, know that Jesus, and Jesus alone, can renew, restore, transform, and heal you. We bless your process of healing. And we pray that the Lord would use Invicta Ministries as His vessel to bring His healing to you. And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb and by the word of their testimony and they did not love their lives to the death. Revelation 12:11 (NKJV) Invicta Ministries © 2014 – 2024 | All Rights Reserved Jesus came to earth as God clothed in humanity to save us from our sin (Matthew 1:21), to heal us (Luke 5:31) and destroy the work of the devil (1 John 3:8). And this work of the devil has always been to steal, kill and destroy every good blessing that God has planned for us, His children. Jesus also came to give power and authority over Satan to those who would have faith in Him. In His name we are to preach the Gospel, heal the sick and cast out demons (Mark 16). And time after time in the Bible, Jesus demonstrated this authority and power over the forces of darkness. We see how He was victorious over temptation, never fell to sin and always overcame the enemy. From the first demon He cast out in Capernaum, to His Temptation in the Wilderness, to the Resurrection, Jesus was victorious over Satan—every single time. You can rightly say that Jesus Christ, the Son of the Living God, was, is and always will be—unvanquished. For our struggle is not against flesh and blood, but against the rulers, against the authorities, against the powers of this dark world and against the spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly realms. Ephesians 6:12 (NIV)
