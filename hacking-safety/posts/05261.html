Gatlinburg here we come

I try to use this blog to record something about my life everyday. On December 31, 2007 I left my job with Sprint/Embarq where I worked as a Software Engineer for 15 years at the Sprint Campus in Kansas City. In January 2008 Mary and I started Steve Brown Ministries and started traveling the United States broadcasting "Live Video" of Revivals, Bible Conferences, Mission Conferences, and other Special Services. Mary and I enjoy serving the Lord Jesus Christ and traveling to churches as we minister through SteveBrownMinistries.com. We are very pleased that our ministry is endorsed and we are sent out of our home church at Heritage Baptist Temple in Little Rock, Arkansas. View my complete profile

We spent the morning packing our equipment and loading it into our van. Some of the men showed up to help and saved my poor aching back a lot of straining. We left Moravian Falls, North Carolina about 1:00 p.m. or so. We stopped off in Gatlinburg, Tennessee to spend the night. Downtown was as busy as ever with tourist. We enjoyed a great meal on the strip and sat on a bench on the street until way after dark watching people, antique cars, and motorcycles. There was a motorcycle meet over in Maggie's Valley and an antique car show over in Pigeon Forge. So we had plenty to look at. posted by Steve Brown | 9/12/2009 11:58:00 PM
