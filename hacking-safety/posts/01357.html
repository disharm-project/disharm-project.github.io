Tum Aakhri Jazeera Ho (تم آخری جزیرہ ھو) is a novel written by a famed female writer Amna Riaz. It features a romantic story of a couple that explains the importance of those relationships from which man can never be separated, like the last island where life stops. The novel’s story revolves around a young girl named Maha. She is a stunning girl. She falls in love with a boy, and soon they get married to each other. But she did not know after this marriage her life, become changed when she finds that their relationship was just like a compromise.
