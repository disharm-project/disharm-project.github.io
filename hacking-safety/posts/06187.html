Concert horse racing stomp and more this week

987kissfmsanangelo.com · https://987kissfmsanangelo.com/concert-horse-racing-stomp-and-more-this-week/

1 of 26 sentences flagged

We keep you up to date with what's going on in and around the Concho Valley from week to week. Here's a look at what's happening this week... COOPER’S BBQ LIVE – SELENA EXPERIENCE AUG 27TH Coopers BBQ Live is bringing you a very unique and awesome tribute this Fri, Aug 27th. Be sure to make it out for Los Chicos Del 512 - The Selena Experience! Get your tickets at originalcoopersbbq.com and be listening to win you spot on our guest list. PAC CENTER - STOMP FRI, AUG 27TH "STOMP", the international percussion sensation, will make an exciting return to San Angelo at The Murphey Performance Hall this Fri, Aug 27th at 7:30 PM. From its beginnings as a street performance in the UK, STOMP has grown into an international sensation over the past 20 years, having performed in more than 50 countries and in front of more than 24 million people. For ticket information and more, click on sanangelopac.org. DOWNTOWN MOVIE NIGHT FRI, AUG 27TH The 2021 "Downtown Movie Night" series has one more great night out this Fri, Aug 27th which is another "Family Night" where in addition to the free movie, two more bicycles will be given away. Get your friends and family together and head to 17 East Twohig between the Cactus Hotel and Miss Hattie's. Bring your lawn chairs and blankets. Concessions will be available and the featured movie this Friday is DoLittle! GILLESPIE COUNTY HORSE RACES SAT & SUN, AUG 28th & 29th The Gillespie County Fair Association has brought you three exciting Pari-Mutuel Horse Racing weekends this season at the fairgrounds in Fredericksburg. This Sat & Sun, Aug 28th & 29th will be the 4th and final weekend of races for this season. Take the short trip to Fredericksburg for all of the fun and be sure to check out their Race Barn where you can bet on other races from top tracks across the nation while enjoying a full bar and casual dining. For more, visit gillespiefair.com. Be listening to win free admission to the races! "ALL SHOOK UP" NOW - AUG 29TH Angelo Civic Theatre presents "All Shook Up" with performances running through this Sun, Aug 29th. The production is loosely based on Shakespeare’s Twelfth Night, and is inspired by and features the music of Elvis Presley. Come see the hip-swiveling’, lip-curlin’ musical fantasy at Angelo Civic Theatre! Call 949-4400 or go to AngeloCivicTheatre.org to reserve your seats! SPORTS NEXT LEVEL -CORNHOLE TOURNAMENTS SATURDAYS - TFN The Kickin’ Country Cornhole Tournaments are back at Sports Next Level with your chance to win big prizes and money every Saturday. Find out how to enter your team at sportsnextlevel.com. CONCHO VALLEY FARMERS MARKET NOW UNTIL OCT. The Concho Valley Farmers Market is open every Tues, Thurs, & Sat until October at the Farmers Market Pavilion, 609 S Oakes across from Ft Concho offering you fresh fruits and vegetables grown within a 75 mile radius of San Angelo along with meats, eggs and more all on sale from 7 am – Noon. For more information, call 245-3338

If you've got an event coming up, let us help you spread the word. Just email the information to chuck.baker@townsquaremedia.com.
