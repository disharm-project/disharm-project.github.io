11, 6, 2023 288 A businessman cum politician in Tamale, Alhaji Musah Fuseini also known as Fuzzy has expressed his desire to contest the Tamale South NPP Primaries when nominations are opened. Speaking to the *Voiceless media* in an interview, Alhaji Fuseini explained that he has nursed the Tamale South Constituency over the years and now feels okay presenting himself for election to represent the NPP in the 2024 Parliamentary elections in the Constituency. Checks by the *Voiceless media* in the Tamale South Constituency shows that Alhaji Musah Fuseini gets along very well with the Chiefs, Religious leaders, opinion leaders and the youth in the constituency and has been of support to them in many ways. When elected as the Parliamentary Candidate, Alhaji Fuseini would embarked on aggressive marketing of government policies and programmes and would make agriculture, entrepreneurship employment and support to the youth and women as his topmost priority in the constituency. The well known businessman in Tamale also indicated that he would used his networks to the benefit of the people of the Tamale South Constituency when elected.
