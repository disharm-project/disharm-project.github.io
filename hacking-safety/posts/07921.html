It may also be used during IVF (in vitro fertilization). Progesterone is a hormone which is required by the reproductive system to function properly. Problems becoming pregnant can occur in some women due to a deficiency of progesterone. Treatment with this gel helps to prepare the womb for receiving a fertilized egg, and also helps to maintain the pregnancy. Always use Crinone Vaginal Gel (Progesterone) as you have been prescribed by your doctor. The correct dosage can vary depending on your health, medical history, and the severity of the condition being treated. Crinone Vaginal Gel (Progesterone) is inserted directly into the vagina, using one of the pre-filled single use applicators provided. Adults are sometimes told to use one application of the gel, 1 to 2 times per day. If a pregnancy occurs, treatment may need to be continued for 10 to 12 weeks. The gel is administered by inserting the applicator into the vagina, and depositing its contents into the vagina, after which the applicator is disposed of. Never self-medicate or change your dosage without first consulting your doctor. Patients using Crinone Vaginal Gel (Progesterone) may experience: Tell your doctor if you are unwell or experience any discomfort during treatment. Severe reactions are rare, but may still occur. Consult your doctor immediately if you notice inflammation of the limbs, a lump on the breasts, pain in the arms or legs, anxiety, visual disturbances or any other symptoms of a serious reaction. There is a slight possibility that using this gel may increase the risk of strokes, breast cancer, blood clots and other serious conditions. Ask your doctor about the risks of treatment before using the gel. If you have a history of blood clots, strokes, liver disease, abnormal bleeding from the vagina, problems related to circulation, or if you have recently undergone and incomplete abortion or tubal pregnancy, this medicine is not suitable for you. Crinone Vaginal Gel (Progesterone) is not always suitable for all patients. Always consult your healthcare provider prior to using this medication if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, trying to conceive, using any other medication (prescription or non-prescription), using any herbal products or supplements, or if you have any allergies or other health problems. Seek immediate emergency medical attention if you suffer an allergic reaction.
