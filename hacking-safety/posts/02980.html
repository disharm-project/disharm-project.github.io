GRAVELKING GK The Panaracer GravelKing is for cyclists are looking to expand their cycling horizons. That includes roads that may not be suited for normal road Tyres. Panaracer's GravelKing tyre solves that problem with widths to suit any rider's tastes. Natural rubber compound, puncture protection breaker and a special low rolling resistance casing make the GravelKing a go-anywhere Tyre. This is the tyre that Panaracer's also base all their other GravelKing range upon so there will be a tread pattern to suit any gravel rider. GRAVELKING SK The GravelKing Sk might just be the tire you need for the more hardcore roads. Aggressive knobs, ZSG Natural rubber compound, puncture protection breaker and AX-a special low rolling resistance casing make the Gravelking a go-anywhere tire. And tubeless compatible up to 60 PSI! SK funnily enough standards for 'small knobs', and this is exactly what the tyre looks like. The small knobs substantially improve the ride quality whilst still ensuring that the user is provided all the benefits of Panaracer's other gravel tyres. The SK will thrive on the rougher roads and in places where extra grip is needed. GRAVELKING SS The GravelKing SS is part of Panaracer's GravelKing family. It's a tyre built for those looking for a middle ground between a slick and knobbly tyre. The idea was to construct a tyre that provides a slightly more aggressive profile to Panaracer's standard GravelKing whilst providing a low rolling resistance. The SS will thrive on rougher roads whilst providing ample speed and minimal rolling resistance. To top that off, the GravelKing SS also features puncture protection from Panaracer's Anti-Flat casing. The GravelKing SS comes in a huge variety of sizes and there are standard and tubeless versions available for this tyre. There are also colour choices so you can ensure that the GravelKing SS will look the part on your bike. GRAVELKING SS+ Panaracer have with the GravelKing SS + built on the success of the standard GravelKing and the GravelKing SK —both industry leading Tyres Gravel tyres in their own right— Panaracer has developed the next generation of the gravel Tyres in the GravelKing SS+. The tyre is designed as a dryer condition, race day ready and fast rolling Tyre, the SS+ was made for those riders who want to go fast while maintaining great control, traction and climbing prowess. The SS+ feature an all-new tread pattern consisting of a smoother, diamond pattern centre with more aggressive side lugs. This tread pattern allows the rider to keep speed going into corners and maintain control throughout any transition a gravel ride could present.
