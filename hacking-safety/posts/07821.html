Hi dolls, I want to share both an absolutely stunning venue with you and an amazing wedding show that is happening this sunday. Tucked away in Ottershaw in Surrey is stunning Foxhills- a resort hotel, health spa and golf club! ? w=150″ width=”400″ height=”400″ />

Foxhills is a hidden gem nestled deep within 400 acres of Surrey countryside, yet is just 20 minutes from Heathrow and easily reached from Windsor, Woking, Chertsey and Kingston! It really needs to be viewed to admire its true beauty. The venue has a range of function spaces including its 19th Century Manor or contemporary Clubhouse to hold your Wedding Breakfast and Evening Reception. You can enjoy stunning backdrops for photos on the Manor Lawn or Clubhouse Terrace. There is also the light and elegant orangery as well as the choice of the period Library for your wedding ceremony. ? w=150″ width=”400″ height=”400″ />
