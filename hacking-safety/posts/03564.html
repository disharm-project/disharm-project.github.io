HSWM builds opportunities for pets and people to thrive across Montana. One in three pets will go missing in its lifetime; and whether you have lost or found a pet the Humane Society of Western Montana is here to help. Missing your pet? Check out the found animals that are currently in the care of HSWM. The Humane Society of Western Montana is committed to being a resource for shelter pets and all pets & people who call Western Montana home. In 2023, we were proud to provide: Adoptions Providing Loving Homes to Shelter Animals Pet Vaccinations Given at no and low-cost clinics Pet Meals To Ensure No Pet is Hungry Spay/Neuter Surgeries To Improve the Health of the Montana Pet Population All of the pets and people at HSWM appreciate your support! We are a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. We receive no government funding, and rely solely on the donations of our generous community members, events and funds received through grants.
