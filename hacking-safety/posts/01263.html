So please spend some time and pray the Lord to be present in our efforts to help the homeless as we share the Gospel message with them. ​ Matthew 25:35-36 35 For I was hungry and you gave me something to eat, I was thirsty and you gave me something to drink, I was a stranger and you invited me in, 36 I needed clothes and you clothed me, I was sick and you looked after me, I was in prison and you came to visit me. Matthew 25:40 “The King will reply, ‘Truly I tell you, whatever you did for one of the least of these brothers and sisters of mine, you did for me.’ Volunteer Join us Saturday Morning: Volunteer Form 7:30 a.m. until 9:30 a.m. @ StoryChurch 502 N Walnut St. Colorado Springs, CO 80905. No special training is required to help set up tables, unload vans, distribute items, talk, pray at this community event. You can still be a help if lifting is not an option. Please join us and help serve our neighbors in need. We do not directly give money out, we believe in handing out items that will be used to make day-to-day life on the street easier. Donate We are a 5019(c)(3) non-profit; your donations are tax deductible. ​ Donations: through PayPal, Facebook, Check. We are an all-volunteer organization with low overhead. This means that donations are being put to use buying clothes that we give out freely. ​ We buy at Thrift stores, Wholesale, and Walmart to reduce costs About, More Than A Meal, Inc., serves citizens who are at or below the poverty level and/or homeless. Every Saturday morning we meet at 7:30 a.m. to cook and serve breakfast and hand out clothing and toiletries to the homeless. We are a collection of diverse people who come together as the Body of Christ to share in serving the community. 7:30 a.m. Parking Lot Saturday Morning StoryChurch 502 N Walnut St. Colorado Springs, CO 80905

For many of the people that we help, the food, clothes, and toiletries they receive really are a hand up and not a handout. We rejoice when someone who was homeless returns with the good news that they will be getting an apartment or have found a good job. Join us to help others and make a difference in people’s lives. ​ Our goal is to treat everyone as the precious person they are in God’s sight. We play Christian music and have a short sermon to encourage the homeless to accept the free gift of salvation. We hand out the clothes, toiletries, and food to those who live on the street so they may feel the love of Christ and know that salvation is for them with "No Strings Attached". We have witnessed the power of Jesus changing lives. Some of our volunteers have more than 10 - 20 years of sobriety and are an example that Jesus can break the chains of addiction and save the lost. Remember not all homeless people have addictions. Many are willing to work they just need help to get back on their feet. If you would like to volunteer please complete the volunteer form and tell us what Saturday you can attend. Large groups please plan your volunteer Saturday with us in advance so we may reserve that weekend for your group. Children are welcome as long as they are accompanied by an adult. Please partner with us and help "Give A Hand Up Not A Hand-Out". Your support will allow us to purchase more clothes like jeans, jackets, and boots. We need your financial support to provide more necessities to pass out to the homeless. We are an all-volunteer organization and spend 100% of your donation on clothing and basic needs. Thank you,
