God’s word is a lamp unto our feet. And when we sing it, it gives us strength to walk with him. Find out how this is true for all of us when you join Charles Morris this week for a new series featuring Scripture Lullabies creator Jay Stocker. PART 1: Jay Stocker joins Charles Morris to talk about how peaceful music based on Scripture can lower anxiety and bring us closer to God. PART 2: Joshua 1:6 is one of the most popular inspirational verses of all time. On today’s program, Charles Morris discusses how God’s radical promises to Joshua also apply to you … just not the way you might think. PART 3: Jeremiah 29:11 is found is often found on T-shirts and coffee mugs, but the deeper meaning is often lost. Be encouraged when you listen to today’s program and learn about God’s great plans for your life. PART 4: Where do you turn when life is out of control? How do you respond to dangers—both expected and unexpected? Many believers throughout history have recited Psalm 121 for strength in hard times. PART 5: Whatever is true, noble, right, pure, lovely, admirable, praiseworthy. The Bible says these are good things to think about. Sadly, we often choose to be anxious instead. PART 6: On this special Memorial Day program, Charles Morris returns to his interview with Jay Stocker about how Scripture Lullabies have become a blessing for both children and adults. PART 7: Whether you hear them from a passing loved one or influential figure, final words are always important. How much more important must they be when we come across them in Scripture. PART 8: “Pain is weakness leaving the body.” This common mantra in sports helps people push past their comfort zones. In the spiritual world, it’s better if we replace “pain” with “confession.” PART 9: Chains often have a negative connotation. In Scripture, however, a famous passage known as “The Golden Chain” does quite the opposite. Instead of bondage and captivity, Romans 8:28-31 promises freedom and love. PART 10: What does it meant to trust in the Lord? Too often we think it means things will work out in our favor. But God often has bigger plans. Hidden In My Heart Vol 1-4 is a journey through deeply moving passages of God’s Word set to beautiful, cinematic music.
