ENVIRONMENTALLY FRIENDLY AND PRACTICAL DURING THE WINTER With our 100% Organic Antifreeze and De-Icer System , we simplify your winter. Simply with a safer and better function but with less effort and complete ease of mind. At the same time, they are an environmentally friendly alternative. Our 100% Organic Antifreeze is totally safe for humans, animals, and nature. Our De-Icer System uses high-performance pumps that have very low energy consumption. VAKE 100% Organic Antifreeze and De-Icer are Swedish-developed products. It all started with the need for a reliable De-Icer. VAKE De-Icer has since then helped many satisfied users who need to clear out ice from around boats and docks. We have later supplemented it with our 100% Organic Antifreeze liquid which contains no glycol or other chemicals and therefore provides a completely non-toxic alternative for antifreeze protection. Thanks to the lack of glycol, the antifreeze can also be used safely in toilets. As an innovative Swedish company, that provides ecological antifreeze and De-Icer solutions. VAKE’s aim is to provide you the best technology in the market while offer a low user cost and high reliability.
