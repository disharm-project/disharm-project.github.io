Benetti motor yacht jacozami for sale

The global authority in superyachting 5 images The 38.1 metre Benetti motor yacht Jacozami, listed for sale by Richard Higgins and Bob Fritsky at Northrop & Johnson, has received a €1M price drop. Built in GRP by Italian yard Benetti to a design by Stefano Righini, she was delivered in 2020 as a Benetti Fast model. An interior by RWD welcomes up to 10 guests in five cabins. The main-deck master suite is spacious and full of light with floor-to-ceiling windows, a walk-in closet, a master office and a his-and-hers en suite bathroom with a shower. The yacht has four additional staterooms, two with queen-size beds and two with twin-size beds and a Pullman, all with television screens and en suite bathroom facilities. Four crew cabins and one captain’s cabin can accommodate eight crew members onboard. The main saloon features a circular seating area, modern armchairs and a chic glass coffee table, creating an eye-catching first impression as guests step on board. A dining area for up to 10 aft of the main saloon creates an intimate space for family meals or entertaining. Forward of the bridge is a Jacuzzi tub alongside an al fresco dining area and cocktail bar. Elsewhere, the sun deck features additional sun loungers and sun pads. Powered by twin MTU diesel engines, she comfortably cruises at 18 knots and reaches a maximum speed of 24 knots with a range of up to 1,750 nautical miles at 12 knots.
