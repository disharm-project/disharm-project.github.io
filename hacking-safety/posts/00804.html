Naana agyemang leaked 2012 iea debate questions to mahama for ministerial appointment napo

Minister of Education, Mathew Opoku Prempeh has alleged that National Democratic Congress (NDC) running mate, Prof Jane Naana Opoku Agyemang leaked a 2012 IEA debate questions to former President John Mahama to gain ministerial appointment. Hon. Opoku Prempeh popularly referred to as NAPO, noted that the New Patriotic Party will be looking to address the integrity of Naana Agyemang before anything else. According to TheBBCghana. Com, the minster revealed in a yet to be aired interview by Multimedia that Jane Naana will not be spared from every fire every politician faces. “We will talk about integrity not only about education. I will talk about integrity because I remember that as Director of Elections 2012 someone went to plant a question which was not part of the questions during the IEA debate for John Mahama to answer so she could be given a Ministerial position, we will talk about it,” he said. Prof Jane Naana Agyemang has been under intense criticism by the governing New Patriotic Party since she was announced as the Vice Presidential candidate for NDC flagbearer, John Dramani Mahama. NAPO further added, “I also know someone who some group of men said they will rape the Chief Justice of the land, a woman who went to support them. We will look into all these before we come and look at education.” Source: TheBBCghana. Com © BBC Ghana Reports | All rights reserved.
