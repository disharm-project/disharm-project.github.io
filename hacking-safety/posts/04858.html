Notify me when this product is available: The Culligan HF-360B Valve-in-Head (VIH) patented technology allows for easy water filter cartridge replacement The Culligan HF-360B (formerly known as the HF-360 or HF-360A Water Filter System) is ideal for residential, commercial, and industrial water filter applications. If installed before appliances with a sediment filter, the Culligan HF-360B water filter housings extend the life of washing machines, water heaters, and other household appliances by reducing sediment before it reaches the appliances. The Culligan HF-360B whole house housings have thick walls for increased strength. The Culligan HF-360B Valve-in-Head involves a simple half-turn of the shut-off valve in the cap to shut off the water supply when changing a water filter. No need to purchase additional shut-off valves (saves money). The Culligan HF-360B water filter system's pressure release button on the cap allows for the release of the whole house water filter pressure in order to more easily unscrew the sump from the cap. Leaking and clogging are prevented by the dynamically sealing, self-cleaning o-ring. Two Culligan HF-360B water filter housings can be connected with a pipe nipple or pipe union. The union will allow for adjustment of the fitting in the event of leakage. Create Account
