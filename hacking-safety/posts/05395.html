AGM 2023 AGM Conference The Nigerian Conference of Women Religious (NCWR) is a body of Religious Congregations of women in the Roman Catholic Church in Nigeria, who came together in a spirit of love and unity for the promotion of the work of evangelization, to give humanitarian services, including fighting against the modern-day slavery of trafficking in women for forced prostitution, giving material and spiritual aid to victims of national crises. These Religious Congregations have diverse charisms and they witness to the core values of the gospel of Jesus Christ. NCWR appreciates and acknowledges the energy and shared wisdom that exists within this group and cherishes their identity as women. The membership of the Conference is open to all the major Superiors of Women Religious Institutes of Consecrated Life and Women Societies of Apostolic Life. – Are you feeling called to serve God and others as a religious woman? Do you: – Have good health and a sound mind – Are you a baptized and confirmed Catholic – Are you a communicant – Do you Believe in God’s love for the world – Do you have a missionary spirit to mission where ever you are sent – Do you have five (5) credits including good grades and mathematics (in O’levels WAEC/GCE or OND, B.Sc, B.A, etc.) – Is your Age limit; 18-26? For inquiry or application write to the vocation directress of any of our member congregations. Their address can be found on the directory page of our website. God’s call is powerful and it is normal to feel some apprehension. We will be with you as you explore the path to which God is inviting you. Please contact us when you are ready to talk.
