The Developer Portal is a centralized platform designed for you to manage the User-Generated Emote feature during your game's run phase. To get started with the Developer Portal, you must first: Create your Developer Portal account by registering here Link your game / virtual world to your Developer Portal account, by registering your Game API Key, in Unity or Unreal Engine. Uploading Avatars is one of the core functions of the Developer Portal, as it allows you to: Preview the outputs of the User-Generated Emote feature, directly on your game's avatars. Enable the Contact-Aware Retargeting for your game. The Developer Portal allows you to try the User-Generated Emote feature so that you can have a look at how it would look like for your end-users. To access the demo, simply log into your Developer Portal account, and head to the "Try UGC feature" section. For more information, visit: To learn more about the specifications of the tool, to optimize the UGC outputs, or find use cases & best practices, visit UGE: Guides & Best Practices Once the User-Generated Emote feature is integrated in your game with the SDK, you can monitor the content created by your players directly from the Developer Portal. Kinetix's Moderation strategy is both proactive and reactive: Proactive moderation is automated Reactive moderation is up to you, and accessible from your Developer Portal, in the "UGC Moderation" tab.
