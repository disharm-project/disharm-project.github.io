Children tend to have a fascination with things that go moo, neigh and cock-a-doodle-doo. In fact, early development goals tend to use farm toys as an introductory form of pattern recognition, vocabulary, shape and memorisation - partly because of the fun and mnemonic quality of onomatopoeia. Farm toys are a great way to introduce children to handling different primary and modular shapes, interaction and even construction development. Toy farm animals help children practice their understanding of different relationships between entities, while tractors and farm vehicles can help children visualise and enact spatial relationships and factors like speed, weight and direction. We stock a range of fun and friendly farm toys and toy farm animals from leading developmentally friendly producers. These toys are great for solo or cooperative play, and can be used with friends or parents at home or outdoors.
