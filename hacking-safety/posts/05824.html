Brooklyn

Interested in adopting me? Click here for our >> Adoption Procedures I was born in a puppy mill; from there I went to Petland and when I was purchased, I thought I was going to my forever home. Well, that did not happen! My person brought me to Harlequin Haven one evening after I got into a fight with another female dog at our home. I lived with many dogs, none altered and well one thing led to another, and we got into a fight. My parents said that I had to go and could not come back so my person brought me here! He and I both cried as he left. I was terrified as I thought I was going to be beaten or shot or something bad because of the stupid fight. Instead, Grandma (the caretaker at the rescue) made me a soft bed, gave me tons of toys, gave me food and treats and treated my wounds. I have been told just to rest and let my wounds heal and in time I will get to look for my real forever home. Update 8/4: Well I kinda lost track of time. My wounds have healed and I have been spayed and now I am ready to start looking for a forever home. I love people and I love being loved on and I love playing and I love giving kisses well if you haven't figured it out yet I love life!! So who wants to adopt a loving, playful beautiful Great Dane? I will have Grandma get a few new pictures of me real soon, but if you are interested in adopting me you should fill out the application!! Please can you spare a few dollars to help the Rescue continue to help me and other Danes like me? It would be greatly appreciated. Without the Rescue who knows where we would have ended up!! Thanks,

Brooklyn
