Rubber is the substance which allows you a limited amount of time, until the rubber bar is full, before the player is terminated. Rubber is usually depleted when running into walls, or next to them. Lag is a myth, but heres some information on how you can minimize it. FPS is the number of frames pass your screen in 1 sec. It is possible to play with an FPS around 40 or higher.
