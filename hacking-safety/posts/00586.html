New curate in 2021

We are delighted to announce that in June 2021 we shall be joined by Assistant Curate Jenny Brady. Jenny will be a Self-Supporting Minister based at St Nick’s but also contributing to the ministry of St Athanasius, Kirkdale. Jenny is a qualified social worker and was a lay reader within the Diocese before she began training on the All Saints’ Scheme. Jenny will bring a great deal of experience and dynamism to our church life and we look forward to welcoming her next year as part of our clergy team. Jenny Brady “I am delighted to be joining the team at Our Lady and St Nicholas church to serve my curacy. I view this as an extremely important threshold in my ministry and it will be a privilege to become involved in the worshipping community at the Parish Church. I am married to Kevin and I have an adult daughter called Lily. I work for Liverpool City Council and I am excited about all that lies ahead. The photograph of me was taken at St Beuno’s in North Wales, a Jesuit Retreat House that I like to visit regularly.” Old Churchyard Chapel Street Liverpool Merseyside L2 8TZ

Tel: +44 (0)151 236 5287 Email: enquiries@livpc.co.uk “Liverpool Parish Church is committed to safeguarding and promoting the welfare of children and vulnerable adults.”
