Pictured Above: Yarn – Paintbox Simply Aran Hook – Tulip Crochet Hook from Leither Co.Notions Tin – Green Fox Farms Designs To make the Quiet River Cowl for yourself you will need the following items: The Quiet River Cowl uses the following stitches to create an amazing cozy feel. Dc2Tog – Double Crochet 2 Together – YO, insert hook into indicated St, YO and pull up a loop, YO pull through 2 loops, YO, insert hook into indicated St, YO and pull up a loop, YO, pull through 2 loops (3 loops on hook), YO, pull through all loops on hook. Hdc2Tog – Half Double Crochet 2 Together – YO, insert hook into indicated St, YO and pull up a loop, YO, insert hook into indicated St, YO and pull up a loop (5 loops on hook), YO and pull through all loops on the hook. A stitch count is given at the end of each row. I find it easier to place stitch markers on the first stitch of each row. Gauge: 8 Sts x 8 Rows = 4” of stitch pattern Finished Size: 21” wide x 14” tall The Quiet River Cowl pattern can be purchased on Etsy and Ravelry.
