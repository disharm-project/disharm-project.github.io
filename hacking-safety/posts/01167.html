Relationship expert April Masini believes Ant's snipes at his estranged wife Lisa Armstrong show he's still not in a healthy place, despite recovering from his addictions Ant McPartlin has staged an epic comeback to the world of showbiz this month - casting off his drink-driving conviction and battle with alcohol addiction to reunite with his best friend Declan Donnelly at Britain's Got Talent. But his shocking comments about his new relationship with PA-turned-girlfriend Anne-Marie Corbett have divided fans, especially over his remarks about estranged wife Lisa Armstrong. By branding Anne-Marie as his "rock" and claiming he's never been in such a good place, many fans were taken aback by how quickly Ant had dismissed his wife of more than 20 years. Here, relationship expert April Masini reveals how Ant's recent public troubles could still doom his new relationship... Any swipes or underhanded comments anyone makes about Ant's wife are just expressions of frustration to the very personal situations between these three people. If Ant and Anne-Marie want to show the world that they are healthy, and that they support his sobriety, they will express empathy and goodwill towards his wife during this tough time for all. Lisa is in the most difficult spot, emotionally, and their empathy is really the healthy choice. Swipes and insults — they're just more negativity stirring the pot. If Anne-Marie wants to support Ant, she'll express empathy towards his wife and the situation Lisa's in. If you don't see empathy, you're not seeing healthy behaviour. It's great that Ant is feeling happy, healthy and sober, and is getting through his divorce and its challenges, with a new partner who he's been with for a long time, but I wouldn't hold my breath waiting for this new love to be his last new love. There are a couple of warning signs that indicate that Anne-Marie may be his Ms Right Now. First of all, sobriety and addiction is very serious and difficult to maneuver. Relapse is part of addiction, and there is a good chance that relapse will be part of the bigger picture. Nobody wants that, but that is the nature of addiction and recovery. So while he's touting his sobriety, he might be prudent to take a long-term view of the recovery process. Next, he has revealed being diagnosed with ADHD, an attention-deficit condition that makes it difficult to stick to one thing for a long time. It's hard not to wonder if his current romantic relationship will lose his attention at some point. In addition, he's going through a divorce and a doggie custody battle — and it's always easier to go through these things with a partner than alone, so the fact that Anne-Marie is ready and willing to support him is great, but it may mean that he is using her to help him get through this. Also, Anne-Marie is still technically married to her husband Scott, who she split from in late 2017. That's not a lot of time for her to 'be single', see what’s out there, process her own divorce, and help her children get through their own loss that comes with divorce. For her to jump into a relationship that is so public, with a man who has challenges (like a wife, a custody battle over a dog, a drink-driving conviction and a sobriety health issue), is going to present challenges for her on a deep personal level.
