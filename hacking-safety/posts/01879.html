Shop Floor Lamps of all styles from Mirage Lighting Floor lamps are freestanding lighting fixtures that provide illumination to a room while also adding style and ambiance. These modern floor lamps are designed to be placed on the floor, typically next to seating areas or in corners where additional lighting is needed. Floor lamps come in a variety of styles, sizes, and materials, such as metal, wood, or glass. They can feature adjustable arms, multiple light bulbs, and different shades to control the direction and intensity of the light. Floor lamps are a versatile lighting solution for any room in the house, from living rooms and bedrooms to home offices and reading nooks.
