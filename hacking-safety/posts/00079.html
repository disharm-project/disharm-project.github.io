Arts & Glass Paint pottery, fuse glass, have a party or take class! While you can join us any time we are open without making a reservation, we’ve got three great workshops planned for this week that you’ll want to sign up for! Thursday night we’re making Fused Glass Gardens like the one above. It’s great for beginners and experiences glass artists alike–we provide the pattern to get you started and will show you how to safely cut and assemble the glass pieces. Personalize your garden with whatever plants you’d like…maybe even make yours into an ever-beautiful succulent garden! Sign up for this workshop here. On Friday night we’ll be painting Halloween Trees (or classic Christmas Trees, if you prefer!). Pick your tree and enjoy access to any and all of our specialty glazes like glitter, rustics, and matte finishes. Also included with your tree are an electric light kit, rainbow assortment of plastic lights, and a FREE ceramic topper to attach to your tree. (We painted a pumpkin like Jack Skellington to perch atop our black glitter tree!) Reserve your tree and spot online here. Then on Saturday we are running amok, amok, amok, amok for our Hocus Pocus Night! $5 Gets you in with a whole sheet of custom-made Magic Stickers and use of our bewitching Hocus Pocus silkscreens to use on whatever pottery you’d like. It’s a fun, laid-back night to celebrate the Sanderson Sisters and probably the most fun Halloween movie ever made. I mean, who doesn’t sing Bette Midler’s version of “I Put a Spell on You” for weeks after each time they watch it and wonder if every black cat they come across might just be a teenage boy trapped in a feline body? Reserve your spot online here.
