Beach house placencia plantation

livitbelize.com · https://livitbelize.com/property/beach-house-placencia-plantation/

1 of 44 sentences flagged

Spread over a manicured half-acre+, 205 Plantation South is the beach house you have been waiting for. Located in The Plantation, this residential neighborhood is a 6-mile ribbon on the Placencia Peninsula featuring luxury beach homes, an exclusive gated community, and nearby a 4-Star Resort. Plantation South offers residents 360-degree views of the Caribbean Sea and the Mayan Mountains. This oversized parcel provides over 100 feet of white sandy beach frontage, exceptional outdoor gathering areas, and the high level of finishing and furnishings that you’ve been looking for. This home is unique in Belize, and only the discernible buyer need enquire. The primary residence is spread over the 2nd and 3rd floors of the home. The 2nd floor contains a Master Suite with bedside views of the Caribbean Sea and a full private ensuite with updated GE laundry appliances. The second bedroom on this floor also has a fully accessible, recently updated, full bathroom. Both bedrooms feature imported beds, window treatments, and furnishings. Your guests will be happy people! The main floor living space dominates the home, and is enveloped by the Caribbean Sea to the east and the Mayan Mountains to the west. Again, luxury finishes and furniture feature throughout the space. 30′ ceilings, and a massive sectional couch. The professional pool table and dry bar are perfectly placed for indoor entertaining. Off the living room is an eat-in gourmet kitchen with seating for 10, Frigidaire Pro double fridge/freezer, Fisher & Paykel Double Dish Drawer, Scotsman CU50 filtered ice maker, stainless gas range, oven, and microwave. Just off the kitchen is a large pantry with an additional storage freezer. The cabinets and granite were all replaced in 2018 and feature contemporary coastal themes and luxury fixtures and furniture. The kitchen and dining area has large windows providing wonderful natural light. A large winding hardwood staircase takes you to your Owner’s Suite, the entire 3rd floor. In addition to the raised views, the Owners Suite provides an oversized King sleeping space, a living area, full bathroom with water closet. A star feature is the huge shower looking over the Mayan Mountains. This is your personal refuge. The second floor living areas extend to two beautifully finished outdoor gathering spaces. On the west you’ll enjoy a fire table with plush and comfy oversized seating. it’s the perfect spot for a pre-dinner drink as you watch the sunset over the Mayan Mountains. To the east you’ll watch the sunrise on your 1200 square foot patio. Features include a heated salt-water infinity pool, large dining table for the family, a Traeger smoker, and additional gas grill. Walk down the steps to the ground-floor entertainment space – Over 2500 square feet, with a large party pool, covered dining area with Thatch Palapa, large sundeck, and outdoor kitchen with a gas rotisserie grill and flat top grill. The entire space was added in 2019 and features high quality finishes, granite counters, serving island, and tons of cabinet space. There’s an outdoor shower and a half bath for guests. It’s conveniently placed off the breezeway. It’s what everyone wants for themselves, and their guests! Beyond the lounging deck is the white sand of the Caribbean Sea, a large beach area walks out to your 153-foot dock (with permit), boat platform, covered palapa, and private sundeck. This feature is a must-have for any perfect Caribbean beach house. The ground floor offers additional features that make the property as functional as it is beautiful. First, a large garage for a single car or golf cart plus storage. The utility spaces include a built in cistern for water storage, a security panel/monitor space, timers for lights and pools and an auto-switch Generac generator. A separate water catchment system cistern tank 30,000 gallon plus 10,000 gallon collects and distributes water for landscaping and the pools. Finally, a recently updated 1200 square foot guest apartment with the same high-end fixtures and furnishings you’ve come to expect. It has a fully furnished living room with a luxury kitchenette and island, a huge bedroom suite with 2 queen beds and a sofa, and an ensuite bathroom with oversize shower. The functionality of these spaces provide convenience and flexibility for owners who want to entertain but maintain privacy in their home.
