Making window defroster can be simple using a mixture of water and vinegar. Mix 1/3 part water with 2/3 part vinegar and use a plant sprayer to spray this on your car window, side windows and mirrors. This mixture can help melt the ice and clear your car window of ice. Make sure you spray the window thoroughly and wait a while before you start scratching. It's a handy homemade solution, but be careful not to use other ingredients that could damage your window. Although antifreeze blankets are specifically designed for car windows, in some cases you may want to consider alternative products. Thick cardboard pieces or garbage bags can temporarily reduce frost on your car window. However, it is important to note that these alternatives may be less effective and not as suitable for long-term use as specially designed anti-freeze blankets. If you plan to use something else, make sure it completely covers the window and is secured securely to prevent it from blowing away. It is best not to use any objects other than an ice scraper as these alternatives can cause damage to your car window. Using hot water to thaw a frozen car window is not a recommended method. The rapid temperature difference can damage the car window, which can result in expensive repairs. It is safer and more efficient to use an ice scraper or window defroster to remove ice. It's not a good idea to use your windshield wipers to remove ice. This can damage the rubber wiper elements and cause them to malfunction.
