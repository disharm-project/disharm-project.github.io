Creative Production Gärde Design Menu Close Work Typefaces Studio Services Creative Solutions Production Solutions Essays Contact Research hello@gardedesign.se +46 (0)70–123 45 67 Sans Serif 3 Styles 3 Weights GD Formo is inspired by the 20th-century architect, Ludvig Mies van der Rohe, and his impressive buildings which remain as relevant today as they did 80 years ago. We began by drawing inspiration from the grid system of the Neue Nationalgalerie, incorporating this into a legible, geometric typeface based on the principles of the square, circle, and triangle. Numbers were given an extra importance in the identification of buildings, so we crafted a unique set of numbers that still adhered to the strong geometric and clear-cut principles of the typeface. This typeface became the foundation of the visual identity, and the primary communicator across all media. Clients Hej Styles Overview 400
