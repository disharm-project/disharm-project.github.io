Rio Ferdinand claims Cristiano Ronaldo’s global appeal is on display in Tyson Fury’s clash with Francis Ngannou on Saturday. The Al Nasr striker took part in a boxing match in Saudi Arabia alongside a host of stars from the worlds of sport, music and television. Rappers Eminem and Kanye West and former UFC champion Conor McGregor were among the familiar faces in attendance, but Ronaldo and partner Georgina Georgina Rodriguez became the most eye-catching star of the night. Ferdinand, who was also in Riyadh, spoke of the immense global influence and star power of former team-mate Ronaldo, with the Euro 2016 winner constantly appearing on the big screen throughout the evening. “He (Ronaldo) walked into the building and was under one roof with the greatest sports legend of all time, he was the biggest superstar in that building,” the former Manchester United defender said on his FIVE YouTube show . “It’s crazy. When people say ‘there are other sports more important than football’… no, there aren’t. “There’s no greater sport than football. Football is the greatest sport. NFL, NBA, I don’t care who you are, they can’t compete with football.” Ferdinand was later asked where Ronaldo sat during Fury’s split-decision victory over the boxing newcomer, and the TNT Sports commentator confirmed that Ferdinand had the “best seat in the house.” “Where else do you think he could sit?” Ferdinand replied. “His excellence (Mohammed bin Salman) would only make the greatest professors sit next to him. “Cristiano sat next to him and that was it. The biggest guy in the house had the best seat next to the main guy. “He did it, Eminem is out there somewhere.” Ronaldo’s global influence continues to rise despite his move to Saudi Arabia in January. The 38-year-old remains the most followed athlete on Instagram, with a staggering 610 million followers. The former Manchester United striker signed a £117m-a-year deal with Al Nasr earlier this year that runs until 2025. Source link
