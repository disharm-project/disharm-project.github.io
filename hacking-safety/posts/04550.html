The CST Abuzz is an all-terrain utility tire built to conquer everything from soft- or hard-pack terrain to mud, sand and rocks. Its lightweight construction gives riders effortless steering, quick braking and faster acceleration. A 6-ply rating gives your ATV or Side-by-Side extra load capacity and durability that won’t leave you stranded. Grab and crawl up rocks with the first ever Side-by-Side rock crawling tire. With a super sticky compound and raised sidewall protection, the LOBO RC was engineered specifically for rock crawling. Extremely lightweight with an 8-ply rating, this tire withstands any hard rock conditions thrown at it. If you’re looking for the perfect “do-it-all” tire for an ATV or SxS, the Stag from CST is the tire for you. The Stag features a non-directional tread pattern with alternating hook-like center lugs that claw and clean out dirt, mud, and other debris. Its 6-ply rated radial carcass absorbs rocks, roots, and bumps for ultimate ride comfort. The Behemoth utility tire features an aggressive tread pattern for hard-hitting grip when accelerating and massive shoulder treads for control in ruts. Enjoy lower rolling resistance in hardpack conditions and confidence at high speeds on the trail or in deep mud. Unleash the full potential of your Side-by-Side or ATV with the Behemoth tire from CST. The Clincher from CST offers a plush ride, absorbing jarring feedback from rocks, roots, braking bumps and other obstacles. A large contact patch ensures more rubber on the ground for better traction, while its interlocking “c” design keeps the center tread rolling smooth over hardpack. Shoulder nobs provide extra bite and an aggressive profile on your ATV or Side-by-Side.
