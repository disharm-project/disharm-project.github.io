Accessibility Options: Back to Directory Rey Paul earned his A.A and A.S at BC and his B.A from FAU. He is better known by his artist name Rey Rey, and is an Entrepreneur, Photographer, Poet, and the founder of The Mind of Rey Rey®, a multimedia and marketing company based out of Plantation, FL, providing services worldwide. Rey Rey built his achievements at BC. He was awarded the 2004 “Irene Fischley Award for Excellence” for EMT and was featured seven times in P’an Ku (Broward College’s Literary & Arts Magazine) showcasing some of his photography and poetry. He was also the president of the Broadcasting & Film Club for one semester. Rey founded his business, The Mind of Rey Rey® in 2014. In 2019, he retired as a paramedic to run his business full-time. In February of 2021, Rey was listed amongst the Yahoo! Finance: Top 10 Entrepreneurs of 2021. Get Connected. Get Involved.
