We are nestled in the serene Rocky Mountains, offering a pathway of alternative solutions to those coming out of sex trafficking. The Cornerstone Project is a faith-based organization that is here to educate, work to eradicate trafficking and provide a residential long-term approach for those seeking a life outside of their sex trafficking. Our programs are tailored to allow individuals to reintegrate into society, heal and find restorative hope to walk powerfully into their futures. Our objective is to provide a safe and secure environment through long-term housing, the provision of daily essential needs, and a consistent caring community. There are less than 600 long-term beds available nationwide for child sex trafficking survivors’ long-term recovery from trauma. Our community is made up of our donors, volunteers, and the leadership of our Board of Directors. Our goal is to unite the community to provide different avenues for thrivers to excel, whether it is through cooking classes, education classes, holistic avenues of health, counseling, art, music, gardening, or whatever talent/enterprise a community member/business can provide.
