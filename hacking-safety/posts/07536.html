A brief post to announce the release of my second book: In the Balance: A 15,000 km Voyage of the Seas of Europe I accepted the income that summer tourism brings, rented out my home, and flew to Norway somewhat depressed about the carbon footprint these choices would lead to. It is difficult to be green. At least the real journey started by bus. That delivered us to Inari in Finland, and the south-west corner of an enormous lake system that I had passed on the final leg of a previous journey. Connecting with nature is connecting with our nature.
